Gate 26: The Gate of the Substitute Palaces

We named it "The Gate of the Substitute Palaces" because, just as holiness has palaces, so the husks have palaces, and they are seven. Its chapters number eight.

Chapter 26:1. Explanation of the first palace.

26:1:1 The Gate of the Palaces of the Husks, which is Gate Twenty-Six:

שער היכלות הקליפות שהוא שער כ"ו:

26:1:2 We have already hinted above that these husks resemble the Emanation in their matter "as an ape before human beings." And therefore, just as in holiness there are seven palaces, which are a dwelling and garment for ten sefirot, so in impurity there are seven palaces, which are a garment for ten rungs. And not that they resemble exactly; for above the seventh palace in the husks there is no impurity at all; and it is not so in holiness; for above the sixth palace there are many, many rungs of the Throne, as we explained:

כבר רמזנו למעלה כי הקליפות האלה הם דומים בענינם אל האצילות כקוף לפני בני האדם ולכן כמו שיש בקדושה ז' היכלות שהם משכן ולבוש אל י' ספירות כן בטומאה הם ז' היכלות שהם לבוש אל עשר מדרגות. ולא שידמו ממש כי למעלה מהיכל שביעי בקליפות אין טומאה כלל ואין כן בקדושה כי למעלה מהיכל ו' כמה וכמה מדרגות הכסא כדפי':

26:1:3 Chapter One

פרק ראשון

26:1:4 The first palace is the one called Pit [בור, bor]; "and the pit was empty" (Gen. 37:24). And whoever comes to enter it has no supporter to take hold of his hand so that he not fall; rather all of them push him so that he falls within it. In this palace there is one appointed one, and his name is Dumah; and he stands here in this palace, and stands at the entrance of the first palace of holiness, outside, with several angels of ruin, to take hold of the souls that the holy angel Tahariel pushes away, as we explained in the Gate of the Palaces of Holiness, chapter 1. And under this appointed one, Dumah, there is another appointed one, and his name is Petut [Seducer]; and under his hand several thousand and myriad angels of ruin. And he stands to seduce human beings from the good way, to look upon adultery and harlotry; as his name, so is his action. And all the angels of ruin that are with him go before human beings, so that their eyes be on the transgression. And this appointed one is also called Sarsarim [another version: Sarsuria]; for he is the go-between [sarsur] of the transgression. And this appointed one stands over the grave at the time that the body is judged, and breaks his eyes, because he inclined after his counsel. For so is the way of the husk: to accuse below, and to rise and accuse above, and to descend and take his soul; and whoever is drawn after it, it itself takes its vengeance on him. Within this palace there are several serpents and scorpions that bite the soul when it enters there. And over this whole palace there is one appointed one, and his name is Gamgima [another version: Gamguma]; and he is red as a rose. And he stands to accuse, when the prayer of a man is pushed away from the first palace of holiness, as we explained in the Gate of Palaces, chapter 1. And when this one receives that prayer that is pushed away, he rises with it up to the seventh palace of the husk; and from there he pleads guilt against him and mentions his iniquities. And this is what the verse says (Job 1:6), "and the Satan also [gam] came among them": gam is the name of the Satan, which is Gamgima [another version: Gamguma]. [There are] appointed ones to take hold of every kind of obscene speech or blemished thing that a man brings out with his lips, and they keep it; and when the man afterward brings out of his mouth words of holiness, such as words of Torah or prayer, then they defile [another version: they are defiled] those holy words with that impurity that he brought out of his mouth before; and then, as it were, the power of holiness is weakened; and then woe to him, woe to the soul of that sinner. Further, in this palace, over all these appointed ones, there is one appointed one, and his name is (Tafsarinia), another version Safsirita; and under his hand several angels of ruin. And they are appointed over all those things that a man throws from his hand in irritation; and they take that thing and raise it above, and say: "This is the offering of So-and-so, which he offered to us"; and they proclaim: "Woe to So-and-so, who went whoring after a strange god." And a heavenly voice goes out a second time and says: "Woe unto them, for they have fled from Me" (Hos. 7:13). And this palace is called the Pit of the Satan:

בהיכל ראשון יהוא הנקרא בור והבור רק וכל הבא ליכנס בו אין לו סומך שיאחז בידו שלא יפול אלא כלם דוחין אותו שיפול בתוכו. בהיכל זה ממונה א' ושמו דומה והוא עומד כאן בהיכל הזה ועומד בפתח היכל ראשון מהקדושה מבחוץ עם כמה מלאכי חבלה לאחוז בנשמות שדוחה המלאך הקדוש טהריא"ל, כדפי' בשער היכלות הקדושה בפ"א. ותחת הממונה הזה דומ"ה יש ממונה אחר ושמו פתו"ת ותחת ידו כמה אלף ורבוא מלאכי חבלה והוא עומד לפתות בני אדם מדרך טובה להביט בניאוף ובזנות, כשמו כן פעולתו. וכל מלאכי חבלה שעמו הולכים לפני בני אדם להיות עיניהם אל העבירה והממונה זה נקרא ג"כ סרסרים (נ"א סרסורי"ה] כי הוא סרסור העבירה. והממונה הזה עומד על הקבר בזמן שהגוף נדון ושובר עיניו מפני שהטה אחרי עצתו. כי כן דרך הקליפה להשטין למטה ועולה ומקטרג למעלה ויורד ונוטל נשמתו וכל הנמשך אחריו נוטל הוא עצמו נקמתו ממנו. לפנים בהיכל זה כמה נחשים ועקרבים הנושכים הנשמה כשהיא נכנסת שם. ועל כל ההיכל הזה יש ממונה א' ושמו גמגימ"א [נ"א גמגומ"א] והוא אדום כוורד והוא עומד להשטין כשתפלתו של אדם נדחית מהיכל א' של קדושה כדפי' בשער היכלות פ"א. וכאשר זה מקבל התפלה ההיא הנדחית, עולה עמה עד היכל ז' של קליפה ומשם מלמד עליו חובה ומזכיר עונותיו. וזש"ה (איוב א׳:ו׳) ויבא גם השטן בתוכם ג"ם הוא שם השטן שהוא גמגימ"א [נ"א גמגומ"א]. ממונים לאחוז בכל מיני דברי ניבול או דבר פגם שמוציא האדם בשפתיו ושומרים אותו וכאשר האדם אח"כ מוציא מפיו דברי קדושה כמו דברי תורה או תפלה אז מטמאים את [נ"א נטמאים אותם] הדברים הקדושים בטומאה ההיא שהוציא מפיו קודם לכן, ואז כביכול תשש כח קדושה ואז אוי לו אוי לנפשו של החוטא ההוא. עוד בהיכל הזה על כל אלו הממונים ממונה אחד ושמו (טפסריני"א) נ"א ספסיריט"א ותחת ידו כמה מלאכי חבלה והם ממונים על כל אותם הדברים שאדם זורק בכעס מידו ולוקחים הדבר ההוא ומעלים אותו למעלה ואומרים זה קרבן של פלוני שהקריב אלינו ומכריזים ווי לפלוני שזנה אחרי אלהי נכר ובת קול יוצאות שנית ואומרת אוי להם כי נדדו ממנו וההיכל הזה נק' שטן בור:

Chapter 26:2. Explanation of the second palace.

26:2:1 The second palace is the one called Corruption [שחת, shachat]; and it is impure and it is darkness. In this palace there are three entrances to three sides. At one entrance stands one appointed one, and his name is Astiria; and under his hand several thousands and myriads, appointed over those who emit seed in vain, who do not see the face of the Shekhinah. And these come and defile them in this world; and afterward, when his soul goes out, these take hold of him and bring him in to be judged among them. And these are called boiling semen; and these take the heat of the transgression and the seed and raise it above, and it causes, God forbid, the holy covenant to be subdued [another version: to enter] beneath the husks. At the second entrance there is one appointed one, and his name is Taskifa; and he is appointed over all those who come upon beasts, or upon the severe forbidden unions in the Torah; and under his hand several thousands and myriads of angels of ruin; and their way with this man is like the way of those of whom we spoke above. In this palace there is one appointed one, and in his hand the cup [another version: whose name is Kos (Cup)] of staggering. And all those killed by the court, who were killed and drank the cup of staggering in this world, are saved from the cup of staggering of the world to come. And what is the cup of staggering? When the soul goes out from the body, this appointed one [in whose hand is] the cup of staggering takes hold of him and makes him drink the cup of staggering. And in the cup are three drops: one gravel, and one more bitter than death, and one dregs. And in this palace stands one appointed one, and his name is Ni'atziriel; and from him go out the three drops of which we spoke, and they enter the cup of staggering; and from there they make him drink it on the day of death. And they are the three drops that are on the sword of the angel of death, of which our Rabbis, of blessed memory, spoke (in Tractate Avodah Zarah, fol. 20b). At the third entrance there is one appointed one, and his name is Sangadiel; and he is appointed over all those who bring in and defile the holy covenant in the daughter of a strange god. And all those who defile themselves with foreign women: here the forms of the women with whom they defiled themselves are portrayed, in this appointed one and in several thousand appointed ones that are under his hand. And when his soul goes out they defile it in that form, and it binds itself in this palace. And this is what our Rabbis, of blessed memory, said (in Tractate Sotah, fol. 3b): "'And he hearkened not unto her, to lie by her, or to be with her' (Gen. 39:10): to lie by her in this world, to be with her in the world to come." Within this palace there is one impure spirit, and on it depend all the sorceries and all kinds of divinations; and all of them depend on the impurity of boiling seed. And this was the wisdom of Balaam the wicked, who would defile himself with his she-ass, and then he would succeed in his sorceries. And on account of this this palace is called impure Corruption; for "he is unclean, and Unclean shall he cry." Beneath this upper impure spirit there is in this palace a further impure spirit, and its name is Sartia; and several thousands and myriads of angels of ruin beneath it. And they are ready, at the hour that the dream descends from the side of holiness: this spirit and all the angels of ruin that are with it descend with that dream and mix with it, and make known to that man words of falsehood, words of mockery, with the true dream. And so our Rabbis, of blessed memory, said (Berakhot 55a): "Just as it is impossible for grain without straw, so it is impossible for a dream without idle things." And from the side of this palace go out two spirits that turn about, sometimes men, sometimes women. And when the upper impure spirits journey, from the side of their journeying the two come into being as flames and blazes, and go and spread out in the world, and appear to human beings in a dream in the likeness of beautiful women, and defile them with nocturnal emission. And all this is for one who is drawn after their words and draws their spirit upon himself by his deeds. And this is what the Torah warned: "then you shall keep yourself from every evil thing" (Deut. 23:10), as our Rabbis, of blessed memory, explained (in Tractate Ketubbot, fol. 46a): that a man should not think by day and come to emission at night, God forbid. And on this matter he explained in the Zohar, Parashat Terumah (fol. 130a), and these are his words: "One appointed one stands on that side, over that hollow of the north, with all those bands of demons; Sanugiria is his name. And at the hour that that smoke bends its way and rises, this appointed one and sixty thousand myriad other camps all stand ready to receive it and to be nourished from it; and they stand in that hollow and enter by one entrance that is called Keri. And this is the secret of what is written (Lev. 26:21), 'And if you walk contrary [keri] unto Me,' etc.; and it is written, 'then I will walk contrary unto you in fury' (Lev. 26:28): in that agitation that goes out from the entrance of Keri. And these are those who roam at night. And at the hour that the souls go out to rise to be seen above, these go out and accuse them, so that they cannot rise and be seen above, except those holy pious ones who split firmaments and airs and rise. And these bands of demons go out and make known lying words to human beings, and appear to them in other likenesses, and laugh with them until they emit seed; and they are called masters of keri, because those who go out from the entrance of Keri caused it to them. And at the hour that the limbs and the fats were consumed, that smoke would sate them and nourish them; as is their honour, so is their sustenance, what is fitting for them; and by this they did not go out and did not roam in the Holy Land." Thus far his words.

שכינה ואלו באים ומטמאים אותם בעולם הזה ואחר כך כשנשמתו יוצאת אלו אוחזים אותו ומכניסים אותו להיות נדון בהם ואלו נקראים שכבת זרע רותחת ואלו לוקחים חימום העבירה והזרע ומעלים אותו למעלה וגורם להיות ח"ו ברית קדש נכנע [נ"א נכנס] תחת הקליפות. בפתח שני יש ממונה אחד ושמו טסקיפ"ה והוא ממונה על כל אותם הבאים על הבהמות או על העריות החמורות שבתורה ותחת ידו כמה אלפים ורבואות מלאכי חבלה ודרכם עם האיש הזה כדרך אותם אשר אמרנו למעלה. בהיכל הזה יש ממונה אחד ובידו כוס [נ"א ששמו כו"ס] התרעלה וכל אותם הרוגי ב"ד שנהרגו ושתו כוס התרעלה בעולם הזה נצולו מכוס תרעלת עולם הבא. ואיזהו כוס התרעלה, בצאת הנשמה מהגוף הממונה הזה [שבידו] כוס התרעלה אוחז בו ומשקהו כוס תרעלה ובכוס ג' טפות הא' חצ"ץ והאחד מר ממות והאחד קובעת. ובהיכל הזה עומד ממונה אחד ושמו ניאציריא"ל וממנו יוצאין הג' טפות שאמרנו ונכנסות בכוס התרעלה ומשם משקין אותו ביום המות והם הג' טפות שבחרב של מלאך המות שאמרו רז"ל (במס' ע"ז ד"כ ע"ב). בפתח ג' יש ממונה אחד ושמו סנגדיא"ל והוא ממונה על כל אותם המכניסים ומטמאים ברית קודש בבת אל נכר. וכל אותם המטמאים עצמם בנכריות כאן הם מצטיירים צורות הנשים אשר טמאו עצמן בממונה הזה ובכמה אלף ממונים אשר תחת ידו. ובצאת נשמתו מטמאין אותה בצורה ההיא ונקשרת עצמה בהיכל הזה וז"ש רז"ל (במס' סוטה דף ג') ולא שמע אליה לשכב אצלה להיות עמה, לשכב אצלה בעה"ז להיות עמה לעה"ב. בתוך ההיכל הזה רוח א' טמא ובו תלויים כל הכשופים וכל מיני נחושים וכלם תלויים בטומאת זרע רותח וזה היה חכמת בלעם הרשע שהיה מטמא עצמו באתונו ואז היה מצליח בכשופיו. ועל זה נקרא ההיכל הזה שח"ת טמא כי טמא הוא וטמא יקרא. תחת הרוח העליון הטמא הזה יש בהיכל הזה עוד רוח טמא ושמו סרטי"א וכמה אלף ורבואות מלאכי חבלה תחתיו והם מזומנים בשעה שיורד החלום מצד הקדושה הרוח הזה וכל מלאכי חבלה שעמו יורדים עם החלום ההוא ומתערבים עמו ומודיעים לאיש ההוא דברי שקר דברי התול עם החלום הצודק וכן ארז"ל (ברכות דף נ"ה) כשם שא"א לבר בלא תבן כך א"א לחלום בלא דברים בטלים. ומצד ההיכל הזה יוצאים שני רוחות מתהפכים לפעמים אנשים לפעמים נשים וכאשר נוסעים רוחות טמאים עליונים מצד נסיעתן מתהוים השנים להבים ושלהובים והולכים ומתפשטים בעולם ומתראים לבני אדם בחלום כדמות נשים יפות ומטמאים אותם בקרי וכל זה הוא לנמשך אחרי דבריהם ומושך במעשיו את רוחם עליו וזהו שהזהירה התורה ונשמרת מכל דבר רע כדפירשו רז"ל (במס' כתובות דף מ"ו) שלא יהרהר אדם ביום ויבא לידי קרי בלילה ח"ו. וכענין הזה פי' בזוהר פרשת תרומה (דף ק"ל.) וז"ל חד ממנא קיימא לההוא סטרא על ההוא נוקבא דצפון בכל אינון חבילי טהירין סנוגי"ריא שמיה. ובשעתא דההוא תננא עקים אורחיה וסליק, האי ממנא ושתין אלף רבוא משריין אחרנין כולהו מתעתדן לקבלא ליה ולאתזנא מניה וקיימן בההוא נוקבא ועאלין בחד פתחא דאקרי קר"י ודא איהו רזא דכתיב (ויקרא כו) ואם תלכו עמי קרי וגו' וכתיב והלכתי עמכם בחמת קרי בההוא רוגזא דנפיק מפתחא דקרי. ואלין אינון דמשטטי בליליא. ובשעתא דנשמתין נפקין לסלקא לאתחזאה לעילא אלין נפקין ומקטרגין לון דלא יכלין לסלקא ולאתחזאה לעילא בר אנון חסידין קדישין דאינון בקעין רקיעין ואוירין וסלקין. ואלין חבילי טהירין נפקין ומודעין מלין כדיבין לבני נשא ואתחזיין לון בדיוקנין אחרנין וחייכאן בהו עד דאושדין זרעא ואקרון מאריהון דקרי בגין דאינון דנפקין מפתחא דקרי גרמו לון. ובשעתא דמתעכלין אמורין ופדרין ההוא תננא הוה רוי לון וזן לון כפום יקרא דלהון הכי מזונא דילהון מה דאתחזי לון ובהאי לא נפקי ולא משטטי בארעא קדישא עכ"ל.

26:2:2 And what he said, "that smoke bends," he explained above in the matter, and explained that in the morning continual offering and in the afternoon continual offering the wind did not overcome the column of smoke; but at night the smoke would bend of itself to the side of the north, so that these masters of keri be nourished from it, so that they not roam in the Land of Israel. And on the matter of the masters of keri it is further explained in the Zohar, Parashat Bereshit (fol. 55a), that Naamah is the mother of the demons; and she is of the seed of Cain; and after her the sons of God, Uza and Azael, went astray. And the children of her children are those who lead human beings astray in the matter of heat; and sometimes also the women are heated and conceive from them and bear demons and evil spirits. And it is possible that all the spirits come into being from this palace, to lead human beings astray, and are clothed in the form of human beings through Naamah. Further, it is explicit there that the first Adam, in the hundred and thirty years that he separated from his wife before he begot Seth, would beget spirits and demons whose work is to lead human beings astray in the matter of keri; and their form is like the form of human beings, and they do not change from form to form, after the way of the other demons; except that they have no hair on their head. And possibly the reason is to show that they have no root above; for the hairs point to the secret of the upper roots, as we shall explain in the Gate of the Soul. And it is possible that they are powers from this palace that were clothed and came into being through Adam in those years. And he explained there (fol. 47b) that these are called "the plagues of the children of men" (2 Sam. 7:14):

Chapter 26:3. Explanation of the third palace.

26:3:1 The third palace is called the Palace of Gloom [Ofil]; and it is darker and gloomier than the first ones; and it is called Silence [Dumah]. For even though we said that he descends into the first palace, his principal is in this palace. And over against this the evil inclination is called Enemy. In this palace there are four entrances, and over them four appointed ones, as we shall explain. At the first entrance, one appointed one, and his name is (Katritia) [another version: Skafortia]; and he stands in the power of agitation, when judgment is stretched out in the world; and he is the stumbling of the wicked. Of this it is said (Prov. 4:19), "The way of the wicked is as darkness; they know not at what they stumble." This appointed one stands at the parting of the ways, to make stumble with his plague those who go alone on the roads and in the markets; and he can injure them if he meets them; and this is when he rules in the world in the force of the stretched-out judgment, God forbid. At the second entrance, one appointed one, Negadiel [another version: Sangadiel] is his name; and under his hand several thousands and myriads of angels of ruin; and all of them stand to receive the notes of judgment from outside the third palace, the palace [another version: from the palaces] of holiness. And when he receives the notes of the judgments he descends to the two preceding palaces, which are Corruption and Pit; and there he has several thousands and myriads of angels of ruin; and all of them roam in the world to complete that judgment. At the third entrance, one appointed one, and his name is Angirihon [another version: Angaryon]; and he is appointed over the fever that burns within the bones, and all kinds of evil sicknesses that inflame and burn the body; and from him go out several thousands and myriads appointed over this matter, like him. At the fourth entrance, one appointed one, and his name is Askarah [Croup]; and this one is appointed over the killing and death of children (up to thirteen years old); and he appears as a woman who rears and nurses them and plays with them, and strangles them; and their soul [is handed over] into the hand of Azriel, the angel who is in the third palace of holiness, as we explained there. In the middle of this palace stands one spirit, and it is called Agirusion [Agirison]; and this one is appointed over the death of the young men from thirteen years old to twenty years old, of whom it is said (Prov. 13:23), "but there is that is destroyed by want of judgment"; for since they do not guard themselves from sin when they are small, because of the evil of their upbringing, and he sees in them a sign that they will sin and be corrupted, he kills them before they come to sin. And of this it is said, "and behold, it was very good": this is the angel of death. From this palace two spirits spread out, and their names are Anger [Af] and Fury [Cheimah]. These Moses our teacher, peace be upon him, saw in his descending from the mountain, and said (Deut. 9:19), "For I was afraid of the anger and the fury." And under their hand several thousands and myriads of angels of ruin; and all of them are appointed over all those who hear a ban or excommunication from the mouth of a master of Torah and do not lay it to heart; and likewise over all those who mock the words of Torah or the words of the sages. And likewise all of them encamp and go to provoke all those who occupy themselves with Torah, and likewise in doing a commandment, so that they not rejoice in it and be saddened with it. Beneath these spirits there is one spirit, and its name is Sikhsikha; and when a man informs on his fellow or stirs up strife [mesakhsekh] against him, this one is aroused with that informing, to record the matter and to kill by that informing. Come and see: even though the Holy One, blessed be He, fixed for all the wild animals and serpents a time for the shedding of their skin, nevertheless [all] depends on evil speech and on informing. When a man informs on his fellow, the lower serpents shed their skin and give voices; and those voices rise above, into the palace that is called Pit, where there are several serpents and scorpions, as we explained above; and all of them plead accusation, to arouse the primordial serpent, the great serpent; and he sheds his skin; and all his scales, that is to say, his camps and his hosts, separate from him, and he brings them down below into the palaces; and he rises to plead provocation with that informing. And if not for the shedding of his skin, no man could stand before him; may the Compassionate One save us. And all this is because of the force of informing and evil speech; and not for nothing our Rabbis, of blessed memory, said (Arakhin 15b) that it is a most severe transgression, like idolatry and forbidden unions and bloodshed:

Chapter 26:4. Explanation of the fourth palace.

26:4:1 The fourth palace is called Guilt [Chovah], and it is called the miry clay [tit ha-yaven] (Ps. 40:3); and over against this the evil inclination is called the stone of stumbling. This palace is called Guilt for the reason that it is over against the Palace of Merit. And all the debts of human beings and their iniquities are in this palace; and several angels of ruin gather them and bring them into this palace. And on the day of judgment, on Rosh Hashanah, when they weigh debt and merit: if the scale-pan of merit (prevails, from the powers of the side of holiness they take hold of him) [of the side of holiness prevails, he is holy], and they judge him for life; and if the pan of liability prevails, several of the masters of debt take hold of him and say, "He is ours, he is ours," and rejoice in him, and they judge him for death. Come and see: as a man conducts himself in this world, so it is drawn and bestowed upon him from above. If he pursues the impure inclination and the transgressions, the spirit of impurity pursues him and helps him to be defiled more, and causes him to come to more severe transgressions and more crushed impurities than the first. And if a man purifies himself and pursues holiness and sanctifies himself, the spirit of holiness is drawn after him and bestowed upon him, and purifies him and sanctifies him and clothes him with humility and fear, and brings him to greater purity; as our Rabbis, of blessed memory, said in Tractate Avodah Zarah (fol. 20b): "Watchfulness brings to alacrity," etc. In this palace is the place of all those that are called other gods; and they are those who seduce human beings with the pleasures of this world, and with adulteries and harlotry. And over all these [that are called] other gods there is a spirit that rules over all of them; and it is called El Nekhar [a strange god]. And it is what seduces those who learn Torah in the house of study with evil, strange thoughts and with defilements; and they say to him: Why do you stand here and waste the days of your youth in the occupation of learning, and weaken your strength? Is it not better for you to rejoice in your youth in adulteries and harlotry? And when a man is seduced after it, several masters of impurity go after him to defile him in this world; and all these are called boiling excrement, who stand to soil the man, as we explained. In the middle of this palace there is one spirit, and its name is Plague [Nega]; and from it spreads out one spirit, and it is called the plague of leprosy. And they stand to defile all those masters of evil speech, more than all those who defile it. And the upper Plague of which he spoke is appointed to curse all those tables that were not arranged on the Sabbath as is fitting; and all of them are set out before him, and he curses them with grievous curses, which there is no need to lengthen upon; may the Compassionate One save us. And as the way of holiness is for good, so the way of impurity is for evil. In this palace all things depend for evil, except for children, life and sustenance, which depend above, in the other palaces, and descend to this palace; and from there accusers go out for evil: whether to diminish the children of a man, (or) to kill them, or to diminish their sustenance; may the Compassionate One save us. From this palace goes out one impure spirit, and it is called Aririya; and under its hand several thousands and myriads of angels of ruin; and all of them are called "they that curse the day" (Job 3:8). And they are appointed over one who curses himself in irritation, and over oaths of imprecation (and the curses); and they take that curse and raise it above, and arouse with it the primordial serpent, "leviathan the crooked serpent," so that it rise and arouse grievous curses, God forbid. And this is what Job intended (Job 3:8) in saying, "Let them curse it that curse the day, who are ready to rouse up leviathan": who are those destined to arouse leviathan, which is the crooked serpent; may the Compassionate One save us:

Chapter 26:5. Explanation of the fifth palace.

26:5:1 The fifth palace is called Sheol; over against this the evil inclination is called Uncircumcised [Arel]; and this is the principal of the foreskin. In this palace there is one entrance, and over it one appointed one, and his name is Enmity [Eivah]; and he arouses strifes and enmities in the world; and after his name this palace is called Enmity. In this palace there is one impure spirit that rules over all, and its name is Shoded [Despoiler]; and it arouses destruction and breaking; and it crouches in the high mountains, among rocks and crags and forests. From this palace draw all those despoilers; and from there go out all those murderers and killers, and go after "the flame of the sword which turned every way" (Gen. 3:24), to kill everything. [From there goes out one spirit, and it is called Shod (Destruction).] And when there is famine in the world, God forbid, this spirit that is called Shod couples with another spirit that is called Kafan [Famine]: this one to despoil the people of the world and to kill them, and this is what is called Shod; and that one to curse the food in their bowels, so that they are not sated; and it is what is called Kafan. And when there are among Israel doers of kindness at the time of famine, they cause these not to rule in the world over Israel; and they rule over the nations of the world; and the side of impurity is subdued, and Israel are blessed. And when, God forbid, there is none among Israel who does kindness, and the nations of the world do kindness, then, God forbid, the husk prevails; and one of these bestows to the nations and draws all the holy effluence. And of this it is said (Song 1:6), "they made me keeper of the vineyards" [these are the nations of the world]; "but my own vineyard have I not kept": these are Israel; for all the effluence descends from holiness to the other side, God forbid. Beneath these of which we spoke there are impure spirits, and they are called Foreskin [Orlah]; and they are the branches of the foreskin and the boughs of the foreskin. And over all of them is one appointed one, and he is called Gezar Dinia; and he is appointed over all those who do not keep the years of orlah, and over those who delay the circumcision. And this is the reason for "a bridegroom of blood, because of the circumcision" (Exod. 4:26), which Zipporah said. And he is appointed over the man when he defiles his covenant, to bring him into Sheol and Abaddon to judge him there. In the middle of this palace there is one spirit that lies in wait on the roads and in the places, to see those who transgress the Torah, to inform on them above, to bring enmity between the upper ones and the lower ones. And likewise all of them stand to seduce the man from the good way to the evil way; and afterward it rises and accuses him and kills him; and it is found that they bring him out of this world and out of the world to come. And there is no enmity greater than this; and therefore it is called the Palace of Enmity. And this spirit is called Afrira; and the intent is the dust of ashes, which does not make fruits and does not grow plants; the reverse of the holy dust (which is Malkhut), which makes fruits. And the reason is that it has already been explained that every spirit of impurity is castrated. And this is from the dust of the burning of the sin offering; for it is held in the sin, which is the crooked serpent. And further, for when a man sins this dust prevails through the serpent; and this is included in "the bitter waters that cause the curse"; and this is the dust of the Tabernacle from which the priest would make the sotah drink, because she played the harlot and inclined after the serpent, the harlot. For this they make her drink of the dust of the floor of the Tabernacle, which is the dust, in the bitter waters; and this spirit rules over her and destroys her. And these are "the last waters are obligatory." May the Name save us:

Chapter 26:6. Explanation of the sixth palace.

26:6:1 The sixth palace: this palace is higher than all the palaces, like the side of holiness; for "God has made even the one over against the other," as we explained. In this palace there are four entrances, and in it four impure powers: one Death [Mavet], and one Evil [Ra], and one Shadow of Death [Tzalmavet], and one Gloom [Ofel]. These stand always to do evil and to accuse. And the four of them are united as one, like the four of the side of holiness; and on account of the union mentioned it is called the House of Association [beit chaver], as it is written (Prov. 21:9; 25:24), "than with a contentious woman in a wide house [beit chaver]"; and this is "or a charmer [chover chaver]" (Deut. 18:11). Of this palace it is said (Prov. 27:6), "but the kisses of an enemy are profuse." Here stand all those kisses and seductions and bodily cravings of harlotry and adultery, through which a man is driven out from this world and the world to come. And all those that are written in Proverbs (chapter 7), in the passage "For at the window of my house I looked forth through my lattice," etc., all those seductions are from the side of the spirit of this palace. And of it it is said, "her feet abide not in her house" (Prov. 7:11); for she goes to seduce human beings; and after she has seduced him she makes him taste "the cruel venom of asps" (Deut. 32:33). And she brings out his soul with her sword, and on it are three drops. With the first his soul is confused and finds no place to rest; and with the second his soul is uprooted and goes out; with the third his face turns green, and his soul goes out "like a hair," etc. All this because he was drawn after her; and this is what the verse said (Prov. 5:4), "But her end is bitter as wormwood," etc. In this palace stand all those accusers who seduce a man in his clothes and in his garments, and make him curl and finger his hair, and wash his body, and adorn his appearance, so that the women will look at him. And over this matter one appointed one, Sktupa is his name. Within this palace [another version: And beneath this appointed one] stands another appointed one (and his name is Mirror), in which and through which the man sees himself, and beautifies himself and curls; and afterward he looks in the mirror and is clothed in it with pride and arrogance and haughtiness. And then another power that is in the palace is aroused, and its name is Asirta, which is appointed over all the dreams, to show a man a likeness that he will be drawn after, to lose his soul. And this appointed one arouses another appointed one that is under his hands, and descends and arouses in the lower palace in which is Askarah; and there he arouses a female [one]; and she is Lilith, the mother of the demons. And when a man in his haughtiness arouses the appointed one Asirta, then Lilith is bound with him; and then on every new moon that mirror of which we spoke is aroused with her, of Lilith; and sometimes the man is injured by them; and he falls to the ground and has a fit and is injured. And all this the mirror caused him; and according to the greatness of his pride with which he was proud, so is the greatness of his evil and his injury and his impurity, "till a dart strike through his liver," etc. (Prov. 7:23). May the Compassionate One save us:

Chapter 26:7. Explanation of the seventh palace.

26:7:1 The seventh palace: this palace is the palace of the dregs of the wine, to be drunk from it, as it is written (Gen. 9:21), "and he was drunken, and he was uncovered within his tent." And they are the grapes that Eve pressed, through which she caused death to her husband and to all those who come after him: "their grapes are grapes of gall" (Deut. 32:32), etc. In this palace stand all those impure souls, to bestow them on all those who defile themselves with a menstruant and a married woman, and on the son that goes out from them, who is a mamzer. And he is called mamzer because he is me-el zar [from a strange god]. And according to the transgression and the matter of the evil inclination, so he draws upon himself the soul from this palace; and "all that see them shall acknowledge them," that they are an unfit seed, a mamzer, from a strange god; and all his deeds testify about him that he is unfit, as he called to a strange god, and they recognized him, that he is from a strange god. From this palace goes out one spirit that is appointed over all those souls, and its name is Tzefoni [the Northern one]; and the sign: "But I will remove far off from you the northern one" (Joel 2:20). And over against this palace Gehinnom is called the Nethermost Land [eretz tachtit]. In this palace there is a thin point, near to the sefirot of the husks; and from it go out (like those) [another version: all those] thin spirits that roam in the world, which see and are not seen. And these stand to do miracles for human beings; for these do not stand in the husks so filthy as the others. And over these is one appointed one, Nesira is his name; and it is an Aramaic word, and its explanation is cut off; for the intent is that it is most separated from the impure husks; and we explained this matter at length above in the previous gate. And these fly in the air and refine to the side [another version: and do harm from the side] of their impurity, for doing [another version: from doing] miracles in the world for those who stand on the side of holiness. From this palace go out strong fire and snow and a strengthening wind, to judge the wicked; and this is what the verse says (Ps. 68:15), "it snowed in Zalmon." In this palace stand four entrances, open to four directions; and these take hold of the side of holiness; and there the light of holiness shines; and it is a sweet place for the pious of the nations of the world, who did not do evil to Israel. At the entrance of the palace, in the middle, outside, are six entrances, and they are directed over against the holiness; and there are places set in order for the kings of the nations of the world who did not do evil to Israel and did not oppress them, and protected them; and they have honour there for this; and the light of holiness shines for them within that darkness. And of this it is said (Isa. 14:18), "All the kings of the nations, all of them, lie in glory." And if they oppressed Israel, several angels of ruin take hold of them and judge them three times every day with several strange judgments; and each and every day they testify concerning Israel and concerning their good faith. Thus far, in general, the matter of these palaces. However, I copied it from the words of Rashbi, peace be upon him, in Parashat Pekudei, fol. 263. And at first sight it would appear that they are palaces for the sefirot of the husks of which we spoke in the previous gate; however, what is reasonable is that the sefirot are included in the palaces; and the palaces and the husks are all one thing and one matter. And over against these seven are seven kinds of evil inclination, which receives power from these seven; and therefore the evil inclination is called by seven names; and these are they: Evil, Impure, Abomination, Enemy, Stone of Stumbling, the Northern One; and over against these seven dwellings in Gehinnom, in which the sinner is judged in these seven kinds; and these are they: Pit, Corruption, Silence, Miry Clay, Sheol, Shadow of Death, Nethermost Land. And they are this over against that: over against purity, impurity; over against fitness, unfitness; over against permitted, forbidden; over against merit, guilt. For "God has made even the one over against the other," as we explained in the previous chapter:

Chapter 26:8. Explanation of the three divisions of the air with respect to the powers of the husks.

26:8:1 The commentators wrote in the name of the Geonim that from the power of impurity three divisions are drawn:

כתבו המפרשים בשם הגאונים כי מכח הטומאה נמשכים ג' חלקים:

26:8:2 "The first division is Ashmedai, the great king of the demons; and he has no permission to accuse or to spoil except on the second day of the week." And it seems to me that the reason is that on it the luminaries were smitten; and for this cause the husk prevailed from the side of the diminishment, as is explained in the words of Rashbi, peace be upon him, in many places. "And Ashmedai is under the dominion of Samael. And know that Samael makes use of Lilith the Elder, his bride, as we explained in the previous gate; and Ashmedai makes use of Lilith the Younger. And these two powers with their mates: each one of them was born with his mate, two-faced, like Adam and Eve. And Lilith the Maiden, the bride of Ashmedai, is the daughter of the king whose name is Kafkefoni, whom we shall explain further on. And the ancient sages said that jealousy was born between Samael, the great prince, and Ashmedai, the king of the demons, over Lilith, whom they call Lilith the Maiden, who is in the form of a beautiful woman from her head to her navel, and from the navel downward blazing fire; for like mother, like daughter: she is the daughter of Mehetabel, the wife of Kafkefoni, the mother of Lilith the Maiden. And both of them, daughter and mother, are occupied in their war with Lilith the Great, the Elder, the wife of Samael. And from the offspring of Ashmedai and Lilith his wife a great prince was formed in heaven, ruling over eighty thousand destroyers and spoilers; and they call him the Sword of King Ashmedai; and his name is Alpafonias; and his face blazes like a flaming fire.

26:8:4 "The second division: Kafkefoni and his wife Tzar'ita. They used her half the year, and half the year they used another woman, and her name is Sagrit. And the offspring that go out from them are forms different from one another; they have bodies, and the recognition of their faces is in two likenesses: the children of Tzar'ita are like the likeness of lepers;" and there are those who say that the lepers are children of menstruation, who become leprous at ten, at twenty, at thirty, at forty, at fifty, at sixty, at seventy [years], as explained in Midrash Tanchuma, Parashat Metzora; "they are born from the power of this ugly offspring. And the children of Sagrit have no recognition of face, but are like the likeness of those afflicted with plague. And there is a long war between them; evil spirits spread out in the air; and all the injuries and the earthquakes go out from the power of these mixtures. החלק הב' קפקפוני ואשתו צרעיתא שמשו בה חצי השנה וחצי השנה שמשו באשה אחרת ושמה סגרי"ת והתולדות היוצאות מהם צורות משונות זו מזו יש להם גופות והכרת פניהם בשני דמיונות בני צרעיתא כדמות מצורעים ויש אומרים כי המצורעים בני נדה שמצטרעים לי' לכ' לל' למ' לנ' לס' לע' כדפי' במדרש תנחומא פרשת מצורע [ד] הם נולדים מכח זה התולדה המגונה. ובני סגרית אין להם הכרת פנים אלא כדמיון מנוגעים ומלחמה ארוכה ביניהם רוחות רעות מתפשטות באויר וכל הזיקים והזועות יוצאות מכח אלו התערובות וממשלת אשמדאי ואימתו מוטלת עליהם וממה שנתחדש להם הם מודיעים אל שלמטה מהם:

26:8:5 "The third division: they are the harmful spirits, created and clothed in several kinds of forms, different from one another. There are of them whose form is the form of dogs; and if the iniquity of the man causes it, the dog formed from this evil offspring happens upon the man and bites him; and his healing is exceedingly hard to be healed. And sometimes his form is changed, in his iniquity, to the form of the dog, and he is changed into that form; and he barks and cries out and bites, and that dog bites again; and he has no healing forever until he dies; and he is again clothed in the mixture of that air by the power of that form; and this is his punishment and his annihilation. And concerning this secret the sweet singer of Israel prayed and said (Ps. 22:21), 'Deliver my soul from the sword, my only one from the power of the dog.' And there are of them whose form is the form of goat-demons, the form of goats; and of these was the chariot of Uza and Azael. And the two of them alone have literally the form of man; and when they fell from the holy place they were clothed in the power of this air that is over us, and were made corporeal in the corporeality of human beings; and the upper power was weakened, and they received a lower power. But the offspring that went out from them were tall in exceeding stature and in strong might beyond all human beings. And there are of them the form of women, seen by human beings; and there is among them only falsehood and lie; and they have jealousy of human beings, and their intent is to deceive them and to make them stumble; and they would almost consume and destroy all that is seen by them, were it not for the attribute of Malkhut, which makes rule, from her emanation, a prince beloved and dear before the Shekhinah, whose name is Yofiel, the Prince of the Face. And were it not for his providence and his terror over them, they would leave no creature in the world. And this prince subdues them and humbles them," as we explained in the Gate of the Palaces of Holiness, in the third palace, chapter 3. "And the prince over this third division, his name is Kafkefoni; and the name of his wife, Mehetabel the daughter of Matred. And the offspring that go out from the two of them leap [kofetzim] with one leap from the end of the air to the end of the air. And permission is given them sometimes to do good and to drive away the injuries from human beings; and they make known future things to human beings when they are seen by them in the forms of human beings; and they have no dominion in falsehoods nor in lies. And whoever asks of them will find his question according to his will, if the asker is worthy to be answered; and if not, they answer him, by the power of the adjuration, that permission has not been given them to answer his question. And sometimes, according to the power and the dominion given to this prince Kafkefoni, he joins and cleaves with one daughter that he has, and her name is Lilith; she is the wife of Ashmedai, as we explained above; and his cause is his cleaving to this Lilith." Thus far the words of the Geonim, peace be upon them. Further, we have found in the words of the commentators: "Three bands of angels of ruin were created in the world, and these are they. One band of them was created when the serpent came upon Eve on the eve of the Sabbath. And the second band was created from 'the plagues of the children of men,' from the emissions that the first Adam saw all those hundred and thirty years that he separated from Eve; and likewise they come into being from the emissions of other human beings. And the third band is created from them; for they are fruitful and multiply, as our Rabbis, of blessed memory, explained (in Tractate Chagigah, fol. 16a): 'Six things were said of demons: three like the ministering angels, and three like human beings'; and one of them, they are fruitful and multiply like human beings." Thus far his words.

26:8:6 And in the Zohar (see the Introduction to the Zohar, fol. 17), in different places (see the Zohar, Bereshit, fol. 48), it appears that the demons are the powers of the impurities and the husk that spread out on the eve of the Sabbath; and it wished to be strengthened in a body, and bestowed and brought out those powers, which are the demons; and because the day was sanctified, impure souls remained without a body at all; as is expounded on the verse "which God created to make" (Gen. 2:3). And they stood and were clothed and took on body through Cain, the nest of impurity; and they took on body only for a time. And of them the verse said (Gen. 36:24), "this was that Anah that found the yemim [mules] in the wilderness," etc. And behold, Anah pursued these impurities, and found them in the wilderness; for there is their dwelling place, as is fitting for them, "in a land of drought and of the shadow of death" (Jer. 2:6), a place where there is no settlement at all. And it is called yemim, written defective; and the reason is because they are neither from the sixth day nor from the Sabbath day; for they were created at twilight; and therefore yemim is written, for they are not, not from one day and not from two days. And Anah, in pursuing them, found them. And the reason is that Zibeon came upon his mother and begot a mamzer son like this, who is Anah; and because of his impurity he pursued the mamzerut; and "God delivered into his hand" a find like this: he met one like himself; and they taught him to bring out mamzerim like themselves; and they would teach him several kinds of divinations and sorceries, to bring forth impurities and husks in the world. And behold, in the earth [Arka] are the children of the children of Cain; and over them in the earth are two appointed ones, princes of impurity; and of the matter of those demons and their deeds it is expounded there at length. Thus far the copying of these things has reached, to be of benefit for the understanding of the Torah and the comprehension of its secrets. And of God we ask help, to save us from all impurity and defilement, so that we may cleave to His holiness, to serve Him with a whole heart. Amen, so may it be His will.
