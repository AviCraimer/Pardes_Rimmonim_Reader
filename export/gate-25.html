Gate 25: The Gate of Substitutes

We named it "The Gate of Substitutes" because in it is explained the matter of the husks, which are evil substituted [temurat] for good, and their existence, whence they come. Its chapters number seven.

Chapter 25:1. Explanation of the coming-to-be of the husks — whence it was — through fitting analogies, and the words of Rashbi from the Zohar.

25:1:1 Gate Twenty-Five is the Gate of Substitutes:

שער כ"ה הוא שער התמורות:

25:1:2 What is intended in this gate is to explain the matter of the husks: what they are, and from what side they were emanated, and how their emanation was, and what their benefit and their matter are, as we shall explain with the help of the Rock and His salvation:

הנרצה בזה השער הוא לבאר ענין הקליפות מה הם ומאיזה צד נאצלו וכיצד אצילותם ומה תועלתם ועניינם כאשר נבאר בעזרת הצור וישעתו:

25:1:3 Chapter One:

פרק ראשון:

25:1:4 After we have spoken in the preceding chapters of all that is inscribed on the matter of the holy angels, it is fitting for us to know that "God has made even the one over against the other" (Eccl. 7:14). For just as there is the side of holiness and purity and righteousness and uprightness and the good disposition of the governance, as we explained, so there is the side of the husk, which is the crushed impurity, which is the accusation; and from its side is the one who accuses and perverts man from the straight way to a way that is not good; and they rise and accuse him and defile him; for "he is unclean, and Unclean shall he cry" (Lev. 13:45), and impurity they pursue. And there are those who ask: since the holiness and the purity and the subtlety are in the Emanation without end, and it is separated from these attributes with the utmost separation, if so, from where did the husks and the impurities go out, and where were they before their formation? And at first sight it is a mighty question, and the intelligent man will be astonished at it. And we have received on this from our teacher a fine answer; and it is the parable of the grain, cleaned of all refuse to the utmost cleanness and purity: behold, at any rate, when a man eats it and that food is digested in his bowels, much refuse and excrement remains there.

אחר שבפרקים הקודמים דברנו בכל הרשום בענין המלאכים הקדושים, ראוי לנו לדעת כי גם את זה לעומת זה עשה האלהים כי כמו שיש צד הקדושה והטהרה והצדקה והיושר וטוב תכונת ההנהגה כדפי'. ע"כ כן יש צד הקליפה שהיא הטומאה הרצוצה שהיא הקטרוג ומצדה המשטין ומעוות את האדם מדרך הישר אל דרך לא טוב ועולין ומקטרגין עליו ומטמאין אותו כי טמא הוא וטמא יקרא וטומאה ירדפו. ויש שואלין מאחר שהקדושה והטהרה והדקות הוא באצילות באין תכלית והוא מובדל מהמדות האלה תכלית ההבדל, אם כן מהיכן יצאו הקליפות והטומאות והיכן היו קודם יצירתן. ולכאורה היא שאלה עצומה וישתומם המשכיל עליה. וקבלנו בזה ממורינו תירוץ נאה והוא משל אל הדגן המנוקה מכל פסולת תכלית הנקיון והטהרה, הנה עכ"פ שיאכל האדם אותו ויתעכל המזון ההוא במעיו ישאר שם פסולת הרבה ופרש.

25:1:5 And behold, shall we say that at the time of his eating he ate that filth and excrement? No. Rather, according to the manifestation of the food before its eating, that thing eaten was cleaner than it could be in manifestation, separated from all excrement and from all filth. However, after the eating and the separation of the choice thing from it, that excrement remains, which was not until now. And so is the thing in the Emanation. The truth is that above, in the place of the Emanation, "no evil thing descends from heaven"; for above, the things are subtle to the utmost subtlety. However, in the thickening of the things and their descent in the order of the emanated rungs, it was necessary that the thing be separated, "food from within food," and refuse came into being there. A further fine parable, which settles better: the parable of the seed of man, which is of the choicest that comes into being in the body of man; and it goes out from the brain by way of the testicles to the organ; and from that drop, in the full womb, the child comes into being from it, and other filthy things apart from the child. And could it enter the mind that in the brain of man, which is the choicest of the body, the manifestation of that filth comes into being? No; certainly, if so, he would perish at once. But that filth comes into being in the drawing of the seminal drop from place to place and from rung to rung: the filths come into being in the separation of the choice part from which the child comes into being, and from the rest the filth comes into being. And so are the things in the Emanation: above, in its place, there is no evil thing; however, in its being drawn below, there comes into being from it, in the separation of the purity and the holiness, an impure thing, which is the refuse and the dross of the gold. And after we pursued the inquiry of the inquiries of wisdom, we found support on this matter in the Zohar, Parashat Terumah (fol. 167a), and these are his words, on the passage "Let there be light": "And because these are five rungs that separated and were drawn from this first light, 'light' is written five times; and all of them were from the side of the right and were included in it. And when they were included in the side of the left, they were included in the secret of the waters, which takes from the right; and therefore 'waters' is written five times. And when they were completed in the secret of the middle, 'firmament' is written five times. And therefore three they are, light, waters, firmament, corresponding to these three rungs in which all five rungs are included; and therefore in all of them five times is written, in each and every one. Here is the secret of secrets: in these three the secret of the likeness of Adam was portrayed and engraved in an engraving. For they are light at first, afterward waters, afterward the firmament spread out within them, which is the engraving that engraved the likeness of Adam; like the engraving of the form of the likeness of Adam in his begetting. For in the begetting of Adam, at first seed, which is light; for the light of all the limbs of the body is that seed, and therefore it is light; and that light is called seed, as it is written, 'Light is sown,' etc. (Ps. 97:11): it is literal seed. Afterward that seed, which is light, spread out and became waters in its moisture. It engraved more and spread out a spreading within those waters, the spreading of the body to every side. When the form and likeness of the body had been portrayed and engraved, that spreading congealed and is called firmament; and this is the 'firmament in the midst of the waters' (Gen. 1:6). And after it congealed it is written, 'And God called the firmament Heaven' (Gen. 1:8); for that moisture that flowed congealed, and that refuse remained in the body, which was within those waters. When the body was clarified and cleaned with cleanness, that moisture that flowed and remained (was) refuse, which was made within the smelting; and they are the evil, turbid waters; and from them was made the refuse, the accuser of all the world, male and female. Afterward, when those turbid waters descended and were smelted below, on the side of the left, they went out to accuse all the world. Happy is he who is saved from them. When the accuser went out, then it is written, 'Let there be lights [me'orot, defective]' (Gen. 1:14), defective, and croup was drawn out among children, and the light of the moon was lacking," etc. Thus far his words.

והנה הנאמר שבשעת אכילתו אכל הזהמא והפרש ההיא. לא. אלא לפי מציאת המזון קודם אכילתו היה הדבר הנאכל ההוא יותר נקי שהוא יכול להיות במציאות מובדל מכל פרש ומכל זוהמא. אמנם אחר האכילה והבדלת הדבר המובחר ממנו ישאר הפרש ההוא מה שלא היה עד עתה. וכ"ה הדבר בהאצילות האמת הוא כי למעלה במקום האצילות אין דבר רע יורד מן השמים כי למעלה הדברים דקים תכלית הדקות אמנם בהתעבות הדברים וירידתם בסדר מדרגות הנאצלות הוכרח היות הדבר נפרד אוכל מתוך אוכל ויתהווה שם פסולת. עוד משל נאה ומתיישב יותר להמשל אל זרע האדם אשר הוא מהמובחר המתהווה בגוף האדם והוא יוצא מהמוח דרך האשכים אל הגיד ומאותה הטפה בבטן המלאה יתהוה ממנו הולד ודברים אחרים מזוהמים מזולת הולד. וכי יעלה על הדעת שבמוח האדם שהוא מבחר הגוף יתהוה בו מציאות הזוהמא ההיא. לא. ודאי שאם כן יספה כרגע. אבל הזוהמא ההיא מתהווה בהמשכת הטפה זרעית ממקום למקום וממדרגה למדרגה מתהווה הזוהמות בהבדל המובחר שיתהוה ממנו הולד ומהשאר מתהוה הזוהמא. וכן הדברים באצילות למעלה במקומה אין דבר רע, אמנם בהמשכתה למטה יתהווה ממנו בהבדלת הטהרה והקדושה דבר טמא שהיא הפסול וסיגי הזהב. ואחר שרדפנו בחקירת דרושי החכמה מצאנו בענין זה סמך בזוהר פ' תרומה (דף קס"ז.) וז"ל בפ' יהי אור ועל דאינון ה' דרגין דאתפרשו ואתמשכו מהאי אור קדמאה כתיב אור ה' פעמים וכלהו הוו מסטרא דימינא ואתכלילו ביה. וכד אתכלילו בסטר שמאלא אתכלילו ברזא דמיא דנטיל מימינא, ובג"כ כתיב מים ה' זימנין. וכד אשתלימו ברזא דאמצעיתא כתיב רקיע ה' זמנין. וע"ד תלת אינון או"ר מי"ם רקי"ע לקבל תלת דרגין אילין דכלהו ה' דרגין אתכלילו בהו, וע"ד בכלהו כתיב ה' זמנין בכל חד וחד. הכא רזא דרזא באילין תלת אתצייר ואתגליף בגלופא רזא דיוקנא דאדם. דאינון אור בקדמיתא לבתר מים לבתר אתפשט בגווייהו רקיעא דאיהו גלופא דגליפו דיוקנא דאדם. כגוונא דגליפו ציורא דדיוקנא דאדם בתולדתיה. דהא בתולדותיה דאדם בקדמיתא זר"ע דאיהו אור דהא נהירו דכל שייפי דגופא איהו ההוא זרע ובג"כ איהו אור וההוא אור אקרי זרע דכתיב אור זרוע וגו' איהו זרע ממש. לבתר ההוא זרע דאיהו אור אתפשט ואתעביד מים בליחותא דיליה. אגליף יתיר ואתפשיט פשיטו גו אינון מים פשיטו דגופא לכל סטרין. כיון דאתצייר ואתגליף ציורא ודיוקנא דגופא אקריש ההוא פשוטו ואקרי רקיע ודא איהו רקיע בתוך המים. ובתר דאקריש כתיב ויקרא אלקים לרקיע שמים דהא אקריש ההוא ליחותא דאנגיד ואשתאר ההוא פסולת בגופא דהוו גו אינון מים. כיון דאתבריר גופא ואתנקי בנקיו ההוא לחותא דאתנגיד ואשתאר (הוה) פסולת דקא אתעביד גו היתוכא ואינון מים הרעים עכורים ומנהון אתעביד פסולת מקטרגא דכל עלמא דכר ונוקבא. לבתר כד נחתו אינון מים עכורים ואתהתכו לתתא בסטר שמאלא נפקו לקטרגא כל עלמא. זכאה איהו מאן דאשתזיב מנהון. כיון דנפיק מקטרגא כדין כתיב יהי מארת חסר ואתמשכא אסכרה ברביי וחסר נהורא דסיהרא כו' עכ"ל.

25:1:6 And his words are clear, and his intent is to what we said concerning the parable of the seed; and this is what he wished in saying "like the engraving of the form of the likeness of Adam." And from it it is explained that the principal of the portraying is in Gevurah, and there the turbid waters remained, as I explained. However, it remains for us to explain, since it is so that the matter of the husk came into being from the matter of the spreading out of the Emanation up to Gevurah: how is the husk bestowed from there by way of rungs, since there are still five rungs of holiness to complete to ten; and all the more since Beriah and Yetzirah still remain, according to what we explained above? And for this it appears that it is explained by what we explained above in the Gate of Channels, chapter 4, on the matter of the second channel that is in Yesod, from whose side is the drawing of the husks: all the surplus of the upper effluence; for all surpluses are fitting for the husks, which are the surplus. And nevertheless the name of impurity did not take effect on it until it went outside. And so they were precise in this in the language of the saying, which said "that moisture that flowed and remained," etc., "and from them was made the refuse, the accuser," etc.

ודבריו מבוארים וכוונתו אל מה שאמרנו לענין משל הזרע. וזה רצה באמרו כגוונא דגליפו ציורא דדיוקנא דאדם. ומתוכו נתבאר שעיקר הציור הוא בגבורה ושם נשארו מים עכורים כדפירשתי. אמנם נשאר לנו לבאר אחר שכן הוא שענין הקליפה נתהווה מענין התפשטות האצילות עד הגבורה איך נשפעת משם הקליפה דרך מדרגות שעדיין יש ה' מדרגות הקדושה להשלים עד י' ומה גם בהיות שנשאר עדיין בריאה ויצירה לפי מה שפי' למעלה. ולזה נראה כי נתבאר במה שפי' למעלה בשער הצנורות בפ"ד מענין צנור השני אשר ביסוד שמצדו יניקת הקליפות כל המותר מן השפע העליון כי כל מותרות ראוי אל הקליפות שהם המותר. ועכ"ז לא חל עליו שם טומאה עד היותו יוצא לחוץ וכן דקדקו בזה בלשון המאמר שאמר ההוא לחותא דאנגיד ואשתאר כו' ומנהון אתעביד פסולת מקטרגא כו'.

25:1:7 Behold, explicitly: the surplus became refuse, and it had no power to accuse or to defile until its descending below. And this is what he was further precise in his language afterward, and said, "Afterward, when those turbid waters descended and were smelted below, on the side of the left, they went out to accuse all the world." And it appears that above, in the place of their first smelting and their surplus, in the holy place, there was not yet the name of impurity on the surplus in substance, even though those waters were fit for that impurity. And from this we may infer concerning the effluence that is bestowed upon them and their sustenance that is given them from the King's table: that while they are before the King, even though it is fit for the husk, the name of impurity does not take effect on them until they descend below from the place of the channel through which it is bestowed to them. And this is no wonder; for behold, excrement (in a man): the ruling of the law is that if he is sitting and it is not seen, it does not prevent the recitation of the Shema, even though the name of excrement has already taken effect on it; and the reason is that the spirit of impurity has not ruled over it until it goes out from the body of the man entirely. And likewise the law with semen: even though it has been uprooted from its place to go out, while it has not gone out no impurity has taken effect on it, and the man is still pure; and it does not defile until it goes out from the mouth of the organ outward; for then that evil power takes effect on it. And for this that channel was needed above from the beginning; because before it goes out to the air it is already fit for them, from the place of the surplus; and from there it is separated to the left side by way of that channel, until it goes outside, where "sin crouches" (Gen. 4:7), and they receive it; and then "he is unclean," and unclean is the one who eats it; "Nabal is his name, and folly is with him" (1 Sam. 25:25):

הנה בפי' כי המותר נעשה פסולת ולא היה לו כח לקטרג או לטמא עד רדתו למטה. וזהו שדקדק אח"כ עוד בלשונו ואמר לבתר כד נחתו אינון מים עכורים ואתהתכו לתתא בסטר שמאלא נפקי לקטרגא כל עלמא ונראה שלמעלה במקום התוכם הראשון ומותרם במקום הקדוש לא היה עדיין שם טומאה על המותר בעצם, ועם היות שהיו המים ההם ראוים אל הטומאה ההיא. ומזה נקיש אל השפע הנשפע עליהם ומזונם הניתן להם משולחן המלך כי בעוד שהם לפני המלך אע"פ שראוי אל הקליפה לא חלה עליהם שם טומאה עד רדתם למטה ממקום הצנור הנשפע להם בו. ואין זה מן התימא שהרי צואה [א] (באדם) פסק ההלכה הוא אם הוא יושב ואינה נראית שאינה מעכבת ק"ש ואע"פ שכבר חל עליה שם צואה והטעם שלא שלט עליה רוח הטומאה עד צאתה מגוף האדם מכל וכל. וכן הדין בשכבת זרע אעפ"י שנעקר ממקומו לצאת בעוד שלא תצא לא חל עליו שום טומאה ועדיין האדם טהור הוא ואינו מטמא עד צאתו מפי האמה ולחוץ שאז תחול עליו הכח הרע ההוא. ולזה הוצרך הצנור ההוא למעלה מתחלה מפני שקודם צאתו לאויר כבר ראוי אליהם ממקום המותר ומשם מתפרד לצד השמאלי דרך אותו הצנור עד צאתו לחוץ אשר שם חטאת רובץ ומקבלים אותו ואז טמא הוא וטמא אוכלו נבל הוא ונבלה עמו:

Chapter 25:2. Explanation of the path of the ascent of souls and the descent of angels without passing through the husks, so as not to be embodied in the impure air (avir tamei).

25:2:1 It remains an obligation upon us to explain a great doubt on the matter of the husks. Since all the rungs of existence, one above another down to the point of the earth, descend in a chain from cause to effect, and every effect is surrounded by its cause, if so, it is fitting to infer according to this that every good and every effluence that is bestowed in this world comes by way of the husk and the impurity, God forbid. And likewise the soul at the time of its rising, even if he was pious, would be compelled to be clothed first in impurity and to be defiled there before its rising above, God forbid. And this is certainly a thing outside the truth and outside the subject.

נשאר לנו לחובה לבאר ספק גדול בענין הקליפות מאחר שכל מדרגות המציאות זו למעלה מזו עד נקודת הארץ משתלשלים מעלה לעלול וכל עלול מוקף מעילתו א"כ ראוי לדקדק לפ"ז כל טוב וכל שפע שישפע בעה"ז הנה יבא דרך הקליפה והטומאה ח"ו. וכן הנשמה בעת עלייתה אע"פ שהיה חסיד יוכרח שיתלבש קודם בטומאה ויטמא שם קודם עלותו למעלה ח"ו. וזה בודאי דבר חוץ מן האמת ומן הנדון.

25:2:2 And further, the manna that descended to Israel: how shall we say that it passed by way of there? And likewise the Torah, and the ascent of Moses on high, and the ascent of Elijah. And there is an answer, to say that they went up in a whirlwind [se'arah]; for God forbid that this whirlwind be a whirling storm; rather, it is the barley [se'orah] through which they would offer the offering (of the Omer), which is Malkhut from the side of judgment, as there were horses of fire and a chariot of fire; but not that we say that they went up by way of the husks, God forbid; and this is strained. And likewise the clothing of the angels and their descent into this world is difficult, and several other matters. And on this matter we were confused in resolving the doubt, until YHVH enlightened the eyes of our intellect with a saying from the book of the Zohar in Parashat Vayakhel (fol. 209a), and these are his words: "And in each and every firmament there is an appointed one, and he is commanded over the world and over [the earth, to conduct all; except] the Land of Israel, which no firmament and no other power conducts, but the Holy One, blessed be He, alone; and behold, they have established it. And if you say: how does the firmament rest in vain over the Land of Israel? For behold, rain and dew descend upon it from the firmament, as upon every other land. Rather, in every firmament there are appointed ones ruling over the world; and that appointed one who rules over that firmament gives of the power that he has in that firmament, in order to give below; and that firmament takes from that appointed one and gives below to the earth. And that appointed one takes only from the residue of above. But over the Holy Land no other appointed one and no other power rules over that firmament that is over it, but the Holy One, blessed be He, alone; and He commands the Holy Land in that firmament. In each and every firmament there are known entrances; and the dominion of every appointed one is marked from entrance to entrance; and from that entrance onward he does not rule even as much as a hair; and this one does not enter into the boundary of the entrance of his fellow, except when permission is given to him to rule one over his fellow; then the kings of the earth rule one over his fellow. In the middle of all the firmaments there is one entrance, which is called Gevilon. And beneath this entrance there are seventy other entrances below, and seventy appointed ones guarding, at a distance of two thousand cubits, so that they do not draw near to it. And from that entrance a way rises above, above, until it reaches within the upper Throne; and from that entrance to every side of the firmament, up to the gate of the entrance that is called Megidon, where is the end of the firmament of the boundary of the Land of Israel. Happy," etc. Thus far his words.

Chapter 25:3. Explanation of the benefit of the husk for the concealed things; and there we explain the benefit of the evil inclination in the human being and its necessity, whence it derives.

25:3:1 Now we are obliged to explain the benefit of this husk and its matter in the world; for to say that it is to punish, etc., does not suffice. For we will ask: the whole substance of the punishment, is not its cause the evil inclination and the husk? Let there be no husk, and there will be no evil inclination. And we say that the reason for their creation by the will of the Creator is that the world and the creatures needed to receive their sustenance and their livelihood by the power of judgment and righteousness and uprightness. And if they were all angels, without choice in their deeds, the world would not be rewarded for their action, because they are compelled in their action like angels. And for this it was necessary that man be a master of choice, and that he be composed of the evil inclination and the good inclination; so that if he wishes by his nature to incline to the good, the evil inclination will be at his left to oppose him, to incline him to evil; and when he wishes to incline after evil, the good inclination will be at his right to chastise him and to remind him of the words of his Creator; in such a way that he receives reward for the inclining to one of the extremes, whether good or whether evil. And "to give every man according to his ways, and according to the fruit of his doings" (Jer. 17:10), it was necessary that there be the impure side, the servant who rebels against his master, to chastise the wicked according to his wickedness, by number, and to seek out his wickedness, and that he cleave to the fitting side, and there receive his punishment. And this matter is well explained in the Zohar, Parashat Terumah (fol. 163a), and these are his words: "All that the Holy One, blessed be He, made above and below, all of it is to show His glory, and all of it is for His service. And who has seen a servant who rebels, that he should be an accuser of his master, and in all that is the desire [re'uta] of his master he becomes an accuser? The desire of the Holy One, blessed be He, is that human beings be always in His service in this world and go in the way of truth, in order to give them merit with several good things. Since the desire of the Holy One, blessed be He, is in this, how does an evil servant come and is found an accuser against the desire of his master, and leads human beings astray to an evil way, distances them from the good way, and makes them not do the desire of their Master? Rather, certainly he does the desire of his Master. [It is like] a king who had an only son, and he loved him greatly, and commanded him in love not to draw himself near to an evil woman; because whoever draws near to her is not worthy to enter within the palace of the king. That son agreed to do the desire of his father in love. In the king's house, outside, there was a harlot, fair of appearance and beautiful of form. After some days the king said: I wish to see the desire of my son toward me. He called that harlot and said to her: Go and seduce my son, to see the desire of my son toward me. That harlot, what did she do? She went after the king's son and began to embrace him, to kiss him, and to seduce him with several seductions. If that son is fitting and obeys and listens to the command of his father, he rebukes her and does not obey her and pushes her away from him. Then his father rejoices in his son and brings him within the curtain of his palace and gives him gifts and presents and great honour. Who caused all this honour to that son? Say, that harlot. And does that harlot have praise in this, or not? Certainly she has praise from every side: one, that she did the command of the king; and one, that she caused to that son all this honour, all this goodness, all this love of the king toward him. And therefore it is written (Gen. 1:31), 'and behold, it was good [tov]': this is the angel of life; 'very' [me'od]: this is the angel of death; who is certainly very good for whoever obeys the command of his master. And come and see: if this accuser were not, the righteous would not inherit those upper treasures that they are destined to inherit in the world to come. Happy are those who have met him, this accuser, and happy are those who have not met him. Happy are those who have met him and been saved from him; for because of him they inherit all those good things and all that bliss and all those longings of the world to come; of which it is written (Isa. 64:3), 'Eye has not seen, O God, beside You, what He will do for him that waits for Him.' And happy are those who have not met him; for because of him they would inherit Gehinnom and be driven from the land of the living: those who do not obey their Master and are drawn after him. And therefore the righteous should hold him a good [turn]; for because of him they inherit all those good things and bliss and longings in the world to come. The benefit of this accuser, when the wicked obey him, what is it? Rather, even though he has no benefit, he does the command of his Master. And further, he is strengthened by this: since he is evil, he is strengthened when he does evil. The wicked man is not strengthened until he kills human beings; when he kills human beings, then he is strengthened and prevails in his power and has rest. So that accuser, who is called the angel of death, does not prevail in his power until he leads human beings astray and accuses them and kills them; then he has rest and is strengthened and prevails in his power, when the wicked obey him and he rules over them. May the Compassionate One save us. And happy are those who merit to overcome him and to subdue him, to merit because of him the world to come; and a man is strengthened in the Holy King always. Of this it is said (Ps. 84:6), 'Happy is the man whose strength is in You; in whose heart are the highways.' Happy are they in this world and in the world to come." Thus far his words.

25:3:2 And his words are clear and refined as silver is refined; and it is a clear reason that the husk is a need on high, fitting for the world, fitting for the righteous. 25:3:3 And behold, the evil inclination is from the side of Gevurah, and the good inclination from the side of Chesed; even though the evil inclination is in the secret of the husk and the good inclination in the secret of holiness. And when the upright man's inclination prevails over him and seeks to incline him from the straight way to a way that is not good, it points to the husk prevailing to act by the power of Gevurah that flows upon it, from which is its drawing; and then judgment prevails over compassion. And when the upright man, by the power of his intellect, makes the good inclination prevail over the evil inclination, then it points to subduing the power of the husk; and the judgment is sweetened, and the power of compassion prevails, and the attribute of judgment is turned to the attribute of compassion. And all this is in the secret of the branches that are in the hand of the upright man, to arbitrate them and to incline them to one of the sides. And when, God forbid, the wicked man makes the evil inclination prevail over the good inclination, it points to the power of Gevurah ruling over the power of compassion; and judgment is present, and the husk prevails. And this is what they, of blessed memory, said: "Cursed are the wicked, who turn the attribute of compassion to the attribute of judgment." For through the lower actions, in the secret of the branches, the roots act. And this is the reason that the Holy One, blessed be He, created the evil inclination: so that it can be subdued beneath the hand of the good inclination; and then the attribute of judgment is turned to the attribute of compassion, and the upper roots draw from the radiance of the upper light and are united in a complete union. And this is a complete reason on the matter of the evil inclination. And there is a further, finer reason, supported by the words of Rashbi in the Zohar. And it is that the matter of the husk: its cause was because of the spreading out, as we explained in the first chapter. (And behold, the spreading out, its cause is the first Adam), as we explained in the Gate of the Reason for the Emanation, chapter 6, in general.

והנה יצה"ר מצד הגבורה ויצה"ט מצד החסד. עם היות שיצה"ר הוא בסוד הקליפה ויצה"ט בסוד הקדושה. וכאשר האיש הישר יגבר עליו יצרו ומבקש להטותו מדרך הישר אל דרך לא טוב יורה על הקליפה המתגברת לפעול בכח הגבורה השופעת עליה שממנה יניקתה ואז הדין גובר על הרחמים. וכאשר הישר בכח שכלו יגביר יצה"ט על יצה"ר אז יורה להכניע כח הקליפה והדין נמתק וכח הרחמים גובר ומה"ד מתהפכת למה"ר וכל זה בסוד הענפים שביד האדם הישר להכריעם ולהטותם אל א' הצדדים. וכאשר ח"ו הרשע יגביר יצה"ר על יצ"ט יורה כח הגבורה שולט על כח הרחמים והדין הווה וקליפה מתגברת. וזהו אמרם ז"ל ארורים הם הרשעים שמהפכין מדת רחמים למה"ד. כי בפעולות התחתונות בסוד הענפים יפעלו השרשים. וזהו הטעם שברא הקב"ה היצה"ר כדי שיכול להיות מוכנע תחת כף היצ"ט ואז מתהפכת מ"ד למה"ר והשרשים העליונים יונקים מזוהר אור העליון ומתיחדים יחוד גמור. וזה טעם גמור על ענין יצה"ר. ויש עוד טעם אחר יותר נאה מוסמך על דברי הרשב"י בזוהר והוא כי ענין הקליפה היתה סבתה מפני התפשטות כדפי' בפ"ק [ו]. (והנה ההתפשטות, סבתו האדם הא') כדפי' בשער טעם האצילות פ"ו בכלל.

25:3:4 And behold, he is found to be the one who perverted, and he was the cause of the corruption; therefore he needed to be the one who sets right what he corrupted. And by what? He subdues the power of the husk when he makes the good inclination prevail over the evil inclination; and the husk is subdued and broken; and then he sets right what was corrupted because of him. And apart from this, he purifies the impure: for when he subdues it and brings it into the fulfilment of the commandment, he returns it to holiness, and it is purified through him; and then he brings it into holiness as it was before; and the husk is included in holiness, and all is pure, as we explained in the first chapter. And certainly the evil inclination is purified and becomes the good inclination. And so Rashbi explained in the Ra'aya Meheimna (Parashat Bo, fol. 43a), and these are his words: "This commandment: to redeem the firstling of an ass, and to break the neck of the firstling of an ass if one does not redeem it; as it is written, 'And every firstling of an ass you shall redeem with a lamb; and if you will not redeem it, then you shall break its neck' (Exod. 13:13). And this secret: the evil inclination can return in repentance, and it becomes the good inclination; as they established, 'If he merits, a help; if he does not merit, against him' (cf. Gen. 2:18). Because they are one likeness, one of a lamb and one of an ass; and if he merits to return in repentance, even though he is an ass, an ignorant man, 'you shall redeem' from exile 'with a lamb,'" etc. Thus far his words, as relevant to our matter. And from it it is explained that the evil inclination is purified and becomes the good inclination; and this is "you shall redeem with a lamb," etc. And the matter is in the way that we explained: that he performs the action of commandments, and behold, the evil inclination becomes the good inclination, and the like. And likewise it is expounded elsewhere in the Zohar, that he said: "The evil inclination above, who is it? Gevurah." He taught that the evil inclination can be from the side of Gevurah, from the side of the wine that gladdens, not from the side of the wine that intoxicates. And likewise now the husk and the intoxicating wine are set right in the secret of the tefillin, which are the Torah: he sets it right and sweetens it, and it becomes the wine that gladdens; for he returns it to its source, and it is purified. And by this is understood the matter expounded in the Zohar, Parashat Noach (fol. 61a), these are his words: "Come and see: if Adam had brought out offspring from the Garden of Eden, they would not have been destroyed for generations of generations, and the light of the moon would never have been darkened; for all of them would have endured forever. And even the upper angels would not stand before them in light and radiance and wisdom; as it is said, 'in the image of God He created him' (Gen. 1:27). But since he caused sin, and went out from the Garden, and made offspring outside, they did not endure in the world and were not as is fitting. Rabbi Hezekiah said: And how could they make offspring there? For if he had not drawn upon himself the evil inclination and sinned, he would have endured in the world alone, and would not make offspring. Likewise, if Israel had not sinned with the calf and drawn upon themselves the evil inclination, they would never have made offspring, and later generations would never have come. He said to him: If Adam had not sinned, he would not have made offspring in this fashion [from the side of the evil inclination; but he would have made offspring from the side of the holy spirit]; for now he makes offspring only from the side of the evil inclination. And because all the offspring of human beings are all from the side of the evil inclination, therefore they have no endurance; for it is impossible for them to endure, because the Other Side is mixed in them. But if Adam had not sinned and had not been driven from the Garden of Eden, he would have made offspring from the side of the holy spirit, holy like the upper angels, enduring for generations of generations, after the pattern of above. Since he sinned and begot children outside the Garden, and did not merit to bring them out from the Garden, they did not endure, even to take root in this world, until Noah came," etc. Thus far his words.

ולא אתחשך נהורא דסיהרא לעלמין דכלהו הוו קיימין לעלמין. ואפי' מלאכין עילאין לא קיימאן קמייהו בנהורא וזיווא וחכמתא כד"א בצלם אלהים ברא אותו. אבל כיון דגרים חטאה ונפיק איהו מגנתא ועבד תולדות לבר לא אתקיימו בעלמא ולא הוו כדחזי. א"ר חזקיה וכי האיך יכלין למעבד תולדות תמן דהא אלמלא דאמשיך עליה יצה"ר וחטא אתקיים איהו בעלמא בלחודוי ולא יעביד תולדות. כגוונא דא אלמלא דחבו ישראל בעגלא ואמשיכו עלייהו יצה"ר לא עבידו תולדות לעלמין ולא ייתון דרין בתראין לעלמין. א"ל אלמלא דחב אדם לא עביד תולדות כגוונא דא [מסטרא דיצה"ר אבל עביד תולדות מסטרא דרוח קדישא] דהשתא לא עביד תולדות אלא מסטרא דיצה"ר. ובגין דכל תולדות דבני נשא כלהו מסטרא דיצה"ר בגין כך לית לון קיום דאי אפשר לון לאתקיימא דסטרא אחרא אתערב בהון. אבל אי לא חב אדם ולא אתתרך מגנתא דעדן עביד תולדות מסטר דרוח קדישא דקדישין כמלאכי עלאין קיימין לדרי דרין כגוונא דלעילא. כיון דחב ואוליד בנין לבר מגנתא ולא זכה לאפקא לון מגנתא לא אתקיימו אפי' לאשתרשא בעלמא דא עד דאתא נח וכו' עכ"ל.

25:3:5 And behold, explicitly: while he was in the Garden of Eden there was in him power to beget from the side of the holy spirit; for this was the beginning of the intent. And he drew upon himself the evil inclination, in that he made the power of the husk prevail over him, and its power prevailed over him. And through this man is compelled to set right and to sweeten the evil inclination, and to return it in its root to the wine that gladdens, as we explained:

והנה בפי' כי עם היותו בג"ע היה בו כח להוליד מסטר דרוח קדישא כי זה היה תחלת הכוונה. והוא המשיך עליו יצה"ר במה שהגביר עליו כח הקליפה וגבר עליו כחו ועי"ז יוכרח האדם לתקן ולהמתיק היצ"ר ולהחזירו בשרשו ליין המשמח כדפי':

Chapter 25:4. Explanation of the ten husks, their names, and their functions, one by one, to establish their reckoning.

25:4:1 What is intended now is to explain, on the matter of the husks, that they have ten Sefirot and seven palaces, like the matter of holiness; for they are over against holiness "as an ape before a man." And just as the side of holiness draws effluence and sustenance for all its pure creatures, so the husk draws and bestows sustenance and effluence for many creatures that are on its side. And this is the reason for the forbidden and the permitted, the impure and the pure, the unfit and the fit. For just as the side of whiteness and purity comes from the side of Chesed, which is the likeness of the elder, "You shall rise up before the hoary head" (Lev. 19:32), so there is whiteness in impurity and husk; and it is "the guilty elder" [zaken ashmai], who is desolate, and the root of desolation and "destruction decreed" (Isa. 10:22). And just as there is permitted redness and blood of purity on the side of Gevurah, which is the side that returns to the pure arbitration, and this is the one who conquers his inclination, as we explained above, so there is impure and forbidden redness and blood. And just as there is green on the side of compassion, the middle line, from the side of Tiferet, where fitness and goodness are, so there is the green gall, which kills by a drop from which "his face turns green" [cf. Avodah Zarah 20b], and the unfitness and the evil; for these are over against those. And just as these three, which are Chesed, Judgment, Compassion, are included in ten Sefirot, as we explained at the end of the Gate of Essence and Governance, so these three have ten Sefirot in the accusing husks, as we shall explain. However, on these names the explanations are many, and we shall abbreviate the much into the little that suffices:

הנרצה עתה לבאר על ענין הקליפות שלהם ע"ס וז' היכלות כענין הקדושה כי הם נגד הקדושה כקוף בפני אדם וכמו שצד הקדושה מושך שפע ומזון לכל נבראיו הטהורים, כן הקליפה היא מושכת ומשפעת מזון ושפע לנבראים רבים אשר בצדה. וזה טעם האסור והמותר הטמא והטהור הפסול והכשר. כי כמו שיבא צד הלובן והטהרה מצד החסד שהוא דמות הסבא מפני שיבה תקום, כן יש לובן בטומאה וקליפה והוא זקן אשמאי שהוא שומם ושרש השממה והכליון חרוץ. וכמו שיש אודם המותר ודם טוהר בצד הגבורה שהיא צד החוזר אל ההכרעה הטהורה וזהו הכובש את יצרו כדפי' לעיל, כן יש אודם ודם טמא ואסור. וכמו שיש ירוק בצד הרחמים הקו האמצעי מצד הת"ת אשר שם הכשרות והטוב, כן יש המרה הירוקה הממיתה טפה שממנה פניו ש"א מוריקות והפיסול והרע כי הם אלו נגד אלו. וכמו שאלו הג' הם חסד דין רחמים נכללים בי' ספירות כדפי' בשלהי שער מהות והנהגה, כן אלו הג' יש להם י' ספירות בקליפות המשטינים כאשר נבאר. אולם שמות אלו רבו בהם הפירוש' ואנו נקצר הרב במעט המספיק:

25:4:2 The first is called Te'omiel. The explanation: it is from the language of te'omim [twins], in the attribute that rebelled against the Lord, blessed be He, and makes itself as a twin to Him; like "for a servant when he reigns … and a handmaid that is heir to her mistress" (Prov. 30:22–23). And there is no wonder at the name El being found in the likeness of the husks; for just as there is the name El on the side of holiness, so there is another el; and this is what the verse says (Num. 24:4), "He says, who hears the words of El"; and he explained in the Zohar: "this is another el." And there are those who received the name of this husk as Tumiel, without Alef; and the explanation is that their whole intent is to make an end and to finish [le-hatem] the holiness, God forbid, and to diminish it from watching over. And these two names Rashbi related, in the Tikkunim (Tikkun 69, fol. 105a; see there), to the husks that are over against Netzach and Hod. And to the first husk he related there the name Katriel, to show that it is a crown [keter] to the other husks; and from this husk is the karet [cutting off]; for it is sometimes called Kartiel, and through it the wicked are cut off. And there are those who received the name of this husk as Kamtiel; and possibly as we explained in Kartiel; and its matter is kamat, from the language of "who were cut down [kummetu] before their time" (Job 22:16). And now this matter is precise: "who were cut down before their time," that is, the nine hundred and seventy-four generations that were cut down and cut off before their time, as our Rabbis, of blessed memory, explained (in Chagigah 13b):

הא' נקרא תאומיא"ל. פי' כי היא מלשון תאומים במדה אשר מרדה באדון ב"ה ועושה עצמה כתאומים אליו כדמיון תחת עבד כי ימלוך ושפחה כי תירש גבירתה (משלי ל). ואין תימה מה שנמצא שם אל בדמות הקליפות כי כמו שיש שם אל בצד הקדושה כן יש א"ל אחר וזש"ה (במדבר כד) נאם שומע אמרי אל ופירש בזהר דא אל אחר. ויש שקבלו בשם הקליפה הזאת תומיא"ל חסר אלף ופי' כי כל כוונתם לכלות ולהתם הקדושה ח"ו ולהמעיטה מלהשגיח. ושתי שמות אלו ייחסם הרשב"י בתיקונים (תקונא ס"ט דף ק"ה. ע"ש) אל הקליפות שהם נגד נצח והוד. ולקליפה הראשונה ייחס שם בשם כתריאל להורות שהיא כתר לשאר הקליפות ומן הקליפה הזאת הכרת שהיא נק' לפעמים כרתי"אל וע"י נכרתים הרשעים. ויש שקבלו בשם הקליפה הזאת קמטיאל. ואפשר כדפירשנו בכרתי"אל, ועניינו קמט, מלשון אשר קומטו ולא עת (איוב כב טז). וידוקדק עתה העניין הזה אשר קומטו בלא עת דהיינו תתקע"ד דורות שקומטו ונכרתו קודם עתם כדפירשו רז"ל (בחגיגה דף י"ג):

25:4:3 The second is called Ogiel. And he explained the reason: that it is a hindrance [ugah] to its fellow; so Nachmanides, of blessed memory, explained. And it is possible to explain it from the language of "would you therefore shut yourselves off [te'agenah]?" (Ruth 1:13); and the intent is that it holds back the effluence and prevents the providence, like a screen that divides between Israel and their Father in heaven. And there are those who read Go'iel, and explain it from the language of "or does the ox low [yig'eh] over his fodder [belilo]?" (Job 6:5); and the intent is that it lows to confuse [levalel] the Emanation and to tear down the building. And possibly from the language of gevi'ah [expiring]; for the aim of its face is to make expire and to destroy all the holy things. And there are those who read without Vav, Ga'iel, or Aga'el; and possibly one can explain it in the way that I explained it with Vav. And in the Tikkunim (there) Rashbi called this Sefirah "the man of Belial": for the holy Chokhmah is called Adam, as is explained in the Gate of the Glossary of Titles; and this, which is over against it, is called the man [adam] of Belial. And these names, Ogiel, etc., he related to over against Tiferet. And there are those who called this husk Beli'el; and the intent is clear: for it denies the existence of God; and this is beli El [without God]:

השנית נק' עוגיא"ל. ופי' הטעם כי היא עוגה לחברתה כן פירש הרמב"ן ז"ל. ואפשר לפרש מלשון הלהן תעגנה (רות א). והכונה מעכב השפע ומונע ההשגחה כמו מסך המבדיל בין ישראל לאביהם שבשמים. ואית דגרסי גועיא"ל ופירש מלשון אם יגעה שור על בלילו (איוב ו') והכונה כי הוא גועה לבלל האצילות ולהרוס הבנין. ואפשר מלשון גויעה כי מגמת פניו להגוויע ולכלות כל הדברים הקדושים. ואית דגרסי בלא ו' געיא"ל או עגא"ל ואפשר לפרשו ע"ד שפירשתי בו'. ובתיקונים (שם) קרא הרשב"י לספירה זו אדם בליעל. כי החכמה קדושה נקראת אדם כמבואר בשער ערכי הכנויים, וזאת שהיא כנגדה נקראת אדם בליעל. והשמות האלה עוגיא"ל כו' ייחס לכנגד התפארת. ויש שקראו לקליפה זו בלי"אל והכונה מבוארת כי הוא מכחיש מציאות האלוה וזהו בלי אל:

25:4:4 The third is called Sitriel; for it hides [mastir] the face of compassion, in the matter of "You have covered Yourself with a cloud," etc. (Lam. 3:44). And others call it Harsiel; and the explanation is the language of harisah [tearing down], that it tears down. And the intent is that it is the reverse of Binah: for Binah causes the Godhead to dwell and reveals it, and likewise she builds the building of holiness; and this one conceals the Godhead and tears down the building. And in this way we can explain Sitriel from the language of setirah [demolition]. And in the Tikkunim (there) Rashbi called it Se'iriel; and from there is Esau, "a hairy [sa'ir] man" (Gen. 27:11); and from there "the hair of a woman is nakedness." And there are those who called it Itiel; and possibly one can explain it from the language of "a man in readiness [itti]" (Lev. 16:21); and the intent is that it is ready to make itself a god, like the matter of the calf that came into being of itself. And the commentators wrote that these three husks were annihilated in a moment; and these are the matter of "He built worlds and destroyed them." And according to their opinion these three kinds of impurity are not in existence, and there are only seven husks. And this thing is not right with the explanation of the Zohar; for explicitly Rashbi explains (there in the Tikkunim) that they are ten kinds of husks; and he calls them ten lower crowns, and over against them ten he-asses and ten she-asses. And possibly their intent in the matter of the annihilation is that its providence is absent from the world; and this too is not right; for behold, Se'iriel is the third husk, and behold, from it is Esau, "a hairy man," and likewise "the hair of a woman is nakedness":

השלישית נקראת סתריאל. כי היא מסתרת פני הרחמים כענין סכות בענן לך וגו' (איכה ג). ואחרים קוראים אותה הרסי"אל ופי' לשון הריסה שהוא הורס והכונה כי היא הפך הבינה כי הבינה משכנת ומגלה האלהות וכן היא בונה בנין הקדושה. וזאת מעלמת האלהות והורסת הבנין. וע"ד זה נוכל לפרש סתריאל מלשון סתירה. ובתיקונים (שם) קראה הרשב"י שערי"אל ומשם עשו איש שעיר ומשם שער באשה ערוה. ויש שקראו אותה עיתיאל ואפשר לפרש מלשון איש עתי (ויקרא טז) והכונה מזומן לעשות עצמו אלוה כענין העגל שנתהוה מעצמו. וכתבו המפרשים כי ג' קליפות אלו היו אפיסתן כרגע ואלו הם העניין בונה עולמות ומחריבן. ולפי דעתם אין ג' מיני טומאה אלו במציאות ואין כ"א ז' קליפות ואין הדבר הזה צודק עם פי' הזהר. כי בפי' מבאר הרשב"י (שם בתקונים) שהם י' מיני קליפות והוא קורא אותה י' כתרין תתאין, וכנגדן י' חמורים וי' אתונות. ואפשר לכוונתם בעינן האפיסה שנעדרת השגחתו מן העולם וגם זה לא יצדק שהרי שעריא"ל היא קליפה שלישית והרי ממנה עשו איש שעיר וכן שער באשה ערוה:

25:4:5 The fourth is called Ga'ash Kalah. And Nachmanides explained that it is appointed over bands of hosts, bands of the jealous; and in its jealousy, in which it is jealous of the purity, it storms [go'esh] and quakes and destroys [mekhaleh] several souls; and then even "the angels of peace weep bitterly" (Isa. 33:7). Thus far. And in the Tikkunim (there) Rashbi, peace be upon him, explained that this husk is called Uziel; and from there are Uza and Azael, who are two powers of impurity. And likewise he called it Chazriel: "The boar [chazir] out of the wood ravages it" (Ps. 80:14). And others said that it is called Samael; and others related Samael to another place, as we shall explain:

הרביעית נקרא געש כלה. ופי' הרמב"ן שהוא ממונה על כתות חיילים כתות המקנאים ובקנאתו אשר הוא מקנא בהטהרה הוא גועש ורועש ומכלה כמה נשמות ואז אפי' מלאכי השלום מר יבכיון ע"כ. ובתקונים (שם) פי' הרשב"י ע"ה כי הקליפה הזאת נק' עוזיאל ומשם עזא ועזאל שהם שני כחות הטומאה וכן קראה חזריא"ל יכרסמנה חזיר מיער (תהלים פ). ואחרים אמרו כי נקרא סמא"ל ואחרים יחסו סמא"ל במקום אחר כאשר נבאר:

25:4:6 The fifth is called Golahav. And the explanation is as "they are driven forth from among [gev] men" (Job 30:5); and the intent is that it is a body [guf] of flame [lahav]; for from this is the beginning of the bodies that are covered over from human beings, etc.; and its throne is surrounded by flames of fire; and if not for the delight of the Name, may He be blessed, in His creatures, the bands of its hosts would destroy all the lower creatures in a moment. And others explained that gu is from the language of "within," the Aramaic language; and its matter is that it is within the flame, etc. And others explained that it is called Za'afiel; and the intent is from the language of za'af [rage]; for it is the prince of rage; and YHVH save us. And in the Tikkunim (there) he called it Agagiel, and said that from there is Agag king of Amalek:

החמישית נק' גו"להב ופי' כמו מן ג"ו יגורשו (איוב ל') והכוונה כי היא גוף של להב כי מזה תחלת הגופים המתכסים מבני אדם וכו' וכסאו מוקף בלהבי אש ואלמלא חפץ השם יתברך בבריותיו כתות חייליו היו מכלים כל הנבראים התחתונים כרגע. ואחרים פי' כי גו מלשון תוך לשון ארמי וענינו שהוא תוך הלהב וכו'. ואחרים פי' שנק' זעפיא"ל והכוונה מלשון זעף שהוא שר הזעף וה' יצילנו. ובתקונים (שם) קראה אגגיא"ל ואמר כי משם אגג מלך עמלק:

25:4:7 The sixth is called Tagrinon. And the intent is that it is ready to put strife [tigra] between the kings with a loud voice of crying, which confounds. And others wrote that it is called Za'amiel; and possibly it is the prince of indignation [za'am]. And in the Tikkunim (there) he called it Ogiel, over against the central pillar; and from there is Og king of Bashan. And the word Ogiel was explained above:

הששית נק' תגרי"נון. והכוונה כי הוא מעותד לשים תגר בין המלכים בקול צעקה גדולה שהוא מבלבל. ואחרים כתבו שהוא נק' זעמיאל ואפשר שהוא שר הזעם. ובתקונים (שם) קראו עוגיאל לקבל עמודא דאמצעיתא ומתמן עוג מלך הבשן ומלת עוגיאל פי' למעלה:

25:4:8 The seventh is called Arvazrak. And the intent is that it is like a cruel raven [orev] and intends to throw away [lizrok] its young. Further explanation: at the time of its receiving from holiness it is sweet [arev] to it, and after it receives from the husk it hates that effluence and throws it away. And it is possible for us to explain that at first it is sweet to a man in its speech, and "smoother than oil is its palate" (Prov. 5:3); and afterward it throws to him a deadly poison, and he finds "the gall of asps" in its honey (cf. Job 20:14). And others wrote that it is called Katzpiel, the prince of the outburst [ketzef]:

השביעית נק' ערבזר"ק. והכונה כי הוא כעורב אכזרי ומתכוין לזרוק ילדיו. עוד פי' כי בשעת קבלתו מן הקדושה ערב לו ואחר שהוא מקבל מהקליפה שונא אותו השפע וזורק אותו. ולנו אפשר לפרש כי בתחלה ערב לאדם בדברו וחלק משמן חכו ואח"כ זורק לו סם המות וימצא מרורות פתנים בדבשו. ואחרים כתבו שנקרא קצפי"אל שר הקצף:

25:4:9 The eighth is Samael the wicked. And there is no need for length on its matter; for it is well known that he is the accuser of Israel. And in the Tikkunim there he explained that husks seven and eight are Te'omiel and Tumiel; and he said that from there is "the depths [tehomot] cover them" (Exod. 15:5). And others wrote that it is called Rogziel; and possibly it is the prince of agitation [rogez]:

השמינית סמא"ל הרשע. והאריכות בעניינו אין צורך כי מפורסם כי הוא קטיגור של ישראל. ובתקונים שם פי' כי קליפ' ז' וח' הם תאומיאל ותומיאל ואמר כי משם תהומות יכסיומו. ואחרים כתבו שנקראת רגזיאל ואפשר שהוא שר הרוגז:

25:4:10 The ninth is Gamliel. And the Geonim say concerning this husk: "There is an accuser in the multitude on high, Gamliel by name. And when he draws nourishment from the (perhaps one should read: from the gazers of the) heavens, all his hosts are hosts of peace; and when they are seen by human beings, they have permission to be seen by them in the form of a man, and they tell them future things. And all this the Holy One, blessed be He, does, so that they not plead accusation against Israel in their prayer." And others wrote that this husk is called Meshulchael; and the reason is that it sends [meshallachat] evil angels; may the Compassionate One save us. And we have found written: "Samael the wicked and Lilith the foolish are like one coupling, by means of a groomsman who receives the emanation of wickedness and insolence from this one and bestows it to that one. And of this secret it is said (Isa. 27:1), 'In that day YHVH with His sore sword,' etc., 'shall punish leviathan the piercing serpent, and leviathan the crooked serpent; and He shall slay the dragon [tannin] that is in the sea.' Leviathan [livyatan]: the explanation, the joining and coupling [levayah] of the two of them, who are like serpents; therefore he doubled: 'the piercing serpent,' over against Samael; 'the crooked serpent,' over against Lilith. 'And He shall slay the dragon that is in the sea': just as there is a pure leviathan in the sea, according to its plain sense, and it is called tannin, so there is a great impure tannin in the sea, according to its plain sense; and likewise above, by way of the concealed. And the tannin above is a blind prince, who is like a middle groomsman between Samael and Lilith; and his name is Taniniver; and it is as tannin iver [blind dragon]. And the masters of the Kabbalah said that that tannin that is in the sea is without eyes; and likewise the tannin above is like a spiritual form without colors, which are the eyes; therefore his name is Taniniver. And he is the one who joins the accompaniment and the coupling between Samael and Lilith. If he had been created whole, in the completeness of his emanation, he would destroy the world in one moment. And when the will comes, and the emanation that comes from the side of Samael and Lilith by means of the blind prince is diminished and lacking, in complete destruction, then the saying of the prophet will be fulfilled: 'In that day YHVH will punish,' etc." Thus far his words.

Chapter 25:6. That the husks do not enter into Atzilut; if so, what is the matter of their blemish that is referred to in connection with the sefirot.

25:6:1 What is intended in this chapter is to save those lacking knowledge from error: let not the thinker think that the husks enter within the pure and holy Emanation, God forbid, far be it, far be it; for whoever thinks this forfeits his head to his Owner. And what is found in some places that would show that the husks enter beneath the holiness is neither in the world of Atzilut nor in the world of Beriah, but it is in the world of Yetzirah; it is the world of Metatron; for "the wicked walk on every side" of it (Ps. 12:9). And so Rashbi, peace be upon him, explained in the book of the Ra'aya Meheimna (Pinchas, fol. 227), and these are his words: "Above, in the Tree of Life, there are no husks; 'for none might enter into the king's gate clothed with sackcloth' (Esth. 4:2). Below there are husks, in Metatron, who is in the likeness of the central pillar. For at the time that the Holy One, blessed be He, is apart from His Kingdom, He is covered with the wings and faces of His servant Metatron; as it is written (2 Sam. 22:11), 'And He rode upon a cherub, and did fly.' And those husks that surround the four living creatures of Metatron are: tohu [formlessness]: 'and behold, a great and strong wind rent the mountains and broke in pieces the rocks … but YHVH was not in the wind' (1 Kings 19:11). Bohu [void]: 'and after the wind an earthquake; but YHVH was not in the earthquake.' Behold two husks, green and white. For the husks of the nut: one, tohu, the green line; the second, bohu, slimy stones, a hard husk like slimy stone. Corresponding to these two husks, the chaff and the straw of the wheat. The third husk is thin, corresponding to the bran of the wheat; for so it cleaves to the wheat and cannot be separated from there until they grind it with millstones, which correspond to the molars of the mouth of a man, who must grind with them the words of Torah until they are like flour of clean fine flour; and in the sieve, which is the lip, the refuse, which is bran, is sorted out, until the law is found clean fine flour. At that time the heart and the brain and all the limbs of the body, in which the soul spreads out, take it; and the soul is sustained by it, just as the body is sustained by the things of the world; for 'God has made the one over against the other': the bread of the body and the bread of the Torah; as it is written (Prov. 9:5), 'Come, eat of my bread.' And this husk is like the husk that cleaves to the kernel of the nut. And at the time that the nut is soft, that husk separates from the kernel of the nut without difficulty; and at the time that the nut is dry, it is hard for a man to remove it from there; for still 'the difficulty stands in its place.' And therefore the Holy One, blessed be He, commanded man to return in repentance in his youth, before the evil inclination grows old in him; as it is written (Lev. 19:32), 'You shall rise up before the hoary head': before your hoary head. And this husk is fire, and it is aroused in it: 'and after the earthquake a fire; but YHVH was not in the fire.' The fourth, the abyss [tehom], the hollow of the nut; in it is 'a still small voice'; there the King comes; 'and out of the midst thereof as the colour of chashmal, out of the midst of the fire' (Ezek. 1:4)," etc. Thus far his words.

הנרצה בפרק זה להציל חסרי הדעת מן הטעות אל יחשוב החושב שהקליפות נכנסות אל תוך האצילות הטהור והקדוש חס ושלום חלילה וחלילה כי החושב זאת חייב ראשו לקונו. ומה שנמצא בקצת מקומות שיורה על שהקליפ' נכנסות תחת הקדושה אינו לא בעולם האצילות ולא בעולם הבריאה אבל הוא בעולם היצירה הוא עולם המטטרון כי סביב לו רשעים יתהלכון. וכן ביאר הרשב"י ע"ה בס' ר"מ (פנחס דרכ"ז) וז"ל לעילא באילנא דחיי לית קליפין כי אין לבא אל שער המלך בלבוש שק לתתא אית קליפה במטטרון דאיהו בדיוקנא דעמודא דאמצעיתא. דבזמנא דקב"ה בר ממלכותיה אתכסי בגדפין ואנפין דעבד דיליה מטטרון הה"ד (ש"ב כב) וירכב על כרוב ויעוף. ואינון קליפון דסחרין לד' חיוון דמטטרון אינון תוהו, (מ"א יט) והנה רוח גדולה וחזק מפרק הרים ומשבר סלעים לא ברוח ה'. בה"ו, ואחר הרוח רעש לא ברעש ה'. הא תרין קליפין ירוק וחיוור. דקליפין דאגוזא חד תה"ו קו ירוק, תניינא בהו אבנין מפולמין קליפא תקיפא כאבנא מפולמא. לקביל תרין קליפין אלין מוץ ותבן דחטה. קליפה תליתאה דקיקא לקבל סובין דחטה דהכי איהו מתדבק בחטה ולא יכול לאתפרשא מתמן עד דטחנין ליה ברחיים דאינון לקבל טוחנות דפומא דבר נש דצריך למטחן בהון מילי דאורייתא עד דיהון כקמח סלת נקייה ובנפה דאיהי שפה אתבריר פסולת דאיהו סובין עד דישתכח הלכה סלת נקייה. בההוא זמנא נטיל לה לבא ומוחא וכל אברין דגופא דאתפשט בהון נשמתא ואתפרנסת בה נשמתא כגוונא דגופא אתפרנסת במלין דעלמא דזה לעומת זה עשה האלדים נהמא דגופא ונהמא דאורייתא הה"ד (משלי ט) לכו לחמו בלחמי. והאי קליפה איהי כקליפא דמתדבקא במוחא דאגוזא ובזמנא דאגוזא איהי רכיכא אתפרשא ההיא קליפה ממוחא דאגוזא בלא קושיא. ובזמנא דאיהי אגוזא יבישה קשה לבר נש לאעברא לה מתמן כי עדיין הקושייא במקומה עומדת. ובג"ד מני קב"ה לבר נש לאחזרא בתיובתא בבחרותיה קודם דיצר הרע יזקין ביה הה"ד (ויקרא יט) מפני שיבה תקום, קודם שיבה דילך. והאי קליפה איהי אש ואתער ביה ואחר הרעש אש לא באש ה'. רביעאה תהום, חלל דאגוזא ביה קול דממה דקה תמן קא אתא מלכא, ומתוכה כעין החשמל מתוך האש (יחזקאל א) כו' עכ"ל.

25:6:2 And we shall not withhold our hands from explaining this saying, because of its great benefit. He said "above, in the Tree of Life," because there is the Tree of Life and the Tree of Knowledge of good and evil. And the Tree of Life is Tiferet in particular, and in general all the Emanation; for there is life, and there is no death except in the place of the husks, which are round about the world of Metatron, which is the Tree of Knowledge of good and evil; as Rashbi, peace be upon him, explained in the Tikkunim (Tikkun 51, fol. 85), these are his words: "And the Tree of Knowledge of good and evil is below: the Tree of Knowledge, good, this is Metatron; and evil, this is Samael." Thus far his words, in brief, because of the great length. And from here is a demolition of the one who says that the Tree of Knowledge of good and evil is in Malkhut; for it is an error in their hand. And what is found in Rashbi, peace be upon him, that sometimes he calls Malkhut the Tree of Knowledge of good and evil, is by way of borrowing; for all that is below Malkhut is related to her; and its principal is in Metatron; and it was borrowed for Malkhut because "her feet," which are Metatron, "go down to death" (Prov. 5:5), which are the husks. And he related the husks to the name Samael, because he is their messenger for every quarrel and for every plague, for every iniquity and for every sin, as is well known in the words of Rashbi, peace be upon him, in several places, and in several midrashim and aggadot in the Talmud. And let us return to the saying, which said that in the Tree of Life there are no husks; for it is a kernel without a husk. And he proved this from the verse "for none might enter into the king's gate clothed with sackcloth [sak]," as we wrote in the entry sak; and sak is the husk. And for this reason those who return wear sackcloth, to show that they take upon themselves the pain and the affliction of the sackcloth in this world, because they hated the knowledge of the holy ones and made the sackcloth king over them. And it is possible to say concerning it that sak is an acronym of sone kedoshim [hater of the holy ones]; and in permutation it is kash [stubble]; and they are the stubble and the straw that are in the grain; and they are hard as sinews. "Below there are husks, in Metatron": he is the Tree of Knowledge, good, and evil round about him, like the likeness of the husk and the fruit. "Who is in the likeness," etc.: he intended to explain to us that all that we say, that the husks enter within the holiness and rule over it, and the like, the matter is in Metatron, who is over against Tiferet and the Sefirot; and Tiferet is covered and concealed sometimes within him, as we explained in the Gate of ABYA. And for this reason we say that they enter the Sefirot; for one who touches the garment of the king is as one who touches the king himself. And we know that according to this it will be difficult for the inquirer from many sayings of the Zohar, and especially in Parashat Acharei (fol. 79), these are his words: "For we have learned: at the hour that the strong serpent is aroused because of the sins of the world, it begins and joins with the female and casts filth into her; the male separates from her, because she is defiled and is called impure, and it is not fitting for the male to draw near to her," etc., "at the time that she is defiled." Thus far his words.

ולא נאסוף ידינו מלבאר המאמר הזה לרבוי תועלתו. אמר לעילא באילנא דחיי, מפני שיש עץ החיים ועץ הדעת טוב ורע. ועץ החיים הוא התפארת בפרט. ובכלל כל האצילות כי שם חיים ואין מיתה רק במקום הקליפות שהם סביב עולם מטטרון שהוא עץ הדעת טוב ורע. כמו שביאר הרשב"י ע"ה בתיקונים (תקונא נ"א דף פ"ה) ז"ל ועץ הדעת טוב ורע איהו לתתא עץ הדעת טוב דא מטטרון, ורע דא סמאל עכ"ל בקוצר מפני האריכות הגדול. ומכאן הריסה להאומר כי עץ הדעת טוב ורע הוא במלכות שטעות הוא בידם. ומה שנמצא להרשב"י ע"ה פעמים קורא למלכות עץ הדעת טוב ורע הוא ע"ד השאלה כי כל מה שלמטה מהמלכות מתייחס אליה ועקרו במטטרון והושאל אל המלכות מפני שרגליה שהם מטטרו"ן יורדות מות שהם הקליפות. וייחס הקליפות בשם סמאל כי הוא שלוחם לכל ריב ולכל נגע לכל עוון ולכל חטאת כמפורסם בדברי הרשב"י ע"ה בכמה מקומות ובכמה מדרשים ואגדות שבתלמוד. ונחזור אל המאמר שאמר כי באלנא דחיי לית קליפין כי הוא מוח בלא קליפה והכריח כן מן הפסוק כי אין לבא אל שער המלך בלבוש שק. כמ"ש בערך שק ושק הוא הקליפה ומטעם זה השבים לובשים שק להראות כי מקבלים עליהם צער וענוי השק בעולם הזה תחת כי שנאו דעת קדושים והמליכו שק עליהם. ואפשר לומר עליו כי שק ר"ת שונא קדושים ובצירוף הוא ק"ש והם קש ותבן שבתבואה והם קשים כגידים. לתתא אית קליפין במטטרון, הוא עץ הדעת טוב, ורע סביבו, כדמות הקליפה ופרי. דאיהו בדיוקנא וכו', כוון לבאר לנו כי כל מה שאנו אומרים כי הקליפות נכנסים בתוך הקדושה ושולטים עליה וכדומה לזה הוא הענין במטטרון שהוא כנגד התפארת והספירות. והתפארת מתכסה ומתעלם לפעמים בתוכו כדפי' בשער אבי"ע. ומטעם זה אנו אומרים שהם נכנסים בספי' כי הנוגע בלבוש המלך כנוגע במלך עצמו. וידענו כי יקשה לפי זה אל המעיין מהמאמרים רבים מהזוהר ובפרט בפ' אחרי (דף ע"ט) וז"ל (תנן) דתנינן בשעתא דחיויא תקיפא אתער בגין חובי עלמא, שארי ואתחבר עם נוקבא ואטיל בה זוהמא, אתפרש דכורא מינה בגין דהא אסתאבת ואתקריאת מסאבא ולא אתחזי לדכורא למקרב בהדה כו' בזמנא דהיא אסתאבת עכ"ל.

25:6:3 And the inquirer will imagine that what he said, that it cast filth into her, is that it had intercourse with her, God forbid, and cast filth into her; as they wrote that the serpent came upon Eve, according to its plain sense, God forbid, far be it, far be it; for who has put forth his hand to this and been guiltless? And the words of Rashbi, peace be upon him, are "deep waters," etc., "but a man of understanding will draw it out" (Prov. 20:5). We have already written in the Gate of Channels (chapter 4) on the matter of the two channels that are in Yesod, one to the right and one to the left; for "from my flesh I shall see God" (Job 19:26). And just as in man there are two passages, one for the holy seed and one for the impure surplus, so above the surplus comes into being in Gevurah; for there is the refining of the silver and the gold, as we explained in the first chapter.

וידמה המעיין כי מה שאמר שהטיל בה זוהמא הוא ששמש הוא עמה ח"ו והטיל בה זוהמא כמ"ש שבא נחש על חוה כמשמעו ח"ו חלילה וחלילה כי מי נגע ידו בכך ונקה. ודברי הרשב"י ע"ה הם מים עמוקים כו' ואיש תבונה ידלנה. כבר כתבנו בשער הצנורות (פ"ד) מענין ב' צנורות שביסוד א' אל הימין וא' אל השמאל כי מבשרי אחזה אלוה וכמו שבאדם שני נקבים א' אל הזרע הקדוש וא' אל המותר הטמא כן למעלה המותר מתהוה בגבורה כי שם צרוף הכסף והזהב כדפי' בפ"ק.

25:6:4 And behold, when merit and uprightness and happiness and righteousness and fitness multiply in this world, then Malkhut is aroused from the side of the souls of the righteous, which are the female waters, and they are the cause of arousal, as is explained in the Gate of Essence and Governance; and then she draws from the side of the good oil; "and iniquity stops her mouth" (Ps. 107:42), and "all wickedness shall vanish like smoke." And when the people of this world are the reverse, then Malkhut draws from the side of the surplus, from the channel that bestows the dross of the silver and the dross of the gold, which is the impure effluence fitting for the husk. And the matter is that since Malkhut has nothing by which to be aroused and to claim the effluence, and there is no merit on which to depend, then the serpent is aroused and pleads accusation with the iniquities and transgressions done in the world, and begins to provoke and to accuse, and says: Master of the world, what have You seen to bestow blessings and good upon Israel? Behold my children, who are the seventy nations, whose souls are emanated from the husk, uncover nakedness, and Israel uncover nakedness; these worship idols, and these worship idols. And since it is so, give me the portion of my children. And then, God forbid, the power of strong judgment is aroused upon the enemies of Israel. And so they explained in the Tikkunim (Tikkun 21, fol. 50), these are his words: "And they are to distance the Satan from them, so that he not draw near to the Throne, which is the heart, to claim judgment against the holy limbs, which are Israel; and the heart is the Shekhinah among them." Thus far his words.

והנה ברבות הזכות והיושר והאושר והצדק והכושר בעולם הזה אז המלכות מתעוררת מצד נשמות הצדיקים שהן מיין נוקבין והם סבת התעוררות כמבואר בשער מהות וההנהגה. ואז יונקת מצד השמן הטוב ועולתה קפצה פיה והרשעה כולה כעשן נמלחה. וכאשר בני העה"ז להפך אז יונקת המלכות מצד המותר מן הצנור המשפיע סיגי הכסף וסיגי הזהב שהוא השפע הטמא הראוי אל הקליפה. והענין כי אחר שאין למלכות במה להתעורר ולתבוע השפע ואין זכות במה לתלות, אז מתעורר הנחש ומלמד קטיגורא עם העונות והעבירות הנעשים בעולם ומתחיל לקנטר ולקטרג, ואומר רבש"ע מה ראית להשפיע ברכות וטוב על ישראל הרי בני שהם ע' אומות שנשמתם נאצלת מן הקליפה מגלי עריות וישראל מגלי עריות אלו עובדי ע"ז ואלו עובדי ע"ז וכיון שכן תן לי חלק בניי ואז מתעורר ח"ו כח הדין החזק על שונאיהם של ישראל. וכן בארו בתיקונים (תקונא כ"א ד"ן) וז"ל ואינון לרחקא השטן מנייהו דלא יתקרב לגבי כורסייא דאיהו לבא לתבעא דינא על איברין קדישין דאינון ישראל ולבא שכינתא בינייהו עכ"ל.

25:6:5 That is to say, "to distance the Satan from them, so that he not draw near to the Throne": that is, the Throne of Glory, the world of Beriah. And when the husk enters into the world of Metatron, as we explained, it certainly draws near to the Throne, as we explained in the Gate of ABYA; for the world of Beriah is the Throne. And he was precise in saying that his nearness there is "to claim judgment": that is, that he says, these uncover nakedness and these uncover nakedness, etc.; and then the judgment is bestowed to the Shekhinah upon the enemies of Israel; and that is "the heart and the limbs" that he spoke of. And because of his crying, Malkhut does not draw from the side of the holy channel, but draws from the side of the left channel, in which the surplus and the dross are bestowed; and then the serpent is wound about her heel to receive this bitter effluence; for this is his power, his god. And then the Shekhinah tastes the bitternesses of that evil effluence. And this is the matter of "and casts filth into her": not that the intent is that he rises above, God forbid; but the intent is that through his arousal there is bestowed upon her what is more bitter than death.

ר"ל לרחקא השטן מנייהו דלא יקרב לגבי כורסייא, דהיינו כסא הכבוד עולם הבריאה. ובהיות הקליפה נכנסת בעולם של מטטרון כדפי', היא מתקרבת אל הכסא בודאי כדפי' בשער אבי"ע כי עולם הבריאה הוא הכסא. ודקדק לומר כי קורבתו שם הוא לתבעא דינא, דהיינו שאומר אלו מגלי עריות ואלו מגלי עריות וכו' ואז הדין נשפע אל השכינה על שונאיהם של ישראל, והיינו לבא ואיברין דקאמר. ובסבת צעקתה המלכות אינה יונקת מצד הצנור הקדוש אלא יונ' מצד הצנור השמאלי שבו יושפע המותר והסיגים ואז הנחש כרוך על עקיבה לקבל השפע המר הזה כי זו כחו לאלוהו. ואז השכינה טועמת מרורות השפע הרע ההוא. וזהו ענין ואטיל בה זוהמא שאין הכונה שיעלה למעלה ח"ו אמנם הכונה כי ע"י התעוררותו יושפע עליה מר ממות.

25:6:6 And behold, this bitter effluence is the blood of menstruation; and these are the stains that defile; and then the woman defiles the one who has intercourse with her, like the menstruant herself. And it is already known that "the portion of Jacob is not like these" (Jer. 10:16); for his inheritance is an inheritance without boundaries, and "in his joy a stranger does not intermeddle" (Prov. 14:10). And therefore she does not come to the King; for she is defiled, God forbid; and then the King does not come to her until after she is purified and reveals the light of her face, which was hidden, and whose lights were darkened. And she counts days, a number sevenfold, so that "the light of the moon be as the light of the sun"; and then she immerses in the waters of a ritual pool of forty se'ahs, which is a known palace in which Malkhut receives her purity and her adornments; and it is explained in the Tikkunim and in the Ra'aya Meheimna at length. And afterward she is united with her husband. And therefore you will find that after the great agitation, its end is compassion and favour.

והנה אין הקליפות לעולם נכנסים תוך הקדושה ח"ו ודי בזה הערה אל המעיין ואזהרה לבל יכשל לעולמי עד:

Chapter 25:7. Explanation that some of Rashbi's passages suggest the husks are ten, some suggest they are three, and some places suggest they are four.

25:7:1 Let us return to the explanation of the saying we were in: "And those husks," etc. Our principal intent in this chapter, in the explanation of the rest of this saying, is on the matter of a mighty question that falls in this inquiry. For what is understood from this saying and from many other sayings appears to be that the husks are three, apart from that thin one, which is "the still small voice," as we shall explain. And above, in chapter 4, we explained explicitly in the name of Rashbi, peace be upon him, and the other commentators, that the husks are ten, corresponding to ten Sefirot, specified and known by name. And there is a difficulty, his words against his words: for in one place he said that they are ten, and in one place he said that they are three or four. And in the answer to this question we need to know that the husks are before the holiness "as an ape before human beings." And just as the ape before the man does every deed of the man by way of mockery, wishing to resemble and not resembling, so the impurity and the husk do as the deed of holiness, whether in the matter of the souls, whether in the matter of the actions, whether in the matter of the colors. And just as the Emanation is included in three lines, the line of Chesed, the line of compassion and the line of judgment, as we spoke at length in the Gate of Essence and Governance at its end, so the impurity is included in Chesed, Judgment and Compassion. Not that the chesed is literal Chesed; rather chesed is as the Targum of "reproach," which is chisuda. And the compassion: "the compassion of the wicked is cruel" (Prov. 12:10). And the judgment needs no explanation; for it is an evil strap to lash the wicked; and further, it is the perversion of judgment, as he explained in the Zohar (Chadash, fol. 110b), in the Midrash of Lamentations, on the verse (Eccl. 12:3), "and the strong men shall bow themselves"; and the matter of their judgment will be further explained in the Palaces. And likewise in the matter of the colors: white, from there Laban [Lavan, white] the Aramean, and from there "an old and foolish king" (Eccl. 4:13), "the guilty elder." And likewise green, a sign of transgression: dropsy [hidrokan]. And likewise red: "from the red, this red [pottage]" (Gen. 25:30). And these three colors are over against the three holy colors that are in the bow. And this matter Rashbi, peace be upon him, explained in the Tikkunim (Tikkun 37, fol. 78a), these are his words: "Three husks there are in the foreskin, corresponding to three husks of the nut; and of them it is said, 'And the earth was formlessness and void, and darkness' (Gen. 1:2). Tohu, the green line, the first husk; bohu, the second husk; darkness, the third husk. And these three, one over another, correspond to three colors of the eye, of which it is said, 'and that you go not about after your own heart and your own eyes,' etc. (Num. 15:39). Corresponding to them, three colors of the bow, in which shines the pupil of the eye, which is a point, the sign of the covenant; of it it is said, 'and I will look upon it, that I may remember the everlasting covenant' (Gen. 9:16), etc. When? After the three evil husks are removed from it. And at the time that she is clothed in these husks, she says, 'Look not upon me, because I am black,' etc. (Song 1:6)." Thus far his words.

נחזור לבאור המאמר שהיינו בו ואינון קליפין וכו'. עיקר כונתינו בפ' זה בביאור שאר המאמר הזה הוא על ענין שאלה עצומה נופלת בדרוש הזה. כי המובן מתוך המאמר הזה וממאמרים אחרים רבים נראה שהקליפות הם ג' מלבד אותה הדקה שהיא קול דממה דקה כמו שנבאר. ולמעלה בפ"ד בארנו בפי בשם הרשב"י ע"ה ושאר המפרשים כי הקליפות הם י' כנגד י' ספירות מפורשות ידועות בשם. וקשה דידיה אדידיה, כי במ"א אמר שהם י' ובמקום א' אמר שהם ג' או ד'. ובתירוץ שאלה זו צריך שנדע כי הקליפות הם לפני הקדושה כקוף לפני בני אדם וכמו שהקוף לפני האדם עושה כל מעשה האדם דרך לעג רוצה להדמות ואינו נדמה. כן הטומאה והקליפה עושה כמעשה הקדושה, אם בענין הנשמות אם בענין הפעולות אם בענין הגוונים. וכמו שהאצילות נכלל בג' קוים קו החסד קו הרחמים וקו הדין כמו שהארכנו בשער מהות והנהגה בסופו, כן הטומאה נכללת בחס"ד די"ן ורחמים. לא חסד חסד ממש אלא חסד הוא כתרגום של חרפה שהוא חסודא. והרחמים רחמי רשעים אכזרי. והדין אין צריך לביאור כי הוא רצועה בישא להלקות הרשעים, ועוד שהוא עוות הדין כדפי' בזוהר (חדש דף ק"י ע"ב) במדרש איכה בפסוק (קהלת יב ג) והתעוותו אנשי החיל ויתבאר ענין דינם עוד בהיכלות. וכן בענין הגוונים לבן משם לבן הארמי ומשם זקן וכסיל זקן אשמאי. וכן ירוק סימן לעביר' הדרוקן. וכן אדום מן האדום האדום הזה. ושלשה הגוונים האלה נגד שלשה גוונים קדושים שבקשת וענין זה ביאר הרשב"י ע"ה בתיקונים (תקונא ל"ז דע"ח.) ז"ל ג' קליפין אינון בערלה לקבל תלת קליפין דאגוזא ועלייהו אתמר והארץ היתה תהו ובהו וחשך. תהו קו ירוק קליפה קדמאה, בהו קליפה תניינא, חשך קליפה תליתאה. ותלת אילין דא על גב דא לקבל תלת גונים דעינא דאתמר בהון ולא תתורו אחרי לבבכם ואחרי עיניכם וגו'. לקבלייהו תלת גוונין דקשת דבהון נהרא בת עין דאיהי נקודה אות ברית. עלה אתמר וראיתיה לזכור ברית עולם וכו' אימתי לבתר דמתעברין תלת קליפין בישין מינה. ובזמנא דאיהי מתלבשא באילין קליפין איהי אמרת אל תראוני שאני שחרחורת וכו' עכ"ל.

25:7:2 Behold, explicitly: these three husks are over against the three colors of the eye. And we have already explained above, in the Gate of Manifestations, in the name of Rashbi, that the three colors of the eye are the three Patriarchs; and so we explained in the entry Sabbath in the Gate of the Glossary of Titles. And likewise he said that they are the three colors of the bow; and the three colors of the bow are over against the three Patriarchs, which are fathers to Chesed, Judgment and Compassion: red, white, green. And for the reason that they are over against ChaDaR [Chesed, Din, Rachamim], therefore their annulment and their cutting down is through them. And so Rashbi, peace be upon him, explained in the Tikkunim (Tikkun 18, fol. 34b), these are his words: "And my son, until these husks of the nut are removed and broken, by teki'ah and shevarim and teru'ah: shevarim, in which is 'you shall break in pieces [shabber teshabber] their pillars' (Exod. 23:24), which are husks of the nut; and with teru'ah, 'you shall break [tero'em] them with a rod of iron' (Ps. 2:9); and with teki'ah, 'and hang them up [ve-hoka] unto YHVH against the sun' (Num. 25:4)." Thus far his words.

הנה בפי' כי ג' קליפות אלה הם נגד ג' גווני העין וכבר ביארנו למעלה בשער המציאיות בשם הרשב"י כי ג' גווני העין הם ג' אבות וכן בארנו בערך שבת בשער ערכי הכנויים. וכן אמר שהם ג' גווני הקשת וג' גווני הקשת הם נגד ג' אבות שהם אבות לחס"ד די"ן ורחמי"ם, אדו"ם לב"ן ירו"ק. ומטעם שהם נגד חד"ר לכן ביטולם וגידועם [ח] הוא על ידם. וכן ביאר הרשב"י ע"ה בתיקונים (תקונא י"ח דף ל"ד ע"ב) וז"ל וברי עד די אלין קליפין דאגוזא מתעברין ומתחברין בתקיע"ה ושברים ותרועה. שברים דבהון שבר תשבר מצבותיהם דאינון קליפין דאגוזא, ובתרועה תרועם בשבט ברזל, ובתקיעה והוקע אותם לה' נגד השמש עכ"ל.

25:7:3 And it has already been explained in the Gate of Names and in the preceding gates that teki'ah, teru'ah, shevarim are over against the three Patriarchs, Chesed, Judgment and Compassion; and we explained it also in the Gate of the Glossary of Titles. And by this our question is answered: that they are ten and are included in three, as we explained. However, one must examine: since they are included in three, which are over against the three Patriarchs, why are they the reverse of the order of holiness? For in holiness they are white, Chesed, first; and afterward green in the middle, over against Tiferet; and the red to the left, over against Gevurah. And in the foreskin they are the reverse: for first is the green husk, and after it white, and after it darkness and fire, which are all one. And Rashbi, peace be upon him, explained this order in the Tikkunim (there, above this), these are his words: "'And I looked, and behold, a whirlwind came out of the north' (Ezek. 1:4): this is the line of tohu, the green husk of the nut, which is the green line. 'A great cloud': the second husk, the white of the nut, and this is bohu. 'And a fire infolding itself': the third husk of the nut [and this is darkness. 'And a brightness was about it': this is the fourth husk of the nut, which] is joined to the kernel. 'And out of the midst thereof as the colour of chashmal': this is the kernel." Thus far his words.

וכבר נתבאר בשער השמות ובשערים הקודמים כי תקיעה תרועה שברים הם נגד ג' אבות חסד דין ורחמים ובארנו גם כן בשער ערכי הכנויים. ובזה ניתרצה שאלתינו כי הם עשר ונכללים בג' כדפי'. אמנם צריך לדקדק כי אחר שהם נכללים בג' שהם נגד ג' אבות למה הם הפך סדר הקדושה כי בקדושה הם לבן חסד ראשונה ואח"כ ירוק באמצע נגד הת"ת והאדום לשמאל נגד הגבורה, ובהערלה הם להפך כי ראשונה היא קליפה ירוקה ואחריה לבנה ואחריה חשך ואש שהכל א'. וביאר הסדר הזה הרשב"י ע"ה בתיקונים (שם למעלה מזה) וז"ל וארא והנה רוח סערה באה מן הצפון דא קו תהו קליפה ירוקה דאגוזא דאיהו קו ירוק. ענן גדול קליפה תניינא חיוורא דאגוזא ודא בוהו. ואש מתלקחת קליפה תליתאה דאגוזא [ודא חשך. ונוגה לו סביב דא קליפה ד' דאגוזא דאיהו] אתאחדא במוחא ומתוכה כעין החשמל דא מוחא עכ"ל.

25:7:4 And behold, he explained explicitly that the first is green, and the second white, and the third is darkness and fire. And it appears to answer by what Rashbi, peace be upon him, explained on the verse "and God divided between the light and the darkness" (Gen. 1:4), and on the verse "Separate yourselves from among this congregation" (Num. 16:21): that the impurity and the husk do not want the arbitration; and therefore Korah and his congregation were separated, for they did not want the arbitration of Moses our teacher, peace be upon him; and they fell into Gehinnom. And we hinted at this matter in the preceding gates. And for this reason the green line, the arbiter, which is the middle line, is not in the middle between the white husk and the red, to show that they are the root of strife and quarrel, and there is no peace between them at all. And the green came first; for since it was pushed out from between the two extremes, it inclined toward the chasadim and the reproaches [cherpot], after the way of holiness; except that it inclined entirely and did not arbitrate between them. For the white is the utmost hardness, and the red, its manifestation is soft; and even if it is hard in separation and division, it cleaves to it with the utmost cleaving, as we shall explain, with the help of the Name. And the two extremes are found together, and the middle pushed outside, as we explained. And let us return to the matter of the saying: because three husks are over against the three Patriarchs, which are three living creatures of the Chariot, therefore they are husks to the living creatures that are in Metatron. And he said that they are three husks, specified by names. The first, tohu, the green line; the whirlwind in Ezekiel, and the strong wind in Elijah. And it is already known that the wind [ru'ach] is the arbiter between the fire and the water on the side of holiness; and in impurity they pushed it outside, and it inclined to the side of chesed and the reproaches, as we explained. The second, bohu, slimy stones; and the explanation of mefullamot is strong; so he explained in the Arukh, in the entry mefullam: "slimy stones, sound, moist and strong." And so it is the hardest husk of all; for the husks are the reverse of holiness; and they are moist, flowing with water: they are "the bitter waters that cause the curse" (Num. 5:18). And likewise it is called earthquake, and "a great cloud," etc.; for the cloud is from the side of the waters, in Chesed; and so this cloud is impure, in the place of the pure. And the third, fire and darkness; for it is already well known that the fire that is on the side of impurity is dark; for the black is red that has been smitten, as we explained in the Gate of Colors. And beneath these three husks is the husk that draws near to the kernel, which draws near to holiness, which is in the air [and it is the husk of Nogah]; and it is almost holiness, as we shall explain in the Gate of the Palaces of the Husks. And it is the fourth division; and it is what is between pure and impure; for it is intermediate, and it is clear. However, one may wonder: for there is no air in the nut between the thin husk and the kernel; but it is before the thin husk. And this is no doubt: for the air that we speak of is the air that there is somewhat when they are separated from one another, that is, the thin husk from the kernel. Or we may say that when it is moist, so that it is easy to be removed, as the saying explained, then there is some separation between this and that. Or we may also say that in the nut the thin husk cleaves to the kernel because of its nearness also to holiness. However it may be, the air, which is the abyss, is the husk nearer to holiness than the three preceding ones. And of the three it is said, "YHVH was not in the wind," "not in the earthquake," "not in the fire"; however, of "the still small voice" it is said, in it is YHVH. And this is "and a brightness was about it, and out of the midst thereof as the colour of chashmal"; for this fourth husk draws near to holiness most; therefore Rashbi, peace be upon him, sometimes does not mention it with the three, and it is not included in the ten impurities at all. And for this, concerning the planting of the tree, the verse said (Lev. 19:23–24), "three years shall it be as uncircumcised unto you"; for the husk always precedes the fruit; however, "in the fourth year all the fruit thereof shall be holy, for giving praise unto YHVH." For just as the firstling of an ass is holy, for it is forbidden to make use of it, and it needs redemption; and this is for the reason that, being a firstborn, it has drawn near to holiness; and if he does not redeem it, it needs its neck broken, so that the power of the husk not prevail over it and it be found separated from the place of holiness; so the fruit of the fourth year is holy and needs redemption.

והנה ביאר בפי' כי הראשונה ירוקה והשנייה לבנה והג' היא חשך ואש. ונראה לתרץ במה שביאר הרשב"י ע"ה בפ' ויבדל אלדים בין האור ובין החושך (בראשית א) ובפ' הבדלו מתוך העדה הזאת (במדבר טז כא) כי הטומאה והקליפה אינה רוצה בהכרעה ולפיכך נבדלו קרח ועדתו שלא רצו בהכרעתו של מרע"ה ונפלו לגיהנם ורמזנו ענין זה בשערים הקודמים. ומטעם זה אין הקו הירוק המכריע הוא הקו האמצעי באמצע בין הקליפה הלבנה והאדומה להורות כי הם שרש התגר והקטטה ואין ביניהם שלום כלל ועקר. וקדמה הירוקה כי כיון שנדחית מבין שני הקצוות נטה כלפי החסדים והחרפות כדרך הקדושה אלא שנטה מכל וכל ולא הכריע ביניהם. כי הלבנה תכלית הקושי והאדומה רפה מציאותה ואם היא קשת ההפרדה והחילוק כי היא נדבקת עמה תכלית הדיבוק כאשר נבאר בעה"ו ונמצאו שני הקצוות יחד והאמצעי דחוי לחוץ כדפי'. ונחזור לענין המאמר לפי שג' קליפות הם נגד ג' אבות שהם ג' חיות המרכבה ולכן הם קליפות לחיות שבמטטרון. ואמר כי הם ג' קליפות מפורשות בשמות. הראשונה תהו קו ירוק רוח סערה ביחזקאל ורוח חזק באליהו. וכבר נודע כי הרוח הוא המכריע בין האש ובין המים בצד הקדושה. ובטומאה דחו אותו לחוץ ונטה המאררים. וכן נקרא רעש וענן גדול וכו' כן הענן מצד המים בחסד וכן הענן הזה טמא במקום טהור. והשלישית אש וחשך כי כבר מפורסם כי האש שבצד הטומאה הוא חשוך כי השחור אדום הוא אלא שלקה כדפי' בשער הגוונים. ותחת ג' קליפות אלו, היא קליפה מתקרבת אל המוח המתקרבת אל הקדושה שהוא באויר [והיא קלי' נוגה] והיא כמעט קדושה כמו שנבאר בשער היכלות הקליפות והיא חלוקה רביעית והוא מה שבין טהור לטמא שהוא בינוני וברור הוא. אמנם וזהו מטעם כי בהיותו בכור נתקרב אל הקדושה ואם לא יפדהו צריך עריפה כדי שלא יגביר עליו כח הקליפה ונמצא מתפרש ממקום הקדושה כן הפרי של שנה הרביעית הוא קדוש וצריך פדיון.

25:7:5 And behold, the firstling of an ass, even though the owner gives it to the priest, the priest cannot make use of it unless he redeems it with a lamb. But the fruit of the fourth year, if he wishes, he may eat the fruit himself in Jerusalem, or redeem it and eat its value in Jerusalem, like the law of all second tithe. And the reason is that the ass is the portion of impurity itself, and its portion is strong; therefore it needs redemption; and even though it is in the hand of a priest, there is still a doubt lest it go out to evil ways; and even in the hand of a priest, who is the right, it needs redemption. But the fruit of the fourth year, when he eats it in Jerusalem, where there is no husk at all, neither thin nor coarse, it is enough; and in this it is clear to us that it will not go out of its holiness.
