Gate 22: The Gate of Titles

We named it "The Gate of Titles" because in it is explained the matter of the titles (kinnuyim), the reason for their multiplicity, what they are and their essence; and further, the matter of the human being as a chariot (merkavah) for the sefirot. Its chapters number four.

Chapter 22:1. Explanation of the matter in saying "eye," "hand," "foot," "ear" with respect to the Godhead — from which aspect each is attributed and in what manner.

22:1:1 The Twenty-Second Gate: The Gate of Titles.

שער כב הוא שער הכינויים

22:1:2 What is intended in this Gate is to set out the matter of the titles: what they are about, and what they are. And in it will be explained the well-known matter that the human being is a chariot for the Sefirot, with the help of Heaven:

הנרצה בשער הזה הוא לכלול ענין הכנויים מה עניינם ומהותם. ובו יתבאר הענין שהוא מפורסם שהאדם הוא מרכבה אל הספירות בע"ה:

22:1:3 Chapter One:

פרק ראשון:

22:1:4 Among the astonishing things in the words of our Torah is what is written in the Torah: "and under His feet," etc. (Exod. 24:10); "written with the finger of God," etc. (Exod. 31:18); "the hand of YHVH," "the eyes of YHVH," "the ears of YHVH," and things like these, which are titles drawn from the body. And this cannot be attributed to Him, may He be blessed, for He is neither a body nor a power in a body. Therefore these matters cannot be attributed to His Sefirot either, for they too are far from all the dimensions of body. For if not, since it is known that He is the soul of His Sefirot and is the power in them, it would follow, God forbid, that He is a power in a body, far be it, far be it. Therefore it is fitting to remove the dimensions of body from the Sefirot, and we are bound to this just as we are bound to remove them from the Self of the Ein Sof. If so, when [Scripture] speaks in dimensions drawn from the dimensions of body, such as "and YHVH saw," "and YHVH heard," "and YHVH said," "and YHVH spoke," which are dimensions that can be gauged only in corporeal organs, it is fitting that we believe that they describe a hidden matter, and not literal seeing, hearing or speech. And there is no doubt that in our holy tongue the eye is not called "eye," the ear "ear" and the mouth "mouth" by chance and happenstance, or by convention as in the other languages. And the proof of this is that this tongue of ours is called the holy tongue [leshon ha-kodesh], which certainly means that it is a divine invention, and not like the other languages. And there is no doubt that it is the tongue that the Holy One, blessed be He, invented when He brought His world into being; so the tongue certainly preceded the world. And the matter is this: when the Holy One, blessed be He, created Adam, the first man, He created him a holy body and a holy soul, beyond all measure, as his praises are recounted in the Book of the Tikkunim, so that his light shone in the body, and the glorious body dimmed the orb of the sun; so that he certainly did not enjoy an outer light, the light of the luminaries, sun and moon, but a fair and pure light, the light of the radiance of the Shekhinah in the Garden of Eden. And since Adam, the first man, was such a holy body, the Holy One, blessed be He, handed over to him the treasures of wisdom, and the keys of the Torah were given into his hand, and he occupied himself with it. Then He gave him the holy tongue, the glorious tongue, and holy letters were fixed in his mouth. And when he sinned, what had been "garments of light" [or] with an Alef, which is the holiness of the union of the world, became, when he abandoned himself to the husk, "[garments of] skin" [or] with an Ayin, which is the seventy [the numerical value of Ayin] branches that spread out from the inner to the outer. And there was not yet a portion particular to the Holy One, blessed be He, because the light of Abraham had not yet shone, until after the generation of the Dispersion, as we explained in the Gate of Gates, chapter 3. And when the light of Abraham our father, peace be upon him, shone, the Holy One, blessed be He, divided seventy nations among seventy princes, for seventy tongues; and He chose us and gave speech to us as our portion, which is our lot, a holy lot, and the holy tongue, a divine convention: that the ear should be called "ear" and the mouth "mouth," to indicate the powers that are a shadow over a man's head; and the organ was emanated from its supernal power, which emanates it and bestows upon it. And now, according to the truth, what is meant by "ear" is not the bodily ear but the action performed by means of the ear through the power that bestows upon it. And of this kind it is said (Ps. 94:9), "He that planted the ear, shall He not hear? He that formed the eye, shall He not see?" The explanation: since the creation of the organs of the human being is through the cycling of the holy alphabet, [letters] that draw effluence from the supernal powers, the roots, which are united in the Root of roots, there must necessarily be in that letter the power of the action through which the created organ acts; and likewise in the root that bestows upon the letter; and so from cause to effect down to the bottom. For from the actions the powers are known; and since their power is seeing and hearing, there must be in that root the power of hearing, and likewise of seeing; but not corporeal seeing and hearing, rather something spiritual. The parable: just as people, when they hear the sound of speech with their ears, weigh the matter that they hear, so the supernal power that receives that voice and weighs it, whether it is fit to receive it or not, is called "ear." And of this it is said (Job 34:3), "For the ear tries words." And likewise the matter with the angels, who have no bodily power such as our mouth, with which they would speak this speech of ours, which is bodily. Rather, when an angel looks at an angel they understand each other. For the effluence of the name YHVH [pours] into the selfhood and the spirituality of the letters that are wanted for the matter that he wants [to convey], and by this his fellow understands the thing that he wants; and that is their speech. And they have no eyes like the eyes of human beings, with which they see; but they have the spirituality of the action that brings about sight, and by it they comprehend and look far off. And it is more glorious than our seeing, as the seeing of the intellect is more glorious than the eye. For the eye sees only what is before it, and not all that is before it, but only a limited thing that the corporeal vessel of the eye can encompass. But the intellect sees even what is not before the eye: as a man now sees in his intellect many things that he saw many years ago, or even things that he has never seen, which he devises in his mind as though he were seeing such a matter; until the seeing of the intellect is so fine that it sees subtle matters that the eye cannot look upon. And greater than this is the seeing of the prophets: for the prophet apprehends spiritual things with his intellect, and as the breadth of the intellect, so the breadth of the apprehension. And so Solomon, peace be upon him, said (Prov. 27:19), "As in water face answers to face, so the heart of man to man": for the heart of man to man, they understand each other from self to self; and it is a bodily organ, and it has this power because of the influx of the soul, and likewise of the intellect that dwells upon it. And nevertheless, since it is a limited thing, its apprehension is limited; and according to the measure of the prophet, so is the level of his prophecy. And from this matter we ascend to the understanding of the angels one for another, and from there we attain many and many levels to know the existence of seeing and of the corporeal actions among the spiritual ones. And as the prophet said (Zech. 4:2), "I have seen, and behold, a lampstand all of gold"; and likewise (1 Sam. 9:9), "for he who is now called a prophet was formerly called a seer": the matter will not be a seeing with organs, nor will the word "seeing" be by way of parable; rather it is literally the seeing of apprehension. So it is above: seeing in the angels is that power of apprehension by which they apprehend matters. And this is the matter of Rabbi Eleazar son of Rabbi Shimon, who went to see Rabbi Yose ben Lakunya, his father-in-law (brought in the Addenda to the Zohar, part 3, fol. 304), and on their way they saw that matter of the serpent; Rabbi Eleazar spoke those words, and the companions were astonished, and he told them that he had not apprehended that matter through wisdom but through a seeing that he saw. Now can one say that Rabbi Eleazar saw those matters with eyes of flesh? Rather, this is certainly the seeing of seclusion [hitbodedut], by which a person apprehends matters that are to come and matters that have passed, and through the subtlety of the intellect hears the matters.

זה. כי שפע שם ידו"ד בעצמיות וברוחניות האותיות הנרצות אל הענין הנרצה אליו, ועי"ז יבין חברו הדבר הנרצה אליו, וזהו דבורם. ואין להם עינים כעיני בני אדם שבהם הם רואים. אמנם יש להם רוחניות הפעולה שהיא פועלת 22:1:5 Behold, after this manner is the seeing among the upper ones, and among the upper ones above the upper ones, until "one higher than the high watches" (Eccl. 5:7). And this is no wonder, for even among the bodily vessels that are in us, the eye is not called "eye" because of the eye itself but because of the power of sight, which is truly called "eye"; and likewise the hand is not called "hand" because of the hand itself, but the action that issues from the hand is truly called "hand." And the proof of this is their saying, of blessed memory, in Gittin (85b): "Hands [yadayim] that are not evident: are they hands or are they not hands?" meaning the matter of taking hold; and so Rashi, of blessed memory, explained: "yadayim: a handle and a grip on the matter, to hold on to it and to say that by it," etc. And likewise in the matter of betrothal and divorce: "a minor girl has no hand," or "has a hand"; and the intent is not that she has no hand because she is maimed, but the intent is the power of receiving, to receive betrothal or divorce. And likewise it is customary in the language of our Sages, of blessed memory: "the matter has legs," or "it has no legs"; and the intent is not that it has legs or has no legs, but it is a parable: just as the legs are a person's standing and support, so when a thing has a basis and an existence that settles in the intellect, it is said that the matter has legs; and when the thing does not settle, it is said that it has no legs. And likewise what is written in the Torah (Exod. 21:24), "eye for eye, hand for hand, foot for foot," where we learn "striking" from "striking" from "he who strikes the life of a beast shall make it good, life for life" (Lev. 24:18); and the tradition has come that the intent is not to say that if he cut off his hand they cut off his hand, and likewise the other organs, but the intent is that he pays him the whole value of the hand, and likewise the other organs; and that is what is called "eye," "hand" and "foot," since it is the worth of the power of the eye, the hand and the foot. And so are the matters among the spiritual ones: the power that spreads out from the Sefirot to watch over the world, from the power of the Ein Sof, is called "eyes." And so (Zech. 4:10), "these seven are the eyes of YHVH, which range through the whole earth"; and the Kabbalists explained that they are seven powers, and they are called Orpaniel, Tagriel, Danel, Palmiel, Asimon, Safiel, Boel, and they are those that watch over the affairs of human beings. And since they have the power of providence they are called "eyes"; not that they are eyes, but that there is spiritual providence in them, as we explained above. And in them all deeds are inscribed, from good to bad; and through them the two luminaries testify concerning "the doings and the steps of a man," "the thoughts of a man and his schemes," and "the inclinations of a man's deeds" before the seventy judges, as is explained in the Gate of Palaces, in the Palace of Merit, chapter 4. And over these eyes there are yet other eyes, for "one higher than the high watches, and there are higher ones over them" (Eccl. 5:7). (And after this manner too in the verse (Micah 6:2), "Hear, O mountains, the controversy of YHVH": for just as the mountains hold up the world and keep it upright on the scale of a balance, as our Rabbis, of blessed memory, explained, and they are high in the heights of the world, so the Patriarchs hold us up by their merit and keep the world standing in its place, and they stand in the heights of the world.) And likewise what Moses our teacher, peace be upon him, said to Jethro (Num. 10:31), "and you shall be to us as eyes"; and Rashi, of blessed memory, explained: "every single thing that is concealed from our eyes, you shall enlighten our eyes." Thus far his words.

22:1:6 And since, just as the eyes show the body the right way in which it should walk, so the teacher of righteousness is called "eyes." And after this manner we may infer for all the other things that are said by way of title of the Sefirot. For example, "father" and "mother": the intent is not, God forbid, that there are a father and a mother there, for that is heresy, God forbid. Rather the intent is that, just as the existence of begetting comes about through the joining of male and female, and through that joining that offspring comes into being, so it is with the Sefirot: through the joining of Chokhmah and Binah, and the bestowal of Chokhmah into Binah, was the cause of the whole Emanation. For in Chokhmah the beings were subtle in the extreme, as the son is concealed in the brain of the father; and after they spread out into Binah they took on body and were given form, a more revealed form. And therefore Chokhmah is called "father": for just as the father's sons are in his self and in his brain, subtle in the extreme subtlety, as a drop, and by means of the joining of the male to the female that drop is aroused and comes to be a seminal drop, and he bestows it into the female, and through his effluence in her that offspring comes into being in her womb; so Chokhmah is the father, and Binah is the female, who receives all the forms that are bestowed from Chokhmah, and a more revealed form is formed in her, and she emanates that Emanation. And for this she is called "female" and "mother" of the Emanation, and the Emanation is called by the name "sons" to them, for the reason mentioned. And it is all a parable from the corporeal to the spiritual. And likewise when Tiferet and Malkhut are called "bridegroom" and "bride," and Binah "mother" or "mother-in-law," and Chokhmah "father" or "father-in-law."

22:1:8 And behold, Tiferet and Malkhut are emanated from Chokhmah and Binah; and in the manifestation of their simple emanation they have no coupling together, as we explained above in the Gate of the Diminishment [of the Moon]. And they have union only after their tikkun, their arming and their adornment. Therefore, from that aspect Tiferet and Malkhut are called son and daughter, and Chokhmah and Binah father and mother. And Chokhmah is called father to Tiferet from the aspect of the concealed Da'at; and Binah is called mother to Tiferet from the side of the establishing by which she establishes him (from the language of "and by understanding [tevunah] it is established" [yitkonan] (Prov. 24:3)), which is a manifestation added to what is emanated from Chokhmah, as we explained above. For from Chokhmah they have a subtle manifestation, and from the side of Binah a more revealed manifestation. And from the side of the subtle manifestation that they have, which is the manifestation of a Yod for Malkhut and the manifestation of a firmament for Tiferet, as we explained in Gate 1, chapter 2, and in the Gate of Manifestations, chapter 4, they are called sons of Chokhmah; and from the revealed manifestation that they have from Binah they are called her sons. And when they are afterward united in the secret of the diadems with which they are crowned, as it is written (Song 3:11), "Go forth, O daughters of Zion, and behold King Solomon," etc., they are called bridegroom and bride, man and his wife. And there are in the union many distinctions and aspects, which will be explained in many places in the book Or Yakar, with the help of the Name. And when the revealed manifestation is united with the revealed manifestation, or the hidden manifestation with the hidden manifestation, then they are simply children of Chokhmah and Binah, united together in a complete union; and they are son and daughter, brother and sister, permitted to one another. However, bridegroom and bride, [and] son and daughter, cannot be attributed to them together in relation to Chokhmah and Binah; for if he is son to Chokhmah he is son-in-law to Binah, and then Binah is called his mother-in-law, or Chokhmah his father-in-law if he is son to Binah. And after this manner the inquirer may infer for all the aspects. Chapter 22:2. Citation of the words of Sefer ha-Orah on revealing the reason for the multiplicity of titles according to the multitude of aspects.

22:2:1 The Rav, the author of Sefer ha-Orah (Gate 1, fol. 2), spoke at length on the titles and their matter, close to what we have dealt with; and therefore we shall copy his language here. And these are his words: "Know that the truth of the Self of the Creator, may He be blessed, is not apprehended by any other than Him; and none of all the multitudes on high know His place, how much less the Self of His truth. Do you not see what the angels on high say: 'Blessed be the glory of YHVH from His place' (Ezek. 3:12), wherever it may be? And if so among the upper ones, how much more so among the lower ones. And those matters that we read in the Torah, such as 'hand' or 'foot,' 'ear,' 'eye,' and all that is like them, what are they? Know and believe that all those matters, although they show and testify to His greatness and His truth, no creature can know and understand the essence of that thing that is called 'hand' or 'foot' or 'eye' or 'ear' and the like. And though we are made in the image and the likeness, let it not enter your mind that the eye is in the literal form of an eye, or the hand in the literal form of a hand, or the foot in the literal form of a foot; rather they are inner matters, and inner within inner, in the truth of the existence of the Name, from which the source and the effluence go out to all that exists (by the decree of the Name, may He be blessed). But the essence of the hand is not like the essence of a hand, nor is its shape like the shape of a hand, as Scripture says (Isa. 40:25), 'To whom then will you liken Me, that I should be equal?' And know and understand that there is no likeness between Him and us on the side of substance and shape; but the intent of the form of the organs that are in us is that they are made in the likeness of signs for inner, closed, supernal matters, which the mind cannot know except as a likeness of remembrance. As when one writes 'Reuben son of Jacob': for these letters and this form are not the self of Reuben son of Jacob, nor his form, his shape or his essence; but a remembrance that this 'Reuben son of Jacob' that is written is a sign corresponding to that known substance and shape that is called Reuben son of Jacob. And because the Name, may He be blessed, wished to give us merit, He created in the body of the human being many organs, hidden and revealed, in the likeness of a sign of the Work of the Chariot. And if a person merits to purify one of his organs, that organ will be like a throne for that supernal inner thing that is called by this name: if eye, eye; if hand, hand; if foot, foot; and so for the rest. How so? For example, if he guards and takes care with the sight of his eyes, that he does not look at forbidden unions or at any other shameful thing, but only at everything that is the holiness of the Name and His service, then that eye becomes as a throne for that thing that is called 'eye' above; and likewise the hand, and likewise the foot, and the other things.

הרב בעל ספר האורה (בשער א' ד"ב) האריך בכנויים ובעניינם קרוב למה שהתעסקנו בו ולכן נעתיק לשונו הנה. וז"ל דע כי אמיתת עצם הבורא יתברך איננו מושגת לזולתו. ואין בכל המוני מעלה יודעים מקומו כ"ש עצם אמיתתו. הלא תראה מלאכי מעלה מה הם אומרים ברוך כבוד ה' ממקומו (יחזקאל א) בכל מקום שהוא. ואם בעליונים כן בתחתונים עאכ"ו. ואותם העניינים שאנו קוראים בתורה כגון יד או רגל אזן עין וכל כיוצא בהם מהו. דע והאמן כי כל אותם הענינים אע"פ שהם מורים ומעידים על גדולתו ואמיתתו אין כל בריה יכולה לדעת ולהתבונן מהות אותו הדבר הנקרא יד או רגל או עין או אוזן וכיוצא. ואם אנו עשויים בצלם ובדמות אל יעלה בדעתך כי עין כצורת עין ממש או יד כצורת יד ממש או רגל כצורת רגל ממש אבל הם עניינים פנימיים ופנימים לפנימיים באמיתות מציאות השם אשר מהם המקור והשפע יוצא לכל הנמצאים (בגזירת השי"ת). אבל אין מהות יד כמהות יד ולא תבנית כתבנית יד כשה"כ (ישעיה מ כה) ואל מי תדמיוני ואשוה. ודע והבן שאין בינו ובינינו דמיון מצד העצם והתבנית, אלא על כוונת צורת האברים אשר בנו שהן עשוים בדמיון סימנים לעניינים פנימיים סתומים עליונים שאין הדעת יכולה לדעתם אלא כדמיון זכרון. כמו שכותב ראובן בן יעקב שהרי אין אלו האותיות וזו הצורה עצמותו של ראובן בן יעקב וצורתו ותבניתו ומהותו, אלא זכרון שזה ראובן בן יעקב הכתוב הוא סימן כנגד אותו עצם והתבנית הידוע הנקרא ראובן בן יעקב. ולפי שהשי"ת רצה לזכותנו ברא בגוף האדם כמה אברים נסתרים ונגלים בדמיון סימן למעשה מרכבה, ואלו יזכה האדם לטהר אבר מאבריו יהיה אותו האבר כדמיון כסא לאותו דבר העליון הפנימי הנקרא בשם זה אם עין עין אם יד יד אם רגל רגל וכן בשאר. כיצד כגון שנשמר ונזהר במראה עיניו שלא יביט בעריות ולא בשאר דבר של גנאי אלא בכל דבר שהוא קדושת השם ועבודתו אז אותו עין נעשית כמו כסא לאותו דבר הנקרא למעלה עין וכן היד וכן הרגל ושאר הדברים.

22:2:2 "And further, our Rabbis, of blessed memory, said: 'The Patriarchs, they are the Chariot.' And they did not say 'each one of the Patriarchs is the Chariot,' but 'the Patriarchs, they are the Chariot.' How so? Abraham our father took the right side in purity and inherited the right [side] on high, which is the attribute of Chesed; and of this it is said (Gen. 12:9), 'And Abraham journeyed, going on still toward the south.' And Isaac took in purity the attribute of the left side, which is Fear [Pachad]; and of this it is said (Gen. 31:53), 'And Jacob swore by the Fear of his father Isaac.' And Jacob took in purity the middle line; and of this it is said (Gen. 25:27), 'And Jacob was a whole man, dwelling in tents,' etc. Thus the three Patriarchs are found to be a throne for the Chariot." And "the words of a wise man's mouth are gracious" (Eccl. 10:12), even though there is some difference between his way and ours. And now we wish to set out one more premise concerning the titles:

ועוד אז"ל האבות הן הן המרכבה. ולא אמרו כל אחד מהאבות הוא המרכבה אלא האבות הן הן המרכבה. כיצד אברהם אבינו לקח בטהרה צד הימין וירש ימין של מעלה שהוא מדת החסד וע"ז נאמר (בראשית יב ט) ויסע אברהם הלוך ונסוע הנגבה. ויצחק לקח בטהרה מדת צד השמאל שהוא הפחד וע"ז נאמר (שם לא נג) וישבע יעקב בפחד אביו יצחק. ויעקב לקח בטהרה קו האמצעי וע"ז נאמר (שם כה כז) ויעקב איש תם יושב אהלים כו'. נמצאו השלשה אבות כסא למרכבה. ודברי פי חכם חן, ועם היות שיש חילוק קצת בין דרכו לדרכנו. ועתה רצוננו להקדים בענין הכנויים עוד הקדמה אחת:

22:2:3 And it is this: at first sight the thoughtful one will be astonished when he sees different titles for the ten Sefirot, without number, whether in the Scriptures or in the words of our Rabbis, of blessed memory; so much so that on this account Rabbi Moses de Leon wrote in the introduction to Sefer ha-Shem that the reason for the multitude of titles is that the sages intended to hide this wisdom, and each one devised hidden names for himself, to suppress the wisdom under his tongue. "Lo, these are but the edges of his ways" (Job 26:14); and we do not see it so, God forbid. But we will further ask: in the Zohar and in the Tikkunim, on the matter of the Tabernacle, the Sefirot are hinted at many times. For the Tabernacle itself is the Shekhinah; the ten curtains are the ten Sefirot; the curtains of the hangings, called in the language of the Torah "court," are the Shekhinah; the inner palace is the Shekhinah; one of the cherubim is the Shekhinah; one of the tablets is the Shekhinah; the ark is the Shekhinah, the table the Shekhinah, the lampstand the Shekhinah, the outer altar the Shekhinah, the frankincense the Shekhinah, the sockets the Shekhinah; and more like them, the bread on the table the Shekhinah, and the lamps on the lampstand the Shekhinah, and many matters besides these. Now what is this matter? Would one hint at the Shekhinah from among these things not be enough for us, without all these matters? Rather, the truth of the matter is certainly that the matter of the Sefirot is not one substance that is not distinguished into different aspects, so that it would be called by one name alone. Rather, the Sefirot are distinguished into many aspects, many matters and many manifestations; and for each and every aspect there is a name and a title; and what this title shows, that title does not show. And now, although this matter has already come before us in the preceding gates, such as the Gate of Channels and the Gate of Manifestations and others, nevertheless we shall not withhold our hands from dealing with it as much as is needed to understand this inquiry well. And we shall illustrate this matter with Malkhut, and from there infer to the other Sefirot.

והיא שלכאורה ישתומם המשכיל בראותו לי' ספירות כנויים שונים עד אין מספר. אם בכתובים אם בדברי רז"ל, עד שמפני זה כתב ר' משה דילאון בהקדמת ספר השם כי טעם רבוי הכנויים הוא שהחכמים כוונו להסתיר החכמה הזאת וכל אחד העלה לעצמו שמות נסתרים להכחיד החכמה תחת לשונו. הן אלה קצות דרכיו. ואנחנו לא נראה כן ח"ו. אבל עוד נוסיף ונשאל כי נמצא בזהר ובתקונים בענין המשכן כמה פעמים נרמזים הספירות. שהרי המשכן עצמו היא השכינה עשר יריעות ע"ס, היריעות של הקלעים הנקראת בלשון התורה חצר היא השכינה, היכל הפנימי היא השכינה, א' מן הכרובים שכינה, א' מן הלוחות שכינה, הארון שכינה השלחן שכינה המנורה שכינה המזבח החיצון שכינה לבונה שכינה האדנים שכינה, ועוד כמוהם הלחם שעל השלחן שכינה ונרות שעל המנורה שכינה, וכמה עניינים זולתן. ועתה מהו הענין הזה וכי לא יספיק לנו ברמז א' מהדברים אל השכינה ולא כל אלו העניינים. אלא ודאי אמיתת הענין הוא כי אין ענין הספירות עצם א' בלתי מתבחן אל בחינות שונות עד שיקרא בשם א' לבד. אמנם הספירות יבחנו אל כמה בחינות ואל כמה עניינים וכמה מציאיות. ולכל בחינה ובחינה שם וכנוי. ומה שיורה הכנוי הזה לא יורה הכנוי הזה. ועתה עם היות שכבר קדם לנו הענין הזה בשערים הקודמים כמו שער הצינורות ושער המציאיות וזולתם, עכ"ז לא נאסוף ידינו מלעסוק בו די צורך להשכיל הדרוש הזה היטב. והענין הזה נמשיל במלכות ומשם נקיש אל שאר הספירות.

22:2:4 Behold, it was explained in the Gate of Essence and Governance that the Sefirot are a power equal to each thing and to its opposite; and accordingly the attribute changes from aspect to aspect, according to the manifestation of its receiving from one of the upper Sefirot, to act by the will of the Emanator. And now, when we consider that Malkhut receives from Yesod, there will be distinguished at least ten aspects in Malkhut that receives, and ten in Yesod that bestows. How so? Yesod may bestow on her what it receives from Tiferet and from its own self: that makes two; or it may bestow what it receives from Hod: that makes two. For Malkhut changes according to the effluence that is bestowed, and according to the bestower too. And likewise if it bestows on her from Netzach, and after this manner for the other Sefirot. And although we say "changes," the intent is not, God forbid, an actual change; rather the aspect is in her, standing for ever, and the intent is that she shines in that aspect. And likewise she is distinguished into ten aspects with relation to each and every Sefirah. And sometimes the aspect is in another manner: for she is distinguished in this aspect at a time when she receives from Tiferet when he is receiving from Gevurah and Tiferet bestows Chesed on her (in the secret of the blending); or she is distinguished in the aspect in which Tiferet receives from Chesed and bestows Chesed. For these aspects are not equal, and each one of them has a title of its own. And this is the reason that the sages of Israel agreed that we have no intention [kavvanah] at present: because the one who prays must be expert in the matter of the titles, to say: this aspect is equal to that aspect, and it is called by this name; and after this manner he will draw the effluence by way of the equal aspects, and the prayer will be received. And now that we have no knowledge of these matters, we say that we do not have intention. And this is the matter of the change of the aspects in each and every attribute. And for each and every title there is an aspect, so that this one shows what its fellow does not show, and a manifestation other than the manifestation of its fellow. And this premise is explained from many places in the words of Rashbi, peace be upon him. And these are his words in the Tikkunim (Tikkun 56, fol. 89b): "Heh, Heh: a woman and her daughter, a daughter-in-law and her mother-in-law; according to the branches of the tree, so are they called." Thus far his words.

הנה נתבאר בשער מהות והנהגה היות הספירות כח שוה לכל דבר ותמורתו, והנה לפ"ז תשתנה המדה מבחינה לבחינה לפי מציאות קבלתה מא' מהספירות העליונות לפעול ברצון המאציל. ועתה כאשר נבחין שהמלכות מקבלת מהיסוד תתבחן לפחות בי' בחינות אל המלכות המקבלת וי' אל היסוד המשפיע. כיצד היסוד ישפיע אליה מה שהוא מקבל מהת"ת ומעצמותו הרי שנים או ישפיע מה שמקבל מהוד הרי ב', כי המלכות תשתנה לפי שפע הנשפע והמשפיע ג"כ. וכן אם ישפיע לה מנצח וע"ד זה לשאר הספירות. ועם היות שאנו אומרים תשתנה, אין הכונה שנוי ממש ח"ו אלא הבחינה היא בה לעולם קיימת אלא הכוונה שתאיר בבחינה ההיא. וכן תתבחן בי' בחינות אל כל ספירה וספי'. ולפעמים תהיה הבחינה בענין אחר, כי תתבחן בבחינה זו בעת שתקבל מהת"ת בהיותו מקבל מהגבורה והת"ת ישפיע לה חסד (בסוד ההמזגה) או תתבחן בבחינת שהתפארת מקבל מהחסד וישפיע חסד. כי בחינות אלה אינם שוות ולכל אחד מהם כנוי לעצמו. וזה הטעם שהסכימו חכמי ישראל שאין לנו כעת כוונה. מפני שצריך המתפלל להיות בקי בענין הכנויים לומר בחינה זו שוה אל בחינה זו ותקרא בשם זה וע"ד זה ישפיע השפע דרך הבחינות השוות ויקובל התפלה. ועכשיו שאין לנו ידיעה בעניינים האלה נאמר שאין אנו מכוונים. וזהו ענין שנוי הבחינות בכל מדה ומדה. ולכל כנוי וכנוי בחינה שיורה זה מה שלא יורה חבירו ומציאות זולת מציאות חבירו. והקדמה זו מתבארת מכמה מקומות מתוך דברי הרשב"י ע"ה. וז"ל בתקונים (תקונא נ"ו דף פ"ט ע"ב) ה"ה אשה ובתה כלה וחמותה, כפום ענפין דאילנא הכי אתקריאו עכ"ל.

22:2:5 Behold, he explained that the two Hehs are a woman and her daughter, from the side of Malkhut's being daughter to Binah; and afterward he said that they are a daughter-in-law and her mother-in-law, meaning that Malkhut is daughter-in-law to Binah; and these are almost opposite things. And he resolved it: this is no wonder, for "according to the branches of the tree, so are they called," meaning that according to her branches and her aspects she is called, with respect to this aspect and this branch, what she is called. Thus this title shows what its fellow does not show. And the matter of the titles is not by chance, as the commentators thought.

והנה ביאר ששני ההי"ן הם אשה ובתה מצד היות המלכות בת אל הבינה ואח"כ אמר שהם כלה וחמותה ירצה היות המלכות כלה אל הבינה והם דברים כמעט הפכיים. ותירץ שאין זה מן התימה כי לפום ענפין דאילנא הכי אתקריאו ר"ל לפי ענפיה ובחינותיה היא נקראת בערך בחינה זו וענף זה הנקרא כך. נמצא שיורה כנוי זה מה שלא יורה חבירו. ואין ענין הכנויים ע"ד מקרה כאשר חשבו המפרשים.

22:2:6 And the matter of the titles, in their matter, is the spreading out of the branches of the Sefirot. It is as if we said that the four-letter name that is in each and every Sefirah is the self of the Sefirah; and each and every one of these ten names is clothed in the ten names that may not be erased. And we do not deny that the four-letter name includes the whole self of the Sefirah and its root; and the holy name that may not be erased, which is one branch among the branches, does not include the whole self of the Sefirah, as is proved from our explanation in the Gate of Names, chapter 1. And yet these branches have branches further, and they are the titles, which are more particular than the names: they are small branches that spread out from the large branches. Accordingly, the titles are a garment for the names that may not be erased, and the names that may not be erased are a garment for the havayot, and the havayot are a garment for the four-letter name in itself, which is explained in the Gate of the Four-Letter Name: like the branches of a tree, whose leaves cover its thin twigs, and its thin twigs cover its large branches, and the branches cover its main stem. And exactly so is the matter above. And this is why the Tikkunim many times mentions havayot, names and titles: the intent is these aspects. And now it is not far-fetched that one title should be more inward than its fellow, according to the difference of the aspects. And all the particulars of these matters will be explained in the Gate of the Glossary of Titles, with the help of Heaven:

וענין הכנויים בעניינם הם התפשטות ענפי הספירות כאלו נאמר שם בן ד' שבכל ספירה וספירה הוא עצם הספירה. ואמנם נתלבש כל שם ושם מי' שמות האלה בי' שמות שאינם נמחקים, ולא נכחיש היות שם בן ד' כולל כל עצם הספירה ועיקרה. ואין השם הקדוש שאינו נמחק שהוא ענף מענפים כולל כל עצם הספירה כדמוכח מתוך ביאורנו בשער השמות פ"א. ואולם עוד לענפים האלה ענפים והם הכנויים שהם יותר פרטיים מהשמות שהם ענפים קטנים מתפשטים מהענפים הגדולים. נמצא לפי זה הכנויים לבוש אל השמות שאינם נמחקים, ושמות שאינם נמחקים לבוש אל ההויו"ת, וההויו"ת לבוש אל שם בן ד' העצמי המבואר בשער שם בן ד'. כענפי האילן שעליו חופים את עציו הדקים, ועציו הדקים חופין את ענפיו הגדולים, והענפים הם חופין את עיקרו. וכן ממש הענין למעלה. וזה שנמצא בתקונים פעמים רבות מזכיר הוויו"ת ושמות וכנויים, והכוונה אל הבחינות האלה. ועתה לא יתרחק שיהיה כנוי א' פנימי יותר מחבירו לפי שנוי הבחינות. ויתבארו כל פרטי העניינים האלה בשער ערכי הכנויים בס"ד:

Chapter 22:3. The matter of the chariot; and there, an explanation of the chariot of David, the chariot of Joseph, the chariot of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, and the chariot of Moses our teacher, and their differences from one another.

22:3:1 The intent in this chapter is to explain the matters of the human being as a chariot, since it is a matter that relates to this inquiry. And before anything we say that any person who is to be a chariot for any Sefirah whatever needs two conditions. First, the purity of that organ more than all the rest of his body, until it is like white glass. And one must not think that the purity of that organ is enough even though he sins with the other organs, God forbid. Far be it to believe such a thing; for if he is blemished in any organ whatever, the Shekhinah does not rest upon him, for the Shekhinah does not rest on a blemished soul, God forbid. And so Rashbi, peace be upon him, explained in the Tikkunim (in the Introduction, fol. 3b), and these are his words: "For He does not dwell in a blemished place; as it is written, 'whoever has a blemish shall not draw near' (Lev. 21:18). So too, He does not dwell in a blemished soul. Happy is he who perfects his soul, so that the name YHVH may dwell in it, and makes it a throne for Him." Thus far his words.

הכונה בפ' זה לבאר ענייני האדם להמרכבה, מפני היותו ענין מתייחס אל הדרוש הזה. וקודם כל דבר נאמר כי כל אדם שיהיה מרכבה לאיזו ספירה שתהיה, צריך שני תנאים. ראשונה טהרת אותו האבר ביותר משאר כל גופו עד שיהיה כזכוכית לבנה. ואין לחשוב כי בטהרת אותו האבר די אע"פ שיחטא בשאר האברים ח"ו. חלילה להאמין כזאת שאם הוא פגום באיזו אבר שיהיה אין השכינה שורה עליו, כי אין השכינה שורה על נשמה פגומה ח"ו. וכן פי' הרשב"י ע"ה בתיקונים (בהקדמה ד"ג ע"ב) וז"ל דאיהו לא שרייא באתר פגום הה"ד כל אשר בו מום לא יקרב. אוף הכי בנשמתא פגימא לא שריא. זכאה איהו מאן דאשלים נשמתיה לשרייא ביה שם יהו"ה ועביד ליה כורסיא לגביה עכ"ל.

22:3:2 And so it is written, "evil shall not sojourn with You" (Ps. 5:5); and our Rabbis, of blessed memory, expounded (Shabbat 149b): "the one who does evil shall not sojourn in Your dwelling," and that is the transgressor. Rather the matter is this: that he should purify his whole body from every blemish, and afterward be meticulous in some commandment and hold on to it most of all; like one whose Torah is his craft, who becomes a chariot for Tiferet. And so it is said of Jacob (Gen. 25:27), "a whole man, dwelling in tents": its plain sense is a complete man, who was not blemished in any of his organs, God forbid, but complete, without blemish; "dwelling in tents" means the tents of Torah, occupying himself with it. And so our Rabbis, of blessed memory, said in Bereshit Rabbah on "and he lay down in that place" (Gen. 28:11): there he lay down, but for the fourteen years that he was in the house of study of Shem and Eber he did not lie down, for all his occupation was in the Torah. And therefore he became a chariot for Tiferet, in the secret of "dwelling in tents." However, Moses our teacher, peace be upon him, was raised above him, because he occupied himself with the Torah more, and further taught it to sixty myriads; and although Moses our teacher, peace be upon him, is also a chariot for Tiferet, he is more concealed, for he is in the secret of Da'at, as we explained in Gate 1, chapter 2, with the help of the Name. And likewise Abraham became a chariot for Chesed, because in this world he held on to the attribute of Chesed without end, as our Rabbis, of blessed memory, spoke at length; and he bestowed kindnesses on souls and on bodies, as explained by our Rabbis, of blessed memory, on (Gen. 12:5) "and the souls that they had made in Haran": for he would feed them and give them drink and draw them near to the service of YHVH. And therefore he became a chariot for Chesed; and this is the attribute of Chesed of which our Rabbis, of blessed memory, said, "and the right hand draws near." And likewise Isaac became a chariot for Gevurah, because he held on to the attribute of judgment as his portion, and stretched out his neck on the altar, and made known to all who come into the world that the world has a Judge and a God, and there is judgment and there is a judge; and so they expounded in the Zohar. And likewise he would rescue the oppressed from the hand of his oppressor, restore the stolen thing from the hand of the robber, "and pluck the prey out of his teeth" (Job 29:17); and all matters like these are those that depend on the attribute of Gevurah. And this is the first condition. He further needs the second condition: that his soul be emanated from that attribute; for through this that attribute will bestow effluence upon him and do its action through him, until the soul becomes a source for the effluence that is bestowed from it. And therefore the soul of Abraham had to be from Chesed, and the soul of Isaac from Gevurah, and likewise after this manner for the other righteous ones. And behold, the soul of Joseph was from Yesod; and when he gained merit and perfected his soul through that great deed, that he did not defile the sign of the holy covenant and overcame his inclination, he became a chariot for the Righteous One; and from there he fed the world in the days of the famine, and likewise from there he sustained all his father's house all the days of his being. For it is the attribute that gives food for the sustenance of Israel. And likewise David, king, peace be upon him, merited kingship for himself and for his seed after him by the merit of his having set this attribute right and adorned it, in contending with her enemies round about. And this is why he would rise at midnight, and likewise never slept except as the sleep of a horse, and matters like these, of the adornments of the bride, as is known. And when we investigate the manifestations of the chariot, we find them to be three aspects. The first aspect is the aspect of David: that he adorned the bride in the aspect of Malkhut. The second aspect is the aspect of Joseph, that is, the aspect of Yesod, which is the passage of the effluence from all the Sefirot to Malkhut. The third aspect is the aspect of the Patriarchs and of Moses our teacher, peace be upon him, though one was raised above his fellows, as we shall explain with the help of Heaven.

נאמר ביעקב (בראשית כה כז) איש תם יושב אוהלים, ופשוטו איש שלם שלא היה פגום באבר מאבריו ח"ו אלא שלם בלא מום. יושב אהלים, ר"ל אהלי התורה ועוסק בה. וכן אמרו רז"ל בב"ר וישכב במקום ההוא (שם כח יא). שם שכב הוא ג"כ מרכבה לת"ת הוא נעלם יותר שהוא בסוד הדעת כמו שפי' בשער א' פ"ב בע"ה. וכן אברהם נעשה מרכבה לחסד להיות שאחז בעה"ז במדת החסד לאין תכלית כאשר האריכו רז"ל והיה גומל חסדים לנשמות ולגופים כמבואר ברז"ל המזבח והיה מודיע לכל באי עולם כי יש לעולם שופט ואלוה ויש דין ויש דיין, וכן דרשו בזהר. וכן היה מציל עשוק מיד עשקו ולהשיב הגזילה מיד הגזלן ומשיניו ישליך טרף. וכל עניינים כאלה הם התלוים במדת הגבורה. וזהו תנאי הראשון. עוד צריך תנאי השני שתהיה נשמתו אצולה מן המדה ההיא, שעי"כ תהיה המדה ההיא משפעת בו שפע ופועלת פעולתה ע"י עד שהנשמה תהיה מקור להשפע הנשפע ממנה. ולכן הוכרח היות נשמת אברהם מחסד ונשמת יצחק מהגבורה וכן עד"ז לשאר הצדיקים והנה נשמת יוסף היתה מהיסוד וכאשר זכה והשלים נשמתו במעשה הרב ההוא שלא טימא אות ברית קדש ונתגבר על יצרו נעשה מרכבה לצדיק ומשם היה זן את העולם בימי הרעב. וכן משם כלכל כל בית אביו כל ימי היותו. כי היא המדה הנותנת מזון לפרנסת ישראל. וכן דהמע"ה זכה למלכות לו ולזרעו אחריו בזכות שתקן המדה הזו וקישטה בהיותו מתגרה עם לוחמיה סביב. וזהו שהיה קם בחצי הלילה וכן מעולם לא ישן אלא כשינת הסוס וכיוצא בעניינים האלה מקישוטי הכלה כנודע. וכאשר נחקור על מציאיות המרכבה נמצאם ג' בחינות. הבחינה האחת היא בחינת דוד והיא שקישט הכלה בבחי' מלכות. הבחינה הב' בחי' יוסף דהיינו בחי' יסוד, והיינו מעבר השפע מכל הספי' אל המלכות. הבחינה הג' היא בחינת האבות ומרע"ה, עם שנתעלה הא' על חביריו כאשר נבאר בס"ד.

22:3:3 And behold, we do not deny that the whole matter of the chariots is the tikkunim of the Shekhinah, that is, of Malkhut. And to this matter there are five aspects. The first is the aspect of Malkhut with respect to her adornments: that is, to bestow effluence from the upper attributes; and not the effluence of food, nor the effluence of the world's needs, but to set the bride right, so that she be adorned with her adornments, as explained in the Gate of Essence and Governance.

והנה לא נכחיש כי כל ענין המרכבות הם תקוני השכינה ר"ל המלכות. ואל הענין הזה ה' בחינות. הא' היא בחינת המלכות בערך קישוטיה ר"ל להשפיע מהמדות העליונות שפע ולא שפע המזון ולא שפע צורך העולם אלא לתקן הכלה שתהיה מקושטת בקשוטיה כמבואר בשער מהות והנהגה.

22:3:4 And behold, these adornments are tikkunim toward the coupling, not the coupling itself.

והנה הקישוטים האלה הם תיקונים אל הזווג לא הזווג ממש.

22:3:5 And behold, from this matter, and from the effluence that is bestowed for this tikkun, there is not yet so much benefit, until the coupling comes about; for then she receives the effluence to feed and sustain the upper ones and the lower ones. And this is the reason that David was in poverty, as explained in the Zohar, Parashat Noach (fol. 63), on the verse "Hearken, O Laish" (Isa. 10:30): because all his matters were tikkunim, so that she would be adorned. And in the days of Solomon there was the coupling, and the moon was in her fullness; and so they explained in the Zohar on the Song of Songs and in other places. And this is the aspect of David. And this is the reason that "the builders," who are the Patriarchs, "rejected" [her] (Ps. 118:22) in this matter: because they saw that her complete coupling was far from being realized, until Israel should be on their land, quiet and at ease, and YHVH should give them rest round about, with the abolition of the husks and the enemies. And because of the remoteness of the matter they set right another aspect, as we shall explain. The second aspect is the aspect of Joseph, and it is the tikkun of Yesod; and it is certainly an honored level, raised above the preceding one, and it is through this matter. But this is not the coupling itself; rather it is a tikkun toward the coupling. For when the Righteous One is set right, he bestows effluence into Malkhut from his own aspect; and he is also prepared for more than the bestowal, for this is his tikkun toward the coupling, which is an aspect more choice than the bestowal of effluence. And in him both are found: effluence that he bestows, and further that he is set right for the coupling, for he is the holy organ, as is known. And just as the organ is needed for the union of a man and his wife, so Yesod is needed for the union of Tiferet and Malkhut, as explained in the preceding gates. The third aspect is the aspect of Abraham and Isaac, and it is an honored aspect (though it is not raised above the aspect of Joseph). And the reason is that it is an aspect toward the bestowal of effluence itself into Malkhut. For it is known that her nourishment is from the right and from the left: for it is the thread of Chesed that is drawn over her by day, and likewise from Gevurah much effluence and food is bestowed upon her. And there is a further need for them, for they are a preparation for the coupling, for the matter of the embrace, as explained in Gate 8, chapter 21, with the help of Heaven. And nevertheless they are not equal to the eminence of Yesod, because the eminence of Yesod is the more glorious, for it is the principal thing for the coupling, as we explained. The fourth aspect is the aspect of Jacob, who is the husband of the Lady [ba'alah de-Matronita]: that is, besides bestowing effluence into the Shekhinah, he further bestows into her the effluence of the coupling; that is, through him Tiferet is aroused to couple in actuality. And it is an aspect more glorious than the aspect of Yesod: for Yesod is the vessel for the coupling, as the organ is to the male; and there is no doubt that the principal thing is the body, which is Tiferet, which is the principal thing for the coupling, and Yesod is a passage and a channel for the matter that is bestowed. And this was the level of Jacob our father, that he was a chariot for Tiferet in these two ways: both in the bestowal of effluence, in the manner of the other attributes, and in the arousal of the coupling itself, which is an aspect all the more glorious, as is explained in the aforementioned gate, with the help of Heaven. The fifth aspect is the aspect of Moses our teacher, peace be upon him; and he is at the level of Jacob in effluence and in coupling, and beyond him, in that he is from the side of the concealed aspect that is in Tiferet, which is the secret of Da'at, and it is a subtle manifestation, as is explained in Gate 1, chapter 2, with the help of Heaven. And there we spoke at length of his eminence, so that the aspect of Malkhut, his mate, is only in the aspect of the rib, which is the Yod, as explained in the Gate of Manifestations; and she is the one who is called "the bride of Moses," certainly a king's daughter, as explained in the Tikkunim many times. Moses our teacher, peace be upon him, further added an eminence above all eminences: that through his aspect, the aspect of Da'at, there comes about the union of Chokhmah and Binah, as explained in the aforementioned gate. And through knowing these aspects many and many concealed sayings will be explained, over which the eyes of the inquirers in the Zohar have been closed. And now that we have explained the matter of these aspects, we shall further explain in what way this matter is in the upper Sefirot, and what is meant by the matter of the chariot. And the matter is that it was already explained in Gate 8, chapter 19, that the matter of the arousal is through the deeds of the righteous. And the Shekhinah receives that spirituality and the matter that comes into being through the Torah or the commandment; and she receives it through a light bestowed from above according to the matter of the commandment: if the commandment is from Chesed, she receives from Chesed; and likewise from Gevurah; and likewise for the other levels. And likewise we explained in the Gate of ABYA, chapters 3 and 4, that the Shekhinah pursues the righteous one, to be united with him, so that through him she may receive that power and that light.

מפני שהיו כל ענייניו תיקונים כדי שתהיה מקושטת. ובימי שלמה היה הזיווג והיתה הלבנה במילואה. וכן ביארו בזהר שיר השירים ומקומות אחרים. וזו היא בחינת דוד. וזה טעם שמאסו הבונים שהם האבות בענין זה מפני שראו היות זווגה שלם ריחוקי המציאות עד היות ישראל על אדמתם שקטים ושלוים וה' יניח להם מסביב בהשבתת הקליפות והאויבים. ולריחוק הענין תקנו בחינה אחרת כאשר נבאר. הבחינה השנית היא בחינת יוסף והוא תיקון היסוד והיא ודאי מעלה נכבדת מתעלת על הקודמת והיא ע"י הענין הזה. אבל אין זה הזווג ממש אלא הוא תיקון אל הזווג. כי בהיות הצדיק מתוקן משפיע שפע במלכות מבחינתו ואף הוא מזומן אל יותר מההשפעה שהוא תקונו אל הזווג שהיא בחינה יותר נבחרת מהשפעת השפע, ובו נמצאו שתיהן שפע שהוא משפיע ועוד שהוא מתוקן אל הזווג שהוא האבר הקדוש כנודע. וכמו שצורך האבר אל יחוד איש ואשתו כן צורך היסוד אל ייחוד ת"ת ומלכות כמבואר בשערים הקודמים. הבחינה הג' היא בחינת אברהם ויצחק והיא בחינה נכבדת, (עם היות שלא תתעלה בבחינת יוסף). והטעם כי היא בחינה אל השפעת השפע ממש אל המלכות. שכן נודע שיניקתה מן הימין ומן השמאל שהוא חוט של חסד המשוך עליה ביום וכן מהגבורה נשפע עליה כמה שפע ומזון, ויש עוד צורך אליהם שהם הכנה אל הזווג לענין החיבוק כמבואר בשער ח' פכ"א בס"ד. ועכ"ז לא ישתוו למעלת היסוד מפני שמעלת היסוד משובחת שהוא העיקר אל הזווג כדפי'. הבחינה הד' היא בחינת יעקב שהוא בעלה דמטרוניתא, דהיינו כי מלבד שהוא משפיע בשכינה השפע עוד הוא משפיע בה שפע הזווג והיינו שע"י יתעורר הת"ת להזדווג ממש. והיא בחינה משובחת מבחינת היסוד, שהיסוד הוא הכלי אל הזווג כהאבר אל הזכר, ואין ספק שהעיקר הוא הגוף שהוא הת"ת שהוא העיקר אל הזווג והיסוד הוא מעבר וצנור אל הענין הנשפע. וזה היה מדרגת יעקב אבינו שהיה מרכבה לת"ת בשתי פנים אלה. אם בהשפעת השפע כדרך שאר המדות, אם בהתעוררת הזווג ממש שהיא בחינה משובחת עאכ"ו כאשר מבואר בשער הנזכר בס"ד. הבחינה הה' היא בחינת מרע"ה, והוא במעלת יעקב בשפע ובזווג ונוסף עליו שהוא מצד בחינה הנעלמת שבת"ת שהוא סוד הדעת והוא מציאות דק כמבואר בשער א' פ"ב בס"ד. ושם הארכנו במעלתו עד שלא תהיה בחי' מלכות בת זוגו אלא בבחינת הצלע שהוא הי' כמבואר בשער המציאות וזאת היא הנקראת כלת משה בת מלך ודאי כמבואר בתיקונים פעמים רבות. עוד הוסיף מרע"ה מעלה על כל המעלות שע"י בחינתו בחי' הדעת יהיה יחוד החכמה והבינה כמבואר בשער הנזכר. וע"י ידיעת בחינות אלה יתבארו כמה וכמה מאמרים נעלמים שנסתמו בהם עיני המעיינים בזהר. ואחר שביארנו ענין הבחינות האלה עוד נבאר במה יהיה הענין הזה בספירות העליונות ומהו הנרצה בענין מרכבה. והענין הוא כי כבר נתבאר בשער ח' פי"ט כי ענין ההתעוררות היותו ע"י מעשה הצדיקים. והשכינה מקבלת הרוחניות ההוא והענין המתהווה ע"י התורה או המצוה והיא מקבלת ע"י אור נשפע מלמעלה כפי ענין המצוה, אם המצוה היא מחסד מקבלת מחסד וכן מגבורה וכן לשאר המדרגות. וכן ביארנו בשער אבי"ע בפרק ג"ד כי השכינה רודפת אחר הצדיק להתייחד עמו כדי שע"י תקבל הכח ההוא והאור ההוא.

22:3:6 And behold, since the matter is so, the soul of the righteous one, which performs the commandments, is found to become a chariot and a throne for the Shekhinah; and the Shekhinah is clothed and concealed within that soul. And it is a throne and a source for all the blessings, and from it the whole world is fed, and it is the source for them. And this is what was said to Abraham (Gen. 12:2), "and be a blessing" [berakhah], meaning: you shall be a pool [berekhah] for the effluence that is bestowed from above. And when the righteous one in this world occupies himself with matters of effluence from the side of Chesed, his level will be at the level of the chariot for Chesed, like Abraham our father, when the Shekhinah was without a palace and was clothed in the bird's nest, until he set right a palace for her in his holy soul. And Isaac set right a palace for her from the side of Gevurah through his deeds, to bestow on her from there; and not actual judgment, God forbid, for many kinds of effluence are bestowed from Gevurah for the benefit of the world, to feed it, etc., as was explained in Gate 8, chapter 16, and as will be explained in the Gate of the Glossary of Titles, with the help of the Name. And Jacob by his Torah merited to bestow into her, and further merited to couple with her in actuality; and he is called the husband of the Shekhinah in most of the words of Rashbi, of blessed memory. And this is the reason the Mishnah said (Peah 1:1), "These are the things," etc., "and the study of Torah [talmud Torah, abbreviated ת"ת, as is Tiferet] is equal to them all": because it brings about the coupling and the union. And therefore one whose Torah is his craft is exempt from prayer. And Joseph the righteous merited that through his deeds his soul became a palace for her, so that through it Yesod might bestow into her the power to feed and sustain the whole world. And Moses our teacher, peace be upon him, merited that his soul became a palace for her, so that through him he might bestow abundant effluence into her, as in the matter of (Exod. 16:4) "Behold, I will rain [bread from heaven]," and the other matters of the miracles.

Chapter 22:4. Explanation of a passage from Sefer ha-Bahir indicating that the patriarchs pushed Malkhut away and did not wish to be a chariot for it.

22:4:1 What is intended in this chapter is to explain a wondrous matter found in Sefer ha-Bahir, where it says that Abraham did not wish to take as his portion anything but Chesed, and likewise Isaac Gevurah, and likewise Jacob Tiferet; and they rejected Malkhut, God forbid. And at first sight these appear to be astonishing words that need explanation. And these are its words: "For Rabbi Meir said: What is it that is written, 'And God said, Let there be light, and there was light' (Gen. 1:3), and it did not say 'and it was so'? This teaches that that light was very great, and no creature could gaze upon it. The Holy One, blessed be He, stored it away for the righteous in the time to come. And it is the measure of all merchandise [sechorah] in the world, and it is the power of a precious stone that is called socheret and dar (Esther 1:6). And on what account is it the measure of dar? Rather, this teaches that the Holy One, blessed be He, took from its radiance one out of two thousand, and built from it a precious stone, comely and adorned, and included in it all the commandments. Abraham came and asked for his power, that it be given to him; and they gave him this precious stone, and he did not want it. He gained merit and took his attribute, Chesed, as it is said (Micah 7:20), 'loving-kindness [chesed] to Abraham.' Isaac came and asked for his power, and they gave him this attribute, and he did not want it. He gained merit and took the attribute of Gevurah, that is, Fear [Pachad], as it is said (Gen. 31:53), 'And Jacob swore by the Fear of his father Isaac.' Jacob came and wanted it, and they did not give it to him. They said to him: Since Abraham is above and Isaac below, you shall be in the middle and take (another version: include) all three. And what is 'the middle'? That is peace. But it is written (Micah 7:20), 'You will give truth to Jacob'! Truth and peace are one, as it is said (Esther 9:30), 'words of peace and truth,' [and] 'Is it not so, if peace and truth shall be in my days?' (2 Kings 20:19). And this is what is written (Isa. 58:14), 'and I will feed you with the heritage of Jacob your father': that is, a complete heritage, which has Chesed and Fear and truth and peace. And therefore it said (Ps. 118:22), 'The stone that the builders rejected has become the head of the corner': the stone that Abraham and Isaac, who built the world, rejected, has now become the head of the corner. And why did they reject it? Is it not said (Gen. 26:5), 'Because Abraham obeyed My voice and kept My charge, My commandments,' etc.? What is 'My charge'? Thus said the attribute of Chesed: All the days that Abraham was in the land, I did not need to do my work, for Abraham stood there in my place. 'And kept My charge': and this is my work, that I acquit the whole world, and even if they have incurred guilt I acquit them.

הנרצה בפרק זה הוא לבאר ענין נפלא נמצא בס' הבהיר באמרו כי אברהם לא רצה לקחת לחלקו כ"א החסד וכן יצחק הגבורה וכן יעקב הת"ת ומאסו ח"ו המלכות. ולכאורה נראה שהם דברים מתמיהים וצריכים ביאור. וז"ל דא"ר מאיר מאי דכתיב ויאמר אלדים יהי אור ויהי אור ולא אמר ויהי כן, מלמד שהאור ההוא היה גדול מאוד ואין כל בריה יכולה להסתכל בו. גנזו הקב"ה לצדיקים לעתיד לבא, והיא מדת כל סחורה שבעולם, והוא כח אבן יקרה שקורין סוחרת ודר. ועל מה היא מדת דר. אלא מלמד שלקח הקב"ה מזיוה אחת מאלפים ובנה ממנה אבן יקרה נאה ומקושטת וכלל בה כל המצות. בא אברהם ובקש כחו ליתנה לו ונתנו לו אבן יקרה זו ולא רצה בה, זכה ולקח מדתו חסד שנאמר (מיכה ו) חסד לאברהם. בא יצחק ובקש כחו ונתנו לו מדה זו ולא רצה בה. זכה ונטל מדת הגבורה דהיינו הפחד שנא' (בראשית לא) וישבע יעקב בפחד אביו יצחק. בא יעקב ורצה בה ולא נתנוה לו, אמרו לו הואיל ואברהם למעלה ויצחק למטה אתה תהיה אמצעי ותטול (נ"א ותכלול) שלשתן. ומאי אמצעי דהיינו שלום. והא כתיב (מיכה ו) תתן אמת ליעקב. אמת ושלום חד הוי כד"א (אסתר ט) דברי שלום ואמת, הלא אם שלום ואמת יהיה בימי (מ"ב כ'). והיינו דכתיב (ישעיה נח) והאכלתיך נחלת יעקב, דהיינו נחלה גמורה דאית לה החסד והפח"ד והאמת והשלום. ולפיכך אמר (תהלים קיח) אבן מאסו הבונים היתה לראש פנה. אבן שמאסו אברהם ויצחק אשר בנו העולם היתה עתה לראש פנה. ולמה מאסו בה והלא נאמר (בראשית כו) עקב אשר שמע אברהם בקולי וישמר משמרתי מצותי וכו'. מאי משמרתי, כך אמרה מדת החסד כל ימי היות אברהם בארץ לא הוצרכתי אני לעשות מלאכתי שהרי אברהם עמד שם במקומי. וישמר משמרתי, ואני זאת היא מלאכתי שאני מזכה את העולם כלו ואפי' נתחייבו אני מזכה אותם.

ועתה ניכנס בביאור המאמר הזה מראשו ועד סופו. ואגב אורחין יתבאר הכל:

22:4:4 First, he found difficult the reason for the doubling of the verse, in saying "and there was light," and not saying "and it was so," as its way is in the other matters. And to this he answered that there were two beings [havayot]. First, the being of the great light, which the world is not worthy to use; and when He saw in His wisdom that the children of the world would have no benefit from it, as we shall explain, there was need of the second being, the second light. And this is "and there was light," whose explanation is the being of the second light. This is the sum of the verse as to the doubling. And now we shall return to explain his language in detail. These two lights: the first light is the first Chokhmah, and the second light is the last Chokhmah. And it is fitting to know: was His knowledge, may He be blessed, too short, God forbid, in the matter of the light, whether the world could gaze upon it or not, so that He needed to store away the one because of the other? A wonder! And let Him create the second and not the first! And this question was already resolved in the Gate of the Order of the Emanation, chapter 4. And there we said that before all the beings, it arose in His primordial will that the beings should come about through Chokhmah; and because of the slightness of the something-ness in it, for it is very close to Nothing, so that "being" cannot properly be said of it at all, therefore there was need of the beings of the Sefirot (the emanation of Binah), as we explained there. And there we explained that our saying "it arose in His will," etc., and likewise "He was incising," etc., is all a parable and a likeness of the decree of His wisdom in the matter of the order of the Emanation. And now our question, that there should be the second light and not the first, is not right. For the first light is the Chokhmah that is at the head, and the second light is the Chokhmah that is at the end. And if we ask: let Him emanate Malkhut and not Chokhmah, we answer that if Malkhut had been the second level, in the place of Chokhmah, then Malkhut would have been the level of Chokhmah, a second effect; and in the end the world could not bear it, and no creature could gaze upon it; that is, the created ones could not draw effluence from it because of its great subtlety and its nearness to its source. Therefore things had of necessity to be level after level: first Nothing [Ayin], and afterward Something [Yesh], and likewise from level to level, from inscription to engraving, and from engraving to hewing, and from hewing to making, as we spoke at length in the preceding gates. For while things are close to their source they are at the utmost subtlety; and as they go out from before Him, they come down wondrously by measured measures, from cause to effect, from point to point, from the upper point to the last point. And now we shall return to the language of the saying. He said: "This teaches that that light was very great." It is already known that Chokhmah is above Gedulah [Greatness], and it emanates Gedulah. Therefore he called it "great," in the secret of Gedulah; and he said "very," to show that it is above it, beyond what is beyond. "And no creature could," etc. It was already explained that the reason for the Emanation is to make known His eminence and His exaltedness to what is other than He; for before the Emanation the greatness of the necessarily existent One was not known to any other, since there was none besides Him. And the Emanation was so that creatures could know His power and His exaltedness, as we explained in the Gate of the Reason for the Emanation; and so that existence would cascade down and go out from before Him and descend by way of the levels of the ladder whose head reaches to the God of gods and whose end is set on the earth. And this is what he meant in saying "no creature could gaze upon it": because of the great concealment of Chokhmah, the created ones have no apprehension of the Emanator through it. And for this he used the language of gazing, which shows much contemplation and great understanding. It is further possible to explain, as we explained above, that the created ones could not draw its effluence, because of its subtlety; and that is the gazing, which is the enjoyment of its radiance. "The Holy One, blessed be He, stored it away for the righteous in the time to come." It is already known that the world to come is Binah; and it is called "the world to come" because it is ever coming, for it is a river that never ceases. And "the world to come" and "the time to come" are all one, near enough, as is well known. And there is a difference between them, although both of them are in Binah: for Binah is called "the world to come" in her lower aspect, which bestows, as we explained, for it is "a river that flows and goes out and whose waters never cease for ever"; but "the time to come" is an upper aspect, which has not yet come but is yet to come, that is, the concealment of Binah, which is a palace in which Chokhmah is laid away and stored away. And it is explained in the Zohar, Parashat Yitro (in the Tosefta, fol. 68b), and these are its words in brief: "That inscription [reshimu] needed to cover itself, and it made for itself a palace to be concealed in. That palace it brought forth from itself, and stretched it with a great and vast stretching to all sides. It set it up in garments of glory, opened for it fifty gates, within within. That inscription was concealed and stored away; once it was stored away in it and entered within it, it was filled with light." Thus far its words.

"And it is the measure of all merchandise [sechorah] in the world." The explanation: Chokhmah is the measure by which all the beings and all the encompassings come into being; that is, sechorah from the word sechor, the Targum of saviv [around]. And they said "in the world" because Binah is called "world," and the beings that are formed within Binah are by the power of Chokhmah, which limits them and gives them a measure without measure, as we explained. "And it is the power of a precious stone that is called socheret and dar": for Chokhmah [chokhmah] is ko'ach mah [the power of What], whose explanation is that the aspect of Chokhmah that is united with Binah is called ko'ach mah, as we shall explain in the Gate of the Glossary of Titles. And it is also a precious stone, that is, the Yod, which is a point within the palace. "That is called socheret": the explanation is the language of going round and encompassing, for it encompasses all the Sefirot and gives power and light in them. And it is possible that the explanation of "precious" [yekarah] is the language of shining, as in "and the moon walking in brightness" [yakar] (Job 31:26). "And on what account is it the measure of dar?" Because in the verse both are mentioned together, when it says (Esther 1:6) "and dar and socheret." And because from the explanation of dar the matter of "and there was light" will be explained, he therefore asked, "what is dar?" "Rather, this teaches that the Holy One, blessed be He, took from its radiance one out of two thousand." The explanation: dar is the manifestation of Malkhut that is taken from Chokhmah, which is the matter of the rib, as we explained in the Gate of Manifestations, chapters 3 and 4, with the help of the Name. And this is what he said, that the Holy One, blessed be He, took "from its radiance," meaning from the radiance of the socheret, which is Chokhmah, that is, the first light. And the rib is "one out of two thousand" of its radiance: they are the two thousand years by which the Torah preceded the world, that is, the ten Sefirot that are in Chokhmah and the ten that are in Binah; and each ten of them comes to a thousand, for they are ten times ten, which are a hundred, and ten times a hundred are a thousand; that is, ten included of ten, and ten of ten, and they are a thousand. And likewise the thousand that is in Binah: they are two thousand. And one out of these two thousand is Malkhut, which completes the two thousand. And they called them "two thousand" to show that her emanation, and the emanation of the other emanated ones, had not yet come about; for then Chokhmah and Binah are related to the name "two thousand," as they said, of blessed memory: "the Torah preceded the world by two thousand years." "And built from it a precious stone, comely and adorned." The matter of "and YHVH God built the rib" (Gen. 2:22) was already explained in the aforementioned gate, that is, the manifestation of the Yod that is in Malkhut. And he said that the rib is a precious stone, the manifestation of the Yod, a sapphire stone, as explained in the Tikkunim, second to Chokhmah. And it is comely and adorned in the secret of the manifestation of the building of her Heh; and that is "and there was light," which is the being of Malkhut from Chokhmah, which is the first light, as we explained. And it is called "precious" [yekarah] from the language of shining, as we explained above. And the explanation of "comely" [na'ah] is from the side of the effluence of Tiferet, who is her husband, who is Vav. And this is na'ah [נאה]: Nun is Malkhut; Heh-Alef comes to six, which is Tiferet; and since his effluence is bestowed into her to adorn her, he therefore is made [the number six] from Vav [and from] Heh-Alef. And the explanation of "adorned" is the adornments of the bride, which is the manifestation of Heh, which are her adornments, as in the matter of the three colors of the rainbow, which are a red line, a green line and a white line, which are Gedulah, Gevurah and Tiferet; and that is Heh, three Vavs, as explained in the Gate of Manifestations. "And included in it all the commandments": the explanation is that in Malkhut are included all the positive commandments and the negative commandments: the positive commandments from the right side, Chesed; the negative commandments from the left side, Gevurah; and she is included of right and left. And the reason that she is included of all the commandments is that the commandments depend on the ten Sefirot, and she is included of ten Sefirot; and therefore she is included of all the branches of the Sefirot, which are the commandments.

22:4:7 "Abraham came and asked," etc. The matter is this: although a person's soul may be emanated from one of the attributes, it is nevertheless possible that through his deeds he may be made a chariot for another attribute. For every soul is included of ten; and when a person exerts himself in Chesed he will be a chariot for the attribute of Chesed, even if his soul is emanated from another attribute, because he makes the side of Chesed prevail over his soul, until the principal shining of it is in Chesed. And likewise for the other Sefirot; and we shall speak at length on this matter in the Gate of the Soul, chapter 1, with the help of the Name. And therefore the Holy One, blessed be He, wished that Abraham our father, peace be upon him, should be a chariot for the attribute of Malkhut, that he should make the portion of Malkhut prevail in himself; all the more so with Malkhut, which is included of all the Sefirot, as we explained, and in particular from the side of Chesed, as we explained. And the reason that the Holy One, blessed be He, wanted the tikkun of this attribute more than all the other attributes is that it is the attribute that needs tikkun more than the other attributes, for the reason that it is the last attribute, and "her feet go down to death" (Prov. 5:5), and she needs tikkun, to set right her diminishment. And for this it was said to Abraham our father, peace be upon him (Gen. 12:1), "Go forth from your land and from your birthplace," etc.: the intent is that he should remove himself from the husks and from the arrays of the heavens, as they said, of blessed memory, "Go out from your astrology," etc. And this is "to the land that I will show you," that is, Malkhut; and the intent is that he should exert himself to set her right. And he took hold of Chesed as his attribute, in the deeds of piety, as [the saying] concludes. And this is "and he did not want it": the explanation, he did not want Malkhut, and he did fitting deeds to merit Chesed. And this is what he said, "he gained merit and took his attribute." And he said "his attribute" for the reason that the principal sparkling of his soul was from Chesed; and therefore he inclined to the principal [root] of his soul. And likewise when it was said to him, "to the land that I will show you," it is written (Gen. 12:9), "And Abraham journeyed, going on still toward the south": to the side of the south, to the side of the right, that is, Chesed. Behold, he pushed it away with both hands, and wished to cleave to his own attribute, which is Chesed:

22:4:8 "Isaac came," etc. All the matters of Isaac are from the side of Malkhut.
