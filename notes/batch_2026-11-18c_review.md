# Batch 2026-11-18c review: italics for transliterations

Ruling of 18 November 2026 (Avi): transliterated Hebrew and Aramaic words italic; names, letter names, letter strings and naturalised loanwords roman. Applied by script to `translation.json` text nodes (`text`, `gates` titles/descriptions/contents lines, `introduction`, `indexNotes`), never inside Hebrew spans, existing `<em>`, Gate 23 entry titles (`gl-entry-title`), tag attributes or image data. Full form list and reasons: `notes/batch_2026-11-18c_inventory.md`.

- Round trip of the file unchanged in format (indent 1, `ensure_ascii=False`, no trailing newline); paragraph counts unchanged.

## Unsure / for Avi

1. **Book titles set roman** (Sefer ha-Temunah ×23, Sha'arei Tzedek, Or Yakar, Kehillat Ya'akov, Me'irat Einayim, Asis Rimmonim, Ra'aya Mehemna in contents lines, Pardes Rimmonim in the introduction and Sefer Pardes Rimonim in indexNotes.about …, 100 in all). The ruling names "books and their titles" as roman and most titles (Zohar, Sefer Yetzirah, Sefer ha-Bahir, Tikkunim) were already roman, but English usage italicises book titles; if Avi wants titles italic as titles (not as transliterations), all book names would need italicising instead.
2. **Divine names in verse glosses** set roman: "God [Elohim]", "God [El]", "the Lordship [Adonai]", "of hosts [Tzeva'ot]" (they render the divine name in the verse).
3. **Italic phrases containing names left as they were:** *El gibbor* (9), *El melekh yoshev*, *El olam*, *Elohei kedem*, *Elohim chayyim*, *Netzach Yisra'el*, *chatzrot YHVH* and other Gate 23 bracket glosses with YHVH, *ben Yah*, *Bereshit bara Elohim et*, *Shabbat Shabbaton*, *Kiryat Arba* … Splitting them ("<em>chatzrot</em> YHVH") would follow the ruling more strictly.
4. **Capitalised titles that are also words, left italic where italic and roman where roman:** *Mi* (92 italic), *Mah*, *Attah*, *Anokhi*, *Eleh*, *Koh*, *Ani*, *Amon*, *Aravot*, *Tzaddik*, *Tzedek*, *Pachad*, *Teshuvah*, *Tevunah*, *Nogah*, *Makom*, *Chai*, *Kol*, *Bereshit*, *Vayyavo/Vayyet/Vayyisa*, the planets (*Shabbetai*, *Ma'adim* …). Mostly the word under discussion, so italic reads naturally; but the same title is roman elsewhere (e.g. Tzaddik 23 roman, Aravot 9 roman).
5. **Tzachtzachot:** capitalised (53, the three lights and Gate 11's title) kept roman as a name; lower-case generic *tzachtzachot* italic.
6. **Ayin "Nothing":** treated as a title of Keter (roman), so its 23 italic occurrences were set roman, matching the 46 roman ones.
7. **Cipher systems** (Atbash, Avgad, Albam, Achas Beta, Ayak Bekhar) treated as names: 24 italic set roman.
8. **Chashmal, Gevurot, Chasadim, Teli, Amon** when capitalised: kept roman as names/titles; the lower-case forms are italic.
9. **Loanwords** (see inventory): roman Torah, Talmud, Mishnah, Kabbalah, Sabbath/Shabbat, rabbi, amen, Halleluyah, cherub, seraph, Sanhedrin, Satan, shofar, matzah, menorah, tallit, shekel, ephah, omer; italic *tefillin*, *tzitzit*, *etrog*, *lulav*, *sukkah*, *mezuzah*, *mikveh*, *chuppah*, *halakhah*, *aggadah*, *baraita*, *midrashim*, *golem*, *gematria*.
10. **sefirah / sefirot** kept roman (recommended).
11. **Gate 23 entry titles** not touched; some are transliterated terms (Mazal, Halakhah, Eruv, Etrog, Lulav, Baraita, Ov, Sitnah, Gezerah shavah, Itkafta, Hatkafta, Va'ed, Sha'atnez …). Italicising them means `<em>` inside the `<b>` of `gl-entry-title`; whether the Glossary page builder accepts that has not been checked.

13. **Names of the tefillin passages** ("Ve-haya ki yevi'akha", "Ve-haya im shamoa", 4:6:3, 6:2:6–7) and *Ehyeh asher Ehyeh* kept roman as names; the lower-case words in them are therefore roman there and italic elsewhere.
