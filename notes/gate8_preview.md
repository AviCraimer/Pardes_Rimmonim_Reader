# Gate 8 (Essence and Governance): reading notes before translation

*Written 10 November 2026 after reading all 26 chapters in Hebrew against the lexicon digest (`python3 tools/lexicon_digest.py`). Nothing here is a ruling; rulings go in `lexicon_translation.json` (reader-facing notes in `lexicon_reader.json`).*

## Rulings that Gate 8 confirms

- ***Metzi'ut* = manifestation.** 8:2:5–8 and 8:9:1–4 count them ("the first manifestation ... the second"), and 8:26:6 sets a light's manifestations against its own self: the world in exile is fed "from manifestation out of manifestation", in the time of union "from the light's own self, not from its manifestations".
- ***Atzmut* = Self.** 8:15:3: the first three "are His very Self"; paired with *mahut* throughout ("essence and Self").
- ***Kanui* = belongs to.** 8:15:3 "His attributes that belong to Him"; 8:26:4 "its nature that belongs to it".
- ***Dak* = subtle, *nimtza'im* = existents, *havayot* = beings**: all hold. Singular *havayah* is often the process ("a new coming-to-be", 8:15:4, 8:17:5), which the entry's word family already allows.

## Rulings that Gate 8 strains

1. ***Tikkun*.** Lexicon: "rectification". Gate 8 has four senses: (a) setting a passage or an argument right (chapter summaries 8, 9, 13, 14; "to mend our statement", 8:2:3); (b) the first of the five operations in ch. 4, the name in its proper written order; (c) adornment, glossed by Cordovero himself as "beauty and adornment" (8:3:4, the pillars are "a *tikkun* for Your exalted rank"; the Emanation is the Emanator's *tikkun*); (d) repair of damage (8:1:6, 8:3:3). The Zohar's *be-tikkuneih*, *ittakan* add "in its proper state", "was set in order".
2. ***Hakhra'ah*.** Lexicon: "mediation"; "tipping" rejected. But 8:5:6 defines it in argument as the opposite of compromise: "a compromise is the middle view that unites two views, not a *hakhra'ah* that tips toward one and denies the other". Elsewhere *hikhria* = "proved" (lexicon covers that).
3. ***Kalul, kelalut*.** "Composed of ten" suggests parts, and 8:2:3 warns against exactly that reading ("not that there is a recognisable part"). The noun *kelalut* is a count noun here ("another *kelalut*, of three") and has no gloss in the lexicon.
4. ***Kinnui* = title** works for names. It is awkward where the *kinnui* is not a name: "eating is a *kinnui* for what is revealed" (8:14:5), "right and left are a *kinnui* for judgment and mercy" (8:5:11). At 8:9:7 it is the name by which YHVH is read aloud.
5. ***Re'uta*.** Lexicon gives *re'ava* = "delight". In Gate 8 it is will or desire (8:22:4 "the desire from above makes males"), and Cordovero glosses it "the lovely will". "Delight" is also wanted for *sha'ashua*.
6. ***Nukva*.** Lexicon: "the Female (Nukva)", the partzuf. In Gate 8 it is nearly always plain "female".
7. ***Yichud*.** "Union" holds, but ch. 13 makes it a genus with three kinds, the third being *zivug*, and 8:14:8 turns on the difference between *mitchabberan* and *mizdavvegin*. Needs fixed, distinct words for *yichud, zivug, chibbur, kesher, devekut*.

**Settled since these notes were written:** *temurah* = "substitute / substitution"; Gates 25 and 26 are "The Gate of Substitutes" and "The Gate of the Substitute Palaces", so "opposites" belongs to *hafakhim* alone. The "thirteen kinds of *temurah*" of chapters 3–4 are still open. *Rachamim* = "compassion" (the line stays "the line of Rachamim"); *kalul* = "includes"; *mamash* = "literal / literally"; the noun *tikkun* is transliterated. Chapters 2–4 are translated. *Makhri'a* = "arbiter", the verb "tip (the balance)", *hakhra'ah* = "arbitration" or, in argument, "ruling"; *pesharah* = "compromise" (12 November; earlier gates converted). The five parts of the name in chapter 4: arrangement, permutation, reading, combining, letter-reckoning ("reckoning" in the quoted sources), with *davar* = "teaching".

## New terms with no entry

- **Chesed, Din, Rachamim as the triad of the gate** (*rachamim* 291 times). Policy needed on transliterating or translating when they are qualities ("simple mercy", "little judgment", "soft judgment") and when they are lines or Sefirot. *Ba'alei ha-din*, *mekatreg*.
- ***Hanhagah*** (governance), the second half of the gate's title.
- **Ch. 4:** *tikkun, tziruf, ma'amar, mikhlal, cheshbon* as a fixed set; *davar*; *temurah*; "the wellspring of the flame".
- **Ch. 13:** knowledge, knower, known; intellect, intellecting, intellected. **Ch. 25:** the intelligible, sensible and natural worlds.
- **Union:** *chibbuk* (embrace), *neshikah* (kiss), *shimmush*, *sha'ashua*, *tiyyul*, *te'uvta*, *chedvah*; *re'im* and *dodim*; *nesirah*; "back to back", "face to face"; *semekh*, *ezer*.
- **Anatomy (chs. 23–24):** *ammah*, *atarah* (also "crown" in chs. 17 and 19, and a name of Malkhut), thighs, kidneys, testicles, loins, the spinal cord. A register decision.
- **Ch. 12:** *batei gavai*, *batei barai*, *Mistarim* (given as a proper name), *atzvut*.
- ***Tachtonim, elyonim*:** "the lower ones", "the upper ones". The contents line for 8:20 has "lower beings", which now collides with *havayot*.
- **Divine names:** spellings YHVH with the vowels of Elohim; the compound names of 8:23:5–6; *Tzeva'ot*.

## Practical

- Long Aramaic quotations in almost every chapter from 6 on; the commentary then requotes them phrase by phrase, so each lemma must match the wording used in the quotation.
- Editorial marks in the Hebrew: bracketed note numbers ([יב], [טו], [טז], [יז]), variant readings (נ"א), and cross-references to Asis Rimonim and Pelach ha-Rimon. Follow the convention of Gates 1–7.
- Cross-references name Gates 9 (Mediators), 11 (Tzachtzachot), 14 (Manifestations), 15 (From Below to Above), 21 (Details of the Names), 23 (Values of the Titles), 24 (Palaces), 30 (Permutation), 31 (Soul). Check each against the ruled gate titles.
